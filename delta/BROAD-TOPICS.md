# Broader topics for Delta (from 2026-11-02)

Isac: "GTA content looks very niche and not so broad. Make the content more wide so more can see it."

Rule of thumb: a person who has never read a GTA 6 article should still understand the hook and want the answer. GTA 6 stays the thread through the channel, but a Short no longer has to be about GTA 6 itself.

## Pillars (rotate, never two of the same pillar in a row)
1. **Numbers and money.** "What $70 buys in GTA", GTA 5 lifetime sales and revenue, price against inflation, biggest entertainment launches ever. Every number sourced (Take-Two filings, Rockstar, press).
2. **GTA 5 and Online for everyone.** Secrets, hidden details, record stats, weird facts. Huge existing audience, evergreen search. Sources: Rockstar Newswire, wiki, press. Our own graphics only.
3. **GTA history.** Every GTA ranked by sales, how the series changed, what each game was the first to do. Easy to share.
4. **Gaming-wide hooks that end on GTA 6.** Longest development times, biggest game launches ever, the biggest games of Nov and Dec, what a $70 game costs to make. The last line ties to GTA 6 on Nov 19.
5. **Real world crossover.** Miami and Florida places and facts, only where Rockstar or a reliable source makes the link (no guessing Leonida = Florida).
6. **Practical and launch week.** Storage, preload, console choice, what to do before launch, no-spoiler tips. Wide because every buyer needs it.
7. **Pop culture.** Famous GTA controversies, songs and celebrities in the series, how the world reacted to the trailers.
8. **GTA 6 deep cuts** (the old pillar). Kept to one in four.

## Format changes
- Hook promises a payoff anyone gets: a number, a ranking, a "did you know", a yes/no question. No insider jargon (Leonida, Grassrivers) without a one-line explanation.
- Titles: lead with the wide idea. "GTA" or "GTA 6" in the title or the first line is enough; it no longer has to be the first spoken word.
- Simple, bold pictures: one hero object per beat, big type, hand-built shapes. Fewer scenes, shorter (25 to 40 s). The jury keeps faulting busy dashboards and picture craft.
- Lists of 3 to 5 ("5 things", "Top 3") are fine and share well.
- Weekly long video: a wide one ("Every GTA ranked", "The GTA 5 money machine").

## What does not change (SAFE-EDIT)
Own graphics and voice, no Rockstar footage, logo or music, no leaks, rumors labeled, every fact sourced, no AI tag (YouTube Altered content No, no "AI voice" line), jury gate with Truth 8.5.
