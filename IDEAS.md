## Motivation / sad / uplifting edits channel (parked 2026-11-12)

**Idea.** A third channel (YouTube first, TikTok later) of motivational, sad and uplifting "movie scene" edits, reusing the factory skills (voice, captions, SEO, posting).

**Reference studied.** A 46 s TikTok by @moviecilps42 (79k views). It is a Rick and Morty diner monologue (Adult Swim / Warner Bros. Discovery) with the original dialogue, word-by-word glowing captions, a fixed top caption "he has got A point" on a black letterbox, and Faouzia's song "UNETHICAL" underneath. Footage, voices and music all belong to someone else.

**Copyright verdict.** Copying that format on YouTube means Content ID claims or blocks, no monetization (reused content policy) and a risk of strikes. TikTok tolerates it more, but Creator Rewards only pays for original videos over 1 minute.

**Format options if it comes back:**
- **A. Original monologue + AI scenes (recommended).** We write the monologues (or use public-domain lines: Seneca, Marcus Aurelius), a deep Kokoro voice reads them, glow captions come from our caption code, and the shots are Higgsfield cinematic footage of one recurring original character (rain, diner, night drive). Altered content = Yes because the footage looks real. Monetizable; costs Higgsfield credits per video.
- **B. Public-domain films + narration.** Real old-film footage (e.g. Charade 1963, Night of the Living Dead 1968) under our own monologue. Free footage, but a narrow look, and restored prints can still draw false Content ID claims that need disputing.
- **C. Commentary on famous scenes.** Delta's `delta/SAFE-EDIT.md` rules: muted clips under 5 s, annotated, with our voice explaining why the scene hits. Safer, but it reads as film analysis rather than an edit.
- **D. Copy the reference as-is.** Not recommended, for the reasons above.

**Name ideas.** Last Booth, Night Counsel, Heavy Words, Low Light, The Quiet Part.

**Why parked.** Isac decided not to do it (2026-11-12). It would also have been a third channel next to Tech, explained and Delta, in the week before GTA 6 launches.
