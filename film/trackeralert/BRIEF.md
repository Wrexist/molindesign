## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**Your phone warns you when a compatible tracker that isn't yours is seen moving with you; it may be something you borrowed, so play a sound to find it, and know that Apple and Google both say turning off Bluetooth won't stop the tracker: if you feel unsafe go to a safe public location and contact law enforcement, and if it's safe, disable the tracker itself.**

**Backlog hook softened (LESSONS wifibattery: the hook follows the source's strength).** "Your phone can tell if you're being tracked." is 8 words (limit 6) and overclaims: neither maker says the phone detects tracking in general. Apple: you're notified if a compatible device "is separated from its owner and seen moving with you over time". Google: alerts "currently work with Find Hub network compatible tags, headphones, and Apple AirTags". It says nothing about stalkerware, shared locations or non-compatible tags. Chosen: **"Your phone warns you about trackers."** (6), with the scope chip **COMPATIBLE TRACKERS · SEEN MOVING WITH YOU OVER TIME** at about 1.2 s, held to 5 s.
