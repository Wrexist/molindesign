# Treatment · Your phone warns you about trackers

Factory #38 (`trackeralert`), the director's plan. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json` (Ming US_James, seed 7, no music, no AI tag). The engine, gates and sound come from `../wifibattery/` (#37, r2 7.6 / Truth 9.0): `CLIP_RUN`, `LABELS_RUN`, the safe box, quote plates, the peel and slap patterns, captions, the SFX gate from `vo/heard.js`, the loop seam at t = 1/60. The look is this film's own. A dim cut-paper street map is the wallpaper. A giant cream paper tag with a coral ring is the thread from frame 0 to the end card, and a red-dot trail is drawn across the map beat by beat.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `wifibattery` (#37, r2 7.6 / Truth 9.0).** The hook follows the source's strength: "warns you", with the scope chip on "trackers". The hero is over 700 px at frame 0, with a second element that has no words (here the coral pulse rings and the red-dot trail). No credit from one company sits on another company's prop, and the check screen never shows what the voice says not to do. **Don't take:** its Control Center button, wallpaper hills, moon and clock, or the paper hand and knit cuff. This film has no hand at all: a tap is a cream touch disc with rings, so nothing can cover a quote.
- **Learn from `juicejacking` (#36, r2 7.8 / Truth 9.0).** A common fear, primary sources that say something calmer, and a concrete "check yours". The picture never does what the voice says not to do, chips print on or after their word, and the hero stamp sits on a surprising state. **Don't take:** its kiosk, cable, gate or terminal, and its mechanical arm.

## Three structures
1. **The trail that keeps drawing (chosen).** One giant paper tag on a paper street map. Each beat lays the next red dot and dashed segment. At `dip` a re-created Bluetooth switch flips off and the trail stops; at `twist` it starts drawing again, past the dead switch, to a nameless OWNER'S MAP card (WON'T STOP IT · APPLE + GOOGLE). At `disable` the ring goes out and the trail stops for good. This is the only structure where the twist is a picture, not a sentence.
2. **The checklist card.** A paper to-do card ticked line by line: sound, public place, law enforcement, disable. Clear, but flat, and the Bluetooth myth has nowhere to fail on screen.
3. **The two phones.** iPhone and Android side by side for the whole film. Fair to both makers, but it locks one layout for 35 s (the darkmode lesson) and makes the tag small.

## The native move: the dotted trail
Coral dots pop one by one and a dashed line joins them in order, as in Apple's own map description ("red dots… dashed lines… the sequence"). The trail is drawn on the hook (behind the tag), on the phone's map at `iphone`, from the map to the backpack at `borrow`, and, after the Bluetooth switch stops it, again on "won't stop it". The DM frame holds the switch off, the trail running on, the stamp and both makers' quotes. At `disable` the ring goes gray and the trail's head stops.

## Layouts (two changes across the middle third)
- **A (0–10 s), the alert.** Frame 0: a re-created banner slides down into place with blank text bars, TRACKER WITH YOU? and RE-CREATED under it, and the 700 px tag with coral pulse rings below. On `iphone` the banner docks on a generic phone (punch-hole, no logo), prints Apple's alert name and opens into the map. On `android` a second generic phone slides in with Google's banner.
- **Change 1 at `borrow` (10–16 s): the backpack.** A torn-paper wipe to a big cut-paper backpack. Its outer pocket flap lifts and shows the tag, with three paper tags for the innocent cases. Then a small phone with a re-created Play Sound button, a tap, and drawn sound arcs from the pocket.
- **Change 2 at `dip` (16–22.5 s): the split.** A phone on the left with a big re-created Bluetooth switch, the tag on the right, the trail running between them and on to the OWNER'S MAP card.
- **Safety (22.5–31 s).** A paper street with a lit PUBLIC storefront (no figures); the phone travels to it and shows a re-created LAW ENFORCEMENT contact card. Then the tag, big, under IF IT'S SAFE, with Apple's Instructions to Disable button.
- **C, check (31–34.5 s).** Two re-created settings cards stacked, each path printed on its word, ending on a neutral chevron. **End:** back to frame 0's banner and tag, YES / NO.

## Rules for this film
- Frame 0 has at most 4 words: TRACKER WITH YOU? plus RE-CREATED. The banner's lines are blank bars until `iphone`. The tag is 700 px.
- Coral only on the tag's ring, the trail's red dots and the one WON'T STOP IT stamp. YES / NO are cream.
- Alert and menu names exactly as each maker writes them: AirTag Found Moving With You, Play Sound and Instructions to Disable (Apple); unknown tracker alert and Unknown tracker alerts (Google); Tracking Notifications; Safety & emergency.
- Re-created banners and screens are tagged RE-CREATED, with no logos and generic phones. The tag is a plain cream disc with no mark.
- Scope is on screen from "trackers" to 5 s: COMPATIBLE TRACKERS · SEEN MOVING WITH YOU OVER TIME.
- No people, no silhouettes, no hand planting anything, no chase. The only taps are the viewer's own, drawn as touch discs.
- "Law enforcement" on screen, never "police". The disable step always sits under IF IT'S SAFE (ANDROID HELP).
- The Bluetooth twist shows the trail continuing, and it is credited to both Apple and Google, each in its own words.
- Quotes word for word, with "…" at every cut. Each quote sits on a generic prop or on its own maker's prop.
- The settings cards end on a chevron: no switch is drawn ON or OFF, so the end question stays open.
- SFX only in the written gaps, ≥ 3 dB under the voice, gated from `vo/heard.js`.

## Jury r1 fixes (r1: 7.06 / Truth 8.5)
In order of impact. Every gate re-run afterwards.
1. **No spoiler.** The APPLE + GOOGLE quote plate is gone from the twist. During "Apple and Google say that" the screen shows only the phone with Bluetooth off, the dimmed tag and the stopped trail, plus two quiet paper chips, APPLE and GOOGLE, each landing on its own spoken word (the attribution, no verdict).
2. **The DM frame rebuilt.** BLUETOOTH OFF? peels away and **WON'T / STOP IT** stamps into its place on "won't". It is two lines because readcheck counts each stamp line separately: one 11-character line needs 2.23 s, and the hero line plus the 1.3 s pause cap only leave 1.92 s (LESSONS energy r2: ink the stamp on its key word). The stamp is 156 px type, about 600 px wide, in the clear band under the phone, never on it. The tag ring relights hard on "won't" with a pulse flash. The trail redraws from the tag past the dead switch into a 396 px OWNER'S MAP, entirely above the stamp. The slap sits in the 1.3 s pause after "won't stop it." (pause raised from 1.0 to 1.3, the house cap) and punches the stamp 1.00 → 1.10. The stamp peels as "Feel unsafe?" starts.
3. **The Android banner** prints Google's "Unknown tracker alert" at the iPhone banner's size. It drops in on "Android"; the bag wipe now starts on "backpack" and the flap lifts as the backpack lands.
