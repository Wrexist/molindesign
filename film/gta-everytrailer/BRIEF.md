## 1. Topic and one hard idea
Rockstar launched GTA 5 and GTA 6 with the same five moves: a first trailer that opens the same way, a trailer for the heroes, a world to sell, a long gameplay reveal and a last trailer ending on a date that had already slipped. Same playbook, thirteen years apart, and a very different scoreboard ($800M+ vs $3.79B on day one).

Main search phrase: **"gta 5 vs gta 6"** (spoken at 0.3 s, on frame 0, first words of the title). Secondary: "gta 6 trailer", "gta 5 trailer", "gta 6 extended look", "gta 6 launch trailer".

DM-able moment: the three end cards in chapter 5 (COMING 2025 → MAY 26, 2026 → NOVEMBER 19, 2026) next to GTA 5's SPRING 2013 → SEPTEMBER 17. Both games missed their first date.
