## Fixes, ranked by impact

1. **0.0–32.4 s, the shirt's colour (Truth, Hook, Share).** The shirt and swatch are hot pink. Rockstar's own image for this item (`VINTAGE_VICE_CITY_WEAPON_PATTERN_01…jpg`) shows a blue-teal palm print. Fans will call out the wrong shirt in the first comment, and the picture contradicts the source the film quotes. **Change:** recolour the shirt's base to a blue-teal close to Rockstar's image (try about #2bb7c9 base, deeper #137a8a palms, cream highlights; keep the Delta pink for the stamp and pills only). Add a BRIEF fact citing the image URL as the colour source, and say "blue" nowhere in the voice.
2. **18.9–20.6 s and the poster, stamp FROM HIS SHIRT (Truth).** Rockstar says "inspired by", and the stamp upgrades it to "from" in the same frame as the quote. **Change:** make the hero stamp **HIS SHIRT → YOUR GUNS** or **INSPIRED BY HIS SHIRT** (≥ 110 px, same thump), never "FROM".
3. **25.8–27.9 s, "Just the shirt." + ROCKSTAR'S PAGE: NAME ONLY (Truth).** It's false as an exclusive claim, because the page also names him in the Ultimate revolvers "sourced from the Vercetti Estate". **Change:** cut "Just the shirt." Either replace it with "Just his shirt, on your guns." (it re-states the payoff and drops the exclusivity), or keep the beat silent and use the 1.7 s for fix 4. Change the chip to **NO "TOMMY IS IN GTA 6"**, or keep NAME ONLY with no "just". Put the revolvers in the pinned comment as the brief already plans.
4. **12.3–21.0 s, raise the wow: show the shirt becoming a gun skin (Clarity, Share, Retention).** The film's whole idea, shirt → guns, never happens on screen. **Change:** after the snip (in the 17.9–19.2 s pause), the swatch flies onto a **paper pistol silhouette** (our own flat cut-paper shape, about 520 × 300 px, no real model, no brand) and the print **wraps across it** in 0.4 s with a paper-slap hit. That frame, the shirt with a hole beside a pistol in Tommy's print, is the poster and the DM moment. On "Look: a pattern for most guns" (12.3 s), let a ghosted dashed pistol outline appear beside the shirt so "guns" is seen before it's said. Rockstar's own image puts the print on a pistol and an SMG, so a generic pistol is within the source; never draw "all" guns.
5. **19.5–23.0 s, chip over the name tag (Craft, a repeated LESSONS mistake).** The PRE-ORDER BONUS · VINTAGE VICE CITY PACK chip cuts off "VERCETTI", and at 22.0–23.0 s the clip-pins overlap the chip and the tags. **Change:** move the chip to y ≈ 1380–1420 under the STANDARD/ULTIMATE tags (centred, ≤ 800 px wide).
