# The original kingpin · treatment (Delta, GTA 6)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-kalaga/** (Delta, held at 6.8–7.0): learn from its engine: voice-first `at(id, word)` keying, the `vo/vad.js` SFX gate, `fontStretch` re-applied after every `ctx.font`, and seeded paper pieces with grain, pale cut edges and baked shadows. Both juries called that look hand-made. Don't take its 840 px clipped box over flat ink (it read as a small window), its second "real world" place, or its pop-ups under 220 px.
- **film/qrcode/** (Tech, explained, passed at 8.0): learn from it that one physical object which changes state carries the whole film. Here that object is the shirt, which gets cut. Don't take its diagram overlays.

## Three structures
1. **Page re-creation + quote cards** (Rockstar's pack card re-created, then quote slips). Rejected: the dashboard and template look that held five Delta films, and a re-created page invites "is this real" questions and RE-CREATED tags on every frame.
2. **Then/now split** (Vice City 2002 on the left, GTA 6 on the right). Rejected: it reads as a history list (gta-vicecity), and a split with Tommy's name on the GTA 6 side implies he is in GTA 6.
3. **One shirt on a hanger, one cut** (chosen). Frame 0 is the paper shirt swinging in on its hanger, with the TOMMY VERCETTI tag and a GTA 6’S PAGE sticker. Rockstar's words arrive on torn strips. Scissors cut one palm out on "button-up"; in the written pause the frame changes shape and the swatch wraps a generic flat paper pistol of our own in the print, under the one hero stamp INSPIRED BY HIS SHIRT. The film closes on an identical YES / NO pair while the swatch flies home, so the last frame is frame 0.

## The native move
**The snip.** A dashed guide draws itself around the pocket palm on "Look". The print slides one repeat on "pattern". On "guns" a dashed pistol outline (our own generic shape) waits below. Paper scissors come in on "Inspired" and cut once around the circle from "iconic" to "button-up". In the written pause: snip, snip, the camera pulls the shirt up and left, the dashed outline becomes a solid ink-paper pistol, the swatch flies onto its slide and the print spreads over slide and frame (the grip stays ink), and INSPIRED BY HIS SHIRT thumps. The hole stays in the shirt until the end.

## Rules this cut is built to pass (LESSONS, Delta)
- Frame 0 shows 5 words: GTA 6’S PAGE (3) and TOMMY VERCETTI (2), under the cap of 6. The shirt is mid-swing on frame 0 (mean pixel change 5.7/255 per 0.1 s), and "GTA 6" is spoken at 0.32 s.
- The pill says **GTA 6’S PAGE**, so it scopes the stake to the page and never to the game. It steps out while the voice is on Vice City (2002) and comes back on "GTA 6's page".
- One stake (the page names him), one object (the shirt), one hero stamp (INSPIRED BY HIS SHIRT, Rockstar's own verb, no stronger), and the poster is taken in the pause with the stamp landed and no caption.
- Rockstar's words sit on torn strips, byte-exact (straight ' in "Vercetti's", … where we cut), never highlighted, and each one peels off before the next lands. The voice scopes the claim ("But the page never says Tommy's in GTA 6."). No person shape, silhouette or empty chair appears anywhere.
- Both editions get identical tags, each dropping on its word and tied to the same pistol. The end vote uses identical YES / NO tags.
- The wall and cork bleed to the full frame. Every other shape and glyph stays inside x 90–930 and above y 1450, checked with a pixel scan of every rendered frame (wall off, no safety clip) plus boundscheck.
- Sound is only in the pauses, gated on `vo/vad.js`, with each hit at least 3 dB under its neighbouring words on the final mix. No music.
