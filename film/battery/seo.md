**Description** (paste it in full; the PC can't read repo files):

> Your phone battery is already shrinking. Lithium-ion batteries lose capacity as they chemically age, and your phone shows how much is left: on iPhone, Settings › Battery › Battery Health (Maximum Capacity); on Pixel 8a and later, Settings › Battery › Battery health.
>
> The twist: sitting at 100% ages a battery faster. Samsung says keeping your battery at a full 100% charge for a long time "can reduce its lifespan", and Apple's Optimized Battery Charging works by "reducing the time your iPhone spends fully charged". Battery University estimates that a lithium-ion battery stored for a year at room temperature (77°F) keeps about 80% of its capacity when kept full, and about 96% when kept at 40%. A peer-reviewed study (Keil et al., 2016) found the same direction: cells stored at high charge and at higher temperature generally aged faster (not in a straight line: fade plateaued across some charge levels).
>
> Heat matters too: Apple says ambient temperatures above 95°F can permanently damage battery capacity. Running a battery all the way down adds wear as well.
>
> That's why iPhone 15 and later, Pixel 6a and later, and Samsung Galaxy phones on One UI 6.1+ let you stop charging at 80%. And overnight, Optimized Battery Charging (iPhone), Adaptive Charging (Pixel) and Adaptive battery protection (Samsung) can wait at 80% and finish before you wake.
>
> So what's your battery health? Drop your number in the comments.
>
> Fine print: the 80% vs 96% figures are Battery University's estimates for battery cells in storage, not a phone in daily use, and not a promise about any phone; how much an 80% limit helps your own phone depends on your model, heat and habits. "Shrinking" means capacity, not physical size. Every battery wears out with time and use, whatever you do; this video makes no safety claim about charging overnight. The Battery Health screen in the video is re-created and the 87% is an example; no real phone interface or logo is shown, and the phones and props are drawn by hand.
