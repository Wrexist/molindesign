## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**The stat everyone shares (one ChatGPT answer ≈ 3 watt-hours, "10 times as much as a Google search") is old: OpenAI's boss now says about 0.34 Wh for the average query, about a second of an oven; Google says 0.24 Wh for its median Gemini text prompt; Epoch AI and Microsoft researchers land at about 0.3. But energy goes with length: in a 2026 Microsoft study, answers 15× longer used about 13× the energy. So a quick question is cheap, and the long thinking mode is where the energy goes.**

Story order: the old stat (hook) → the belief, struck (ten Google searches?) → the question with the search phrase → the maker's number → **hero: about one second of your oven** (stamp, hold) → Google's number, its own comparison (TV) → outside researchers agree → **but** long thinking answers → **dip: 13× more, 2026** → takeaway: quick question, skip the thinking mode → the question that loops into line 1.

**Native move: the kitchen timer.** One notch for a short answer. The film never spins it for the long answer (that would put Microsoft's ratio on Altman's oven); the long answer gets its own prop, a paper answer scroll that unrolls 15× and drives a paper meter to 13×.

Show **HOW MUCH ENERGY DOES CHATGPT USE?** in the upper third from the start of `ask` and in the caption's first line. Secondary (volumes not checked): `chatgpt energy per query`, `chatgpt electricity`, `how much power does ai use`, `is chatgpt bad for the environment`, `gemini energy per prompt`.

## 6. Risks

- **Truth: company numbers.** Altman's 0.34 Wh has no published method; Google's says it is unverified by a third party. The voice says "says" and the chips say COMPANY'S OWN FIGURE; the outside estimates follow at once.
- **Truth: "about the same".** Epoch (0.3, GPT-4o, 500 output tokens, Feb 2025) and Microsoft (0.31 median, >200B models on H100, 2026) are estimates of different things than Altman's average. They agree in size, which is all the line claims; each chip carries its scope.
- **Truth: "thinking answers".** The paper models test-time scaling as answers 15× longer; it does not measure any named app's thinking mode. The chip says MODEL ESTIMATE and ANSWERS 15× LONGER; the tip is advice.
- **Truth: Microsoft v1 vs v2.** Some press still quotes v1 (0.34 / 4.32 Wh / "13-fold"). The film uses v2 (0.31 / 3.91 / "13x"), the Joule version; the ratio is 13× in both.
- **Packaging:** frame-0 test is the weak mark (5/6). The fastest lift, if the jury flags Hook: tallies that pop in on frame 0 so the eye has something to count.
- **No AI tag rule:** no line or chip may say the video or voice is AI-made.
- **Hero sound gate:** the 1.2 s hero silence and the 0.5 s and 0.6 s gaps are the only spaces for the big hits; they are written into `pause` now (detect polish).
