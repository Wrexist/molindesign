## Benchmarks
- **Learn from:** incognito (7.5, Truth 9.0, 1,281 views: a belief about a thing everyone uses, turned over by the sources themselves, one mechanism hook to end) and battery (a check the viewer can act on). Here the belief is printed big on frame 0 (**CHATGPT = 10× GOOGLE?** on a paper chat bubble over a search box filling with ten tallies), and the whole film is one mechanism: energy goes with how much the answer writes.
- **Don't take:** vision (7.2 × 3: one small print on a dark stage; here the paper oven is 720 × 700 px and fills the hero), detect polish (the biggest beats went silent: the script now carries written gaps of 0.5, 1.2, 0.6 and 0.4 s for the strike, the stamp, the meter and the toggle), password r2 (stamps everywhere: one coral stamp; the strike, the meter and every source chip are cream), wifi/gps (empty stage under a wipe: each incoming scene is drawn in full above the torn edge).

2. **Four price tags in a row** (Altman, Google, Epoch, Microsoft as one chart). Honest and compact, but it is an infographic with no hero, the look juries call a template (gta-pc).
3. **The long answer first** (open on the 13× meter). A stronger shock, but it would put Microsoft's ratio on the frame-0 belief and hide that a short answer is cheap.

## Native move
**The kitchen timer.** A 320 px mechanical paper knob on the oven's front ticks exactly one notch (15°, with a cream tick on the bezel) on "second", and the coral **ABOUT 1 SECOND** (tilted 5°) inks onto the door on the same word, silently, since no gap holds a hit there; the oven window light blinks; in the silence after "oven." the film's one heavy thump lands. The long answer never touches the oven (no scope mixing): it gets its own scroll and meter.

## Motion personality
Paper on a desk: springs at ζ ≥ 0.85, slides along one axis, two torn edges falling top to bottom, a hanging tag that swings and settles, nothing bounces. One heavy sound (the stamp); the rest are paper (a pen scratch, a meter clunk, a toggle click).
