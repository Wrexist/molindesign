## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 6's 13-year wait feels endless, but famous sequels made fans wait longer, up to 26 years for Streets of Rage 4, almost twice as long.** One hero number: **26 years**.

**The one measure (on screen, every list card):** `RELEASE → NEXT NUMBERED GAME · FIRST RELEASE ANYWHERE`. Main game to main game, the earliest release date in any region for both ends. We never say "record", "longest ever" or "the longest" about any of these; the voice says "famous sequels", the screen says `FAMOUS WAITS · OUR PICKS`.

Search phrases (say "GTA 6 took thirteen years" from 0.2 s; caption and chip `GTA 6 · 13 YEARS`): `GTA 6 13 years`, `how long since GTA 5`, `longest wait for a sequel`.

DM-able moment: line `nov`: Shenmue III launched **November 19**, 2019, the same calendar day GTA 6 launches (November 19, 2026). Show both years on screen so no one reads it as the same year.

## 2. Hook options
1. **"GTA 6 took thirteen years." + "And that's not a record."** **Chosen.** Same hook as gta-13years (the best Delta performer) turned over in line 2, in under 4 s. Anyone gets it, GTA 6 in the first second, one number.
2. "Fans waited twice as long as GTA 6." (7 words) Stronger contradiction, but 7 words, and "twice" needs Streets of Rage (unknown to many GTA fans) before it lands. Kept as backup title and payoff (`dbl`).
3. "GTA 6 isn't the longest wait." Says the claim without a number; weaker than a number in word 4.

Frame 0 (6 words/tokens max, GTA first): huge **13** already moving, `GTA 6` pill above, stamp `NOT A RECORD` under it (GTA, 6, 13, NOT, A, RECORD). `YEARS` can sit as a small unit under 13 only if it keeps the count at 6; otherwise let the voice carry "years" (the director's call, recount after every fix per LESSONS gta-editions r2).

## 6. Left out, and why
- **Duke Nukem 3D (Jan 1996) → Duke Nukem Forever (June 2011), ~15 y:** famous, but Forever isn't a numbered entry, so it breaks the one measure. Could return as a spoken "not counting Duke Nukem" only if the director wants it; I'd keep it out (whiplash qualifier).
- **Metroid Prime 3 (Aug 27, 2007) → Metroid Prime 4: Beyond (Dec 4, 2025), 18 y 3 m:** verified date announced in a Nintendo Direct Sept 12 2025 (e.g. https://www.techradar.com/gaming/metroid-prime-4-beyond-release-date-finally-confirmed-by-nintendo-along-with-hyrule-warriors-age-of-imprisonment-and-theyre-both-arriving-sooner-than-you-think); not re-verified that it shipped on that date. Swap-in for Psychonauts or Shenmue if the director wants a bigger US name; it ties Shenmue at 18 years, so not both.
- Any claim about how long GTA 6 was **in development**, or why it took 13 years: Rockstar hasn't said; no line touches it. The film only measures release to release.
- "Longest ever" / "the record": not verified and not claimed for any game. Games still unreleased (Half-Life 3, The Elder Scrolls VI) are not "waits" under our measure.
- Kickstarter, budgets, reviews of the other games: off-stake.
