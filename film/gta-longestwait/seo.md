## Description
GTA 6 is a 13-year wait. And that's not a record. These famous sequels took longer.

GTA 5 came out on September 17, 2013, and GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026: a wait of 13 years and 2 months between numbered games. Our picks of famous waits, measured the same way (release of one numbered main game to the release of the next, using each game's first release anywhere):

- Kingdom Hearts 2 (Japan, Dec 22, 2005) to Kingdom Hearts 3 (Japan, Jan 25, 2019): 13 years 1 month. Almost a tie.
- Psychonauts (Apr 19, 2005) to Psychonauts 2 (Aug 25, 2021): 16 years 4 months.
- Shenmue 2 (Japan, Sept 6, 2001) to Shenmue 3 (Nov 19, 2019): 18 years 2 months. Shenmue 3 launched on November 19, the same day of the year as GTA 6.
- Streets of Rage 3 (Japan, Mar 18, 1994) to Streets of Rage 4 (Apr 30, 2020): 26 years 1 month, almost twice GTA 6's wait (1.98x).

These are our own picks and our own date math, not a complete list and not a ranking of every game ever. With US dates instead of first release anywhere, Kingdom Hearts is 12 years 10 months.

GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. So, is 13 years long? Yes or no?
