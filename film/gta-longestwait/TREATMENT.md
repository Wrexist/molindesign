# GTA 6's 13 years isn't a record · treatment (Delta, pillar 4: gaming-wide hooks that end on GTA 6)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-price/** (Delta, last pass at 7.4): keep its engine whole (voice-first `at(id, word)`, the `vo/vad.js` SFX gate, paper pieces with grain and pale cut edges, `fontStretch` re-applied after every font change, paper-tape captions, quantized counters whose tags pop only once the number lands, the rail scan, the SCORE trims and `duck.smr_db` 36 that fixed its hits). **Don't take** its set: the jury called the synthwave sun, striped horizon, sweeping spotlights and confetti a generic "Vice" template, the top 20-25 % of the frame stayed empty sky, and the pink torn-band wipes read as flat rectangles.
- **film/gta-editions/** (Delta, the first prop juries called hand-made): take the idea of one physical prop the viewer can read; don't take its frame 0, which showed a story everyone knew. Here frame 0 shows the myth being broken (13 years, NOT A RECORD).

## Three structures
1. **A tape measure pulled out per game** (the brief's idea). Rejected as the hero: a horizontal tape on a vertical frame is a thin strip, it never fills 700 px, and five pulls read as five copies of the same move.
2. **Five dated cards on a timeline.** Rejected: a list of cards is the dashboard look that held five Delta films.
3. **One calendar tower that grows** (chosen). Every year is a thick wall-calendar pad; GTA 6's 13 years is a stack of 13 pads on frame 0. Its height is marked on the wall with pink tape (the doorframe height-chart move), and then one rival tower builds under, up to and through that mark: 13 → 13 → 16 → 18 → 26. The camera pulls back each time the tower outgrows the frame, so the pink GTA line sinks from the top of the frame to exactly half the height. The Shenmue III and GTA 6 launch days are two pages torn off the two towers' top pads.

## The native move
**The calendar tower and the height mark.** A pad drops onto the stack for every year, with a paper tick that speeds up; the counter beside the top shows neutral grey digits while it climbs and gets its exact `YRS · MO` tag only when the last pad lands (LESSONS gta-vicecity r1). The pink GTA 6 line is never moved by hand; only the camera moves, so every rival is read against it.

## The new set (no synthwave)
A waiting room built from paper: a plum wall with a darker wainscot, kraft floorboards, a paper wall clock at the top centre whose hands sweep the whole film (it fills the top band the last jury called empty sky), a pale height-chart ruler on the wall that scales with the camera, and torn calendar pages that drift down behind the props (time passing, in place of confetti). No sun, no spotlights, no stripes, no grid. Scene changes are made of the medium: pages fly in, the tower collapses, and one big torn calendar page sweeps across for the loop seam (no pink band wipes).

## Rules this cut is built to pass (LESSONS: Delta, "more graphical", gta-*)
- Frame 0, six tokens, fully printed: `GTA 6` pill, the huge `13`, the stamp `NOT A RECORD`. The thirteenth pad is already falling onto the stack and the 13 tag swings (≥ 300 px of travel in the first second). `YEARS` pops under the 13 on "years" (≈ 1.4 s).
- The measure is on screen from "ask" to the end of the comparison, on a floor strip: `RELEASE → NEXT NUMBERED GAME · FIRST RELEASE ANYWHERE`. `JP` marks the Japan dates (Kingdom Hearts both ends, Shenmue II, Streets of Rage 3). We never say or print "record" for any rival, "longest" or "the longest"; the chip says `FAMOUS WAITS · OUR PICKS`.
- Every number on screen is in BRIEF.md §3 or its derived table: 13 YRS 2 MO, 13 YRS 1 MO, 16 YRS 4 MO, 18 YRS 2 MO, 26 YRS 1 MO, +3 / +5 YRS, 1 MONTH SHORT, ≈ 2× (1.98×), NOV 19 2019, NOV 19 2026.
- The two calendar pages print both full years, so "same day" can't be read as the same year.
- One hero number (26 YEARS), and the poster carries it with the pink GTA line at half height.
- A new object every 3-4 s and a visible change about every 1.2 s; no beat holds still (the clock and pages keep moving, the camera breathes).
- Sound only in the voice track's pauses (`vo/vad.js`), the hero thud the loudest hit, every hit ≥ 3 dB under the voice; no music.
- End vote: YES and NO identical; nothing on the end card leans either way. The last frame cuts into frame 0 under the page sweep.
