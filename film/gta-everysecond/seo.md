## Description
GTA 5 has sold nearly 235 million copies since it launched on September 17, 2013. That is Take-Two's own count from November 2026, and Take-Two counts units sold-in: copies shipped to stores, plus digital downloads.

Spread over the 4,801 days from launch to Take-Two's November 9, 2026 report, that works out to one copy about every 1.8 seconds, or about 48,000 copies on an average day. That is more than the 46,537 seats at Yankee Stadium, every day, for 13 years.

GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Can it keep that pace up for 13 years?
