## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **Give the native move its sound, and take it out from under the words** (sound only, re-mix). At 1.5–28.5 s, only 2 of about 16 landings make a sound, and the releases click inside words. Move each landing thock (`land*`) into the nearest VAD gap with tolerance 0.35, not 0.06. Gate the release ticks with `G()` (drop any that don't fit, rather than keeping them under speech). Put one real case-drop thunk at ≥ −8 dBFS peak in the hook pause (2.95–3.55 s), where the BRIEF promised it. Raise `calK`, `slipS` and `y13k` to the level of the stamp (about −6 dBFS), then re-measure every hit on the final mix (≥ 3 dB under the neighbouring voice peaks). This is the full point docked on Voice/sound.
2. **Scope "Every day."** (Truth; **re-voice preferred**, picture-only minimum). At 21.7–24 s, "More than a full Yankee Stadium. Every day." states a literal daily rate. Re-voice it as "More than a full Yankee Stadium, on an average day." If there is no re-voice, change the caption to `EVERY DAY · ON AVERAGE` and add a ≥ 48 px `ON AVERAGE` to the `FULL.` stamp (LESSONS energy r2: a qualifier shown alone must be ≥ 48 px).
3. **Label our arithmetic on the slip** (Truth; picture only). At 12.3–15.1 s, `4,801 DAYS` / `= 414,806,400 SECONDS` print with no `OUR MATH`, over a `TAKE-TWO · NOV 2026` tag. Add an `OUR MATH` tag (≥ 36 px) on the days row when it lands. Move `TAKE-TWO · NOV 2026 / UNITS SOLD-IN` so it hangs off the `235,000,000` row only, not under the whole slip.
4. **Fix "Take-Two"** (re-voice `count` only). Whisper heard "Take-do" in the mix and "Take no" in the voice track, and the director's check flagged it soft (0.20). This is the source credit, so it must land. Try `say: "Take Two counts…"` with a comma, or "GTA's publisher, Take-Two, counts…". Accept only when Whisper hears "Take-Two" in the mix.
5. **Make the end question fair, and let the vote breathe** (re-voice `end`, plus picture). Every launch beats 1.8 s in its first weeks, so the current question has a trick answer. Change it to "Can GTA 6 keep that up for thirteen years?" (keep "1.8" on the end card so the loop still lands). Hold the vote card ≥ 1.2 s after the last word: the kraft sweep is now 0.5 s after the question.
6. **Wow: make the 1.8 seconds visible** (picture only). Add a big digital readout on the stopwatch face (`0.0` → `1.8 S`, ≥ 90 px, resetting on each drop), so a sound-off viewer counts along. Print `GTA 5` (≥ 36 px, our type) on every case strip, so the pile, the truck, the day mountain and the stadium spill are all visibly GTA 5. Draw the frame-0 case sharp (no motion blur on frame 0) and 1.5× bigger, mid-air above the dial rather than over it.
7. **Put the verb on frame 0** (picture only). `GTA 5 · EVERY 1.8 SECONDS` has no verb. Use `GTA 5 SOLD EVERY 1.8 SECONDS` (6 words) and fold sold-in into the same strip (`SOLD-IN` as the pink highlight under "SOLD"), which stays at 6 words. Otherwise, make the tag pop in on the first tick at 0.5 s. Recount the words on the render after the change (LESSONS gta-editions r2).
8. **Break the centred stack** (picture only). This is the craft point docked for the repeated lesson. Every beat is caption → centred prop → floor pile, and y 0–300 is empty curtain throughout. Put the slip off-axis left with the stopwatch big on the right (8–15 s). Show the stadium wide and low so it bleeds the rail edge to edge, with the case spill coming toward camera. Let the truck enter from the frame edge. Move one hero up into y 300–500 in at least three beats.
9. **Thin the end card** (picture only). Cut each end card to one line (`GTA 5 · 1.8 S AVG` / `GTA 6 · NOV 19`, with `PS5 · XBOX SERIES X|S` on the calendar). Take the tilted pile out from behind the cards, remove the flying case, and make YES and NO the biggest things under the caption (≥ 110 px, identical). Redraw the teal ring so it circles only `19`.
10. **Clean the transients** (picture only). At 24.0–24.4 s the GTA 6 stopwatch sweeps across `GTA 6 LANDS NOVEMBER 19.`; bring it in from below the caption. Re-walk every wipe boundary frame by frame after fixes 3, 6 and 7 (LESSONS embeddings).
