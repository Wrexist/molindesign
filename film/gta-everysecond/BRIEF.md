**Our math (derived; show it as `OUR MATH`, never as Take-Two's):**
- Days: Sept 17 2013 → Nov 9 2026 (the deck's date) = **4,801 days** = 4,801 × 86,400 = **414,806,400 seconds** (13.14 years).
- Seconds per copy: 414,806,400 ÷ 235,000,000 = **1.765 s**; ÷ 230,000,000 = **1.804 s**. Both round to **1.8 s**. (Ending on Sept 30 2026, the 10-Q quarter end, instead: 1.750-1.788 s, still 1.8.) Because the deck says *nearly* 235M, the true value is a bit above 1.765, so 1.8 is honest; "about every 2 seconds" would also be fair but less exact, so the film uses 1.8.
- Per day: 235M ÷ 4,801 = **48,948**; 230M ÷ 4,801 = **47,907**. Spoken "about 48,000 a day" (both ends within 2%).
- Per hour (screen chip only, optional): 1,996-2,040 → `≈ 2,000 AN HOUR`.
- Stadium: 47,907 > 46,537, so "more than a full Yankee Stadium" holds even on the low end (by about 1,400).
- "Thirteen years": 13.14 years; "for thirteen years straight" is true (13 full years plus).

**Not used:**
- The caller's "someone bought" (sold-in is not buyers; one person can own several copies across three console generations).
- Any present-day pace or "while you watch" counter (no source for the current rate; the 230M (Aug 7) → nearly 235M (Nov 9) step gives no exact delta).
