# Treatment: "GTA 5 Sold Every 1.8 Seconds" (Delta, gta-everysecond)

One hard idea: **Take-Two's own count (nearly 235 million, sold-in) spread over the 4,801 days since launch is one copy every 1.8 seconds on average, about 48,000 a day: more than a full Yankee Stadium every day.** One hero object: **the paper stopwatch and the drop.** Its hand sweeps once per 1.8 s and every sweep drops one plain GTA 5 case onto the floor pile. Hero sounds: the tick + case thunk on every sweep, the counter rattle, the 1.8 stamp, the truck door, the seat riffle.

## Three structures
1. **The stopwatch and the drop (chosen).** A paper stopwatch sweeps 1.8 s and drops a case per sweep from frame 0; it shrinks to a corner ticker while the math is done on a paper slip, then the pile, the day disc and the ballpark scale the same drop up; the end puts a second, blank stopwatch beside it.
2. A long-division whiteboard. Rejected: the infographic look that held five Delta films.
3. A live counter "since you started watching". Rejected: it would claim today's pace, which no source gives (BRIEF Truth-proofing).

## Native move
The 1.8-second tick. The hand reaches 12 every 1.8 s and a GTA 5 case drops onto the floor pile on that frame (tick + thunk). It never stops: big on the hook, a corner ticker under the math, back to hero size at the end. It is a rhythm, never a counter: no number of "copies since you started" is shown.

## Rules this cut is built to pass
- Frame 0: `GTA 5 · EVERY 1.8 SECONDS` (5 words) on caption tape + `SOLD-IN` tag (1 word) = 6 words; the stopwatch is already sweeping and a case is mid-drop (≥ 300 px glide in the first second). `AVERAGE · OUR MATH` pops in on "seconds", during the hook (not on frame 0, to keep the 6-word cap).
- Every derived number (`1.8 S`, `414,806,400`, `≈ 48,000 A DAY`, `≈ 2,000 AN HOUR`) carries `AVERAGE · OUR MATH` in the same frame. Take-Two's number carries `TAKE-TWO · NOV 2026` and `UNITS SOLD-IN`.
- No Rockstar logo, key art or footage; cases are plain paper with a `GTA 5` strip in our type. The ballpark is generic (no logo, no interlocking letters, no pinstripes); the name appears only as plain type on our chip.
- No music. Voice + own SFX.
- GTA 6 card: `NOV 19, 2026` + `PS5 · XBOX SERIES X|S`; the GTA 6 stopwatch is blank and still; YES / NO identical.
