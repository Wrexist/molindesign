## Description
Thirteen years later, GTA 5 still hides things you can go and find yourself in story mode.

1. In the very first mission in North Yankton, an alien is frozen under the ice by the train bridge. Shooting the ice does nothing.
2. Ride the cable car to the top of Mount Chiliad, GTA 5's highest mountain. Inside the station is a mural: a glowing eye, connected boxes, and what looks like a UFO, an egg and maybe a jetpack. Fans argued about what it means for over a decade.
3. Climb Mount Gordo at 11 PM game time. A ghost appears on the rock, and the name Jock is written in blood. Jock is her husband's name. She is gone at midnight.

GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Will it have a ghost?
