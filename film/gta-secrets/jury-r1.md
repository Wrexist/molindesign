## Generic / AI-looking
- The alien is the stock grey-green "big black almond eyes, arms up" clip-art alien. Give it one hand-made detail (paper torn edge, a cut-paper outline, frost crystals glued over it) so it reads as our object, not a sticker.
- The ghost is a featureless glow pillar with two dot eyes, close to the default "ghost" emoji silhouette. A hint of hair or a dress hem in cut paper would make her a person (the film's payoff is that she is someone's wife).
- The grey foil slate with a giant number and a dashed SCRATCH HERE box, three times, is the "SECRET #1 / #2 / #3" countdown template the TREATMENT said it rejected. Shorten it (fix 5) and vary the foil colour per secret (ice-blue, dusk-purple, night-navy).
