# GTA 5 Still Hides These 3 Secrets · treatment (Delta, pillar 2: GTA 5 and Online for everyone)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-longestwait/** (Delta, last pass 7.1, Truth 8.5): take its engine whole (voice-first `at(id, word)`, the `vo/vad.js` SFX gate, paper pieces with grain and pale cut edges, `fontStretch` re-applied after every font change, paper-tape captions swapped inside one frame, the rail scan, `duck.smr_db` 22). **Don't take** its one identical centred composition on every beat (the jury docked it): here every secret is its own full-bleed world (ice, mountain, mural wall, night hill), each with a different camera move.
- **film/gta-gators/** (Delta, the cut-paper diorama both juries called hand-made): take the layered paper landscape with life in it (snow, clouds, stars, waves). **Don't take** its second region that diluted the stake, or its frame-0 threat that read as a shadow: the shape under the ice is big, dark and centred.

## Three structures
1. **A map with three pins** (fly from North Yankton to Chiliad to Gordo). Rejected: drawing our own Los Santos map is close to Rockstar's map art, and a map reads as a dashboard.
2. **A countdown list (3, 2, 1) on cards.** Rejected: cards on flat ink is the template look that held eight Delta films.
3. **A scratch-off ticket** (chosen). The ask line puts a paper ticket with three silver panels on screen; each secret starts with a full-bleed silver foil sheet printed `SECRET n`, a coin scrapes it off in three zigzag strokes and the world underneath is revealed. Hidden → found in one gesture, a hit at every reveal, and the end hands the same foil to GTA 6.

## The native move
**The scratch-off.** The foil is one full-bleed sheet (silver, sheen bands, printed `SECRET 1/2/3` and `SCRATCH HERE`); the coin travels a zigzag and each stroke cuts a hole with a darker scraped rim; when the third stroke ends the leftover foil drops away. Scrape sound per stroke (high-passed so it never masks the voice; louder in the pauses).

## Rules this cut is built to pass (LESSONS: Delta, "more graphical", gta-*)
- Frame 0: five words (`GTA 5` / `STILL HIDES THIS`), full-bleed pale ice with a dark, big, unreadable shape under it, a crack creeping and a torch pool gliding ≥ 300 px in the first second, snow falling. Conflict, not the answer: the alien is only drawn sharp on "alien".
- Every secret drawn by us is tagged `OUR DRAWING` (≥ 36 px). The mural is our redraw from PC Gamer's description (eye, connected boxes, UFO · egg · jetpack, the jetpack with a `?`), credited on screen. The alien is a generic big-head grey-green figure, not any film creature. `JOCK` is red paint brushed on the rock, no drips.
- Nothing claims more than BRIEF §3: no "murder", no "troll", no meaning for the mural; fan readings are on sticky notes under `FAN THEORIES`; the ghost's time is `11 PM · GAME TIME`; GTA 6 is `NOV 19, 2026` with `PS5 · XBOX SERIES X|S` (rockstargames.com/VI).
- One hero per beat, a new object every 1-2 s, fill y 300-1430, shapes and type inside x 90-930 (the moving car and coin clip to the rail), backgrounds bleed full frame.
- Captions on paper tape at y 400, one card at a time, swapped inside one frame.
- SFX only in the voice's pauses (`vo/vad.js`) except quiet high-passed scrapes; no music.
- Loop: a silver foil sheet sweeps up over the GTA 6 panel and lands on frame 0.
