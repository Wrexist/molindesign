## The native move: the take-a-number ticket
Flat cream paper tickets with a punched hole and a printed number. A paper dispenser sits on the tower's door; when the tower is busy, every phone's data pulls a ticket and waits in a queue at the door; the door lets one through at a time (each one a soft tick). Your ticket is the only one with a heavy ink outline and a phone icon. On "back" it is torn (a paper rip), re-printed with a bigger number and slides to the back of the queue (≥ 400 px); on the NEXT BILL CYCLE flap it slides forward to its old place.

## Transitions
A paper ticket roll wipes across the frame (a long strip of tickets pulled right → left, opaque, with a drop shadow; the next scene is already built behind it). Each wipe has its own paper-pull swish. Card slaps for the time cards (DAY 1, AFTER 50 GB, NEXT BILL CYCLE, TODAY).

## Rules for this film
- Coral only on the hero marker circle and YES / NO. The frame-0 ≠ is cream on ink.
- No ✓ / OK chips. ONLY WHEN THE TOWER IS BUSY (≥ 52 px) and SOME PLANS (≥ 52 px) stay readable for as long as their claims are on screen.
- Carrier words only: "may temporarily slow", "prioritize … behind other traffic", "by plan and brand". Never "throttling" or "deprioritize". No ranks, no Mbps, no carrier colours or logos; AT&T and T-Mobile 50 GB chips equal size, alphabetical, cream.
- Every quote word for word with "…" at each cut, source and RE-CREATED on it; the label is FCC format only (no carrier, no price, no speed), RE-CREATED · FCC LABEL FORMAT.
- Lower band y 1000–1430 filled every second (the card, the tower base and queue, the month strip, the phone, the label).
