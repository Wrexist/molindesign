## Description
GTA 6 hit 7.9 million live viewers at the same time when it launched. That is the peak of concurrent viewers across livestream platforms on launch day, measured by Streams Charts (7,915,475).

The old game-launch record was Valorant's closed beta in April 2020, at about 1.74 million. GTA 6 is more than four and a half times that.

Twitch alone peaked at 3.31 million viewers for GTA 6, the most of any game ever on Twitch by peak viewers (ahead of League of Legends at 3.11 million). That is a record among games, not every Twitch category.

Viewers are not buyers: these are stream viewers at one moment, not sales or players.
