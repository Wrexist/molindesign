# gta-streampeak — treatment

**One idea:** at its launch peak, 7.9 million live viewers were watching GTA 6 streams at the same moment (Streams Charts data), more than four and a half times the old game-launch record, Valorant's closed beta (1.74M, 2020). Kicker: on Twitch alone it is now the biggest game ever by peak viewers (3.31M, past League of Legends' 3.11M). Viewers are not sales.

## Benchmarks

| gta-storylength remake (6.9 → 7.3) | One hand-built hero prop (stopwatch) carrying the whole film; a change every ~1.2 s. | The punch card and button box. |

## Three structures
1. **Tally room (chosen).** A brass hand tally counter is the hero; viewers become paper discs: a heap that lands all at once, then towers on a shelf. One tower drop answers "is that a record?" with a picture: 4.5 grey Valorant towers stack to the GTA 6 tower's height.
2. Stadium scoreboard. Rejected: flipping scores roll numbers under labels (LESSONS: slam final, never roll).
3. Bar chart of platforms. Rejected: platform peaks happened at different times and must never be shown as parts of 7.9M (BRIEF ban), and it is the dashboard template the juries dock.

## Native move
**The tower drop.** Discs fall one by one and stack, each settling once (no bounce); in `times` whole blocks of ten drop onto the Valorant tower on "more / four / and / half". Twitch's tower instead rises out of the shelf (same discs, a new direction). The tally click is the hero sound (in the breath after "G T A six").
