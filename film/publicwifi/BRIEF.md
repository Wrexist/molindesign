## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**The FTC says that because most websites now encrypt your data, using public Wi-Fi is usually safe; but scammers encrypt their fake sites too, so your data arrives sealed right in the scammer's hands; encryption protects the trip, not the site, so check the site's name letter by letter (Apple: some fakes change one or two letters).**

**Backlog hook rewritten.** The backlog hook ("The FTC says public Wi-Fi is usually safe.") is 8 words (limit 6). Chosen: **"Airport Wi-Fi is usually safe now."** (6). It names a place the viewer pictures (the FTC lists airports by name), keeps "usually", and "now" carries the FTC's "things have changed". The source lands at 3 s ("That's the FTC.") and again on the hero stamp.

**The lock was dropped from the takeaway.** The FTC page (Feb 2023) says "Look for a lock symbol or https in the address bar". But Google's own Chrome help (read today) now calls the secure icon "Default (Secure)" and never calls it a lock, and Apple's Safari guide tells you to watch for the words "Not Secure" instead. Apple Community threads say Safari 18.4 dropped the lock icon (secondary, not used). A "look for the lock" step could send a 2026 Chrome or Safari viewer looking for something that isn't there. So the check in the voice is the one all three primary sources support today: **check the site's name**. The FTC's lock line can go in the description, quoted and dated "FTC, February 2023".

## 3b. Truth rules for the director and jury (check every frame)

1. **"Usually" on every safety claim.** The hero stamp is **USUALLY SAFE** (two lines, ≥ 150 px), never "SAFE" alone, never a green tick on the Wi-Fi list by itself. Under it, ≥ 40 px: **PUBLIC WI-FI · FTC · FEB 2023**. Frame 0 never shows a check mark (frame 0 shows the conflict, not the answer).
2. **Encryption protects the trip, not the site.** At `sealed`, the sealed capsule arrives intact and is opened **by the fake storefront**, not cracked on the way. Plate **"…YOUR DATA MAY BE ENCRYPTED ON ITS WAY TO THE SITE, BUT IT WON'T BE SAFE FROM SCAMMERS OPERATING THE SITE." · FTC** prints on or after "scammer" (no spoiler chip). Scope chip **IF YOU VISIT A SCAMMER'S SITE** ≥ 40 px at `sealed`.
3. **Don't call public Wi-Fi risk-free.** No "100 %", no "NO RISK", no "SAFE NOW" without "USUALLY". The `honest` beat must be visible: the fake storefront still stands.
4. **No crime statistics.** No counts of hacks, victims or fake sites; none of the primary sources gives one. No "1 in X" chip.
5. **"Most", not "all".** The `now` beat lights most site cards (for example 9 of 12), not every one; one or two stay grey. Chip **MOST SITES · FTC**.
