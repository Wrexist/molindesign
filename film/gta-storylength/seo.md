## Description
How long is GTA 5's story? Players average 32 hours for the main story on HowLongToBeat, from thousands of submitted play times. Going for 100% takes almost 90 hours (89 and a half), nearly three times as long.

Why the gap? The story missions are only 55% of the game's 100% checklist; the rest is Strangers and Freaks, hobbies and pastimes, random events and miscellaneous extras like collectibles.

As of Nov 17, 2026, Rockstar hasn't said how long GTA 6 is. So for GTA 6: story first, or 100 percent?
