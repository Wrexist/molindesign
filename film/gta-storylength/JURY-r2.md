The previous Delta factory film (gta-outsold r2) scored 7.8 / Truth 9.0, so this is 0.9 lower. Round 1 of this film scored 7.0 / 8.0.

## Fixes, ranked by impact

1. **0.0–30.3 s, the set (channel rule, Hook, Craft; blocks posting).** What I saw: gta-outsold's frame 0 with a palette swap, compared side by side. Change four things:
   - **Covered tower:** drop the sheet-and-`?` device. Open on the 100% tower already built but running off the top of the frame (top edge above y 0), with the 32 h story stack tiny at its foot. The size gap is the hook, and "Guess." asks how tall the cut-off tower is. Reveal its height with a 0.8 s tilt-down from the top (a camera tilt, not a rip) on "But 100 percent?".
   - **Palm:** replace it with something in the street set: a streetlight or a traffic signal at x 40–140.
   - **Sky:** kill the peach horizon. Use a flat noon blue sky, or an overcast one.
   - **Blocks:** stop drawing case strips. Make hour blocks that read as time, not boxes: stacked round paper clock faces, like coins, or hourglass tiles.

   Re-run the frame-0 word count after the change (LESSONS gta-editions r2).
2. **25.3–30.3 s, the GTA 6 outline (Truth −0.5).** The 650 px dashed box on the hour scale draws a length. Give it no height: a `GTA 6` base with a single dashed column whose dashes fade out by y 700 and run off the top, so it is open-ended, or just a base plate with a big `?`. Put `? HOURS` in a boxed tag (≥ 44 px) on the base.
3. **21.8–24.6 s, the checklist hold (Retention).** All four rows enter together at 21.0 s, then only 30 px bar slices change. Bring each row in on its word: Strangers 21.68, Hobbies 22.80, Random 23.46, "and more" (Misc) 24.00. Each pops at scale 1.12 → 1.0 over 0.18 s with a pin tick at gain 0.4, and its swatch slides into the bar. Add a slow push 1.00 → 1.06 on the clipboard from 21.0 to 24.6 s.
4. **26.8–29.6 s, end-card hold (Retention, Loop).** Build on the words: 4 story blocks drop on "story first" (26.68), and the 100% tower shakes ±6 px on "one hundred percent" (27.44). On "Pick one." (28.72) both plates tilt ±4° toward the camera. Trim the tail so the swish lands at ≤ 29.3 s and `dur` is about 29.8.
5. **Wow: 16.6–19.5 s, make the tower become the checklist.** Instead of trucking both towers off and dropping a new clipboard, on "Why?" (16.74) tip the 100% tower forward 90° so its front face unrolls down into the checklist sheet (0.8 s, torn edge leading). The answer then visibly lives inside the 100% tower: one object, one idea, and the strongest picture in the film. Don't colour the blocks by weight, because that would claim an hours split no source gives.
6. **8.6–10.3 s and 14.6–16.2 s, the two remaining holds (Retention).** At 9.5 s (the pause after "players") give the cut-off 100% tower (or the current sheet) a 20 px lurch upward, a tease before "But". At 14.6–16.2 s add a summary drawing: draw the dashed 3× level line slowly left to right across the frame at 14.6–15.6 s, and push 1.00 → 1.05 on the brackets.
7. **18.4–20.6 s, the 55 % read (Clarity).** Add a 36 px sub-line under the header: `SHARE OF THE 100% SCORE` (GTABase's percentages are completion weights). This stops viewers from comparing 55 % with the hours.
8. **Poster and cover (Share, seo).** The poster has no game name. Add a caption strip `GTA 5: STORY vs 100%` at y 300–420 in the empty sky. Widen `STORY · 32 H` to 340 px, or set it above the tower, so `STORY` isn't printed twice in one column. In seo.md change the cover text to `GTA 5: NEARLY 3×` and the upload file to `gta-storylength-vo-upload.mp4`.
9. **13.7–16.6 s, tag crowding (Craft).** `PLAYERS' AVERAGE` touches both sides of the story tag, and the `100% · 89½ H` tag ends at x ≈ 925, right at the 930 limit. Give every tag ≥ 24 px of inner padding and keep right edges ≤ 900.

## Generic or AI-made?
Nothing reads as AI imagery: the paper grain, taped tags, stamps and clipboard look hand-made. What reads as generic is the **series template**. A third Delta film with a small stack, a covered `?` tower and a palm under a caption strip turns the channel into one repeated graphic. The flat checklist bar is also close to a stock infographic; fix 5 makes it this film's own.
