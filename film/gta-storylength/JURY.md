## Fixes, ranked by impact

1. **16.3–22.3 s, the "why" beat (Truth −1.0, Clarity, Share).** What I saw: "Why so long?" over the `NEARLY 3×` hero, answered by "Three leads, not one." No source says the leads make 100% take 3×. **Required minimum:** stop it being a cause. Rewrite `why` as "And the story? Three leads, not one." (same slot, 16.3 s), peel the `NEARLY 3×` stamp and the brackets before the caption changes (the towers truck off at 17.5 s now; start the truck at 16.3 s), and fix the same sentence in seo.md. **Recommended wow version** (one beat replaces `why` and `missions`, using a source already in BRIEF.md): "Why? The story is only 55 percent of the checklist." The clipboard shows five labelled sections drawn to their weights (STORY 69 · 55%, STRANGERS & FREAKS 20 · 10%, HOBBIES 42 · 10%, RANDOM EVENTS 14 · 5%, MISC 16 · 20%, all from GTABase). The story block ticks 69/69 on "story", the other 92 boxes stay empty, and the stamp reads `STORY = 55%`. This actually answers the 3× and gives the film its DM line. If this beat goes in, keep the three-leads line as one short caption, or cut it.
2. **23.7–28.0 s, the clipboard (Truth −0.5).** `100% CHECKLIST` over exactly 69 boxes with `69 / 69` says the whole checklist is 69 items. If you keep the current beat: header `100% CHECKLIST · STORY PART`, counter `69 STORY MISSIONS` (no `/69`), and draw 4 more greyed, unticked rows below labelled `+ 4 MORE PARTS`. Better, use fix 1's sheet.
