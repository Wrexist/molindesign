Delta catalog Short, launch week. Ming US_James seed 7, no music, official Rockstar clips in every card, our own graphics around them.

## Three structures
1. **Lineup board (chosen).** A split-flap board asks the question on frame 0; each person is a numbered card with a window (their official clip, then our flat drawing of their Rockstar line), a name bar and a quote strip; a row of eight number slots tracks the lineup. Ends on a ballot of all eight; the board drops back on for the loop.
2. Phone contacts list: eight contacts scrolling, each "calls" with a clip. Rejected: a UI re-creation reads as a template, and a call screen implies a relationship Rockstar hasn't stated.
3. Map pins across Leonida. Rejected: we don't know where most of them live; it would invent facts.

## Native move
**The number tile flips over** from "?" to the person's number (1/8 … 8/8) on the spoken name, with a split-flap rattle. The name bar slams in on the same word. The same flap language opens the film (the board's letters ripple at 0.1 s) and closes it (the board drops back on).

## Set
A night board: ink-indigo ground, faint coral awning stripes, halftone dots denser toward the bottom, an official Rockstar plate (Out Now spot, dimmed 50 %, blurred) behind the board on four beats. Cards are cream with an ink name bar, coral number tile and coral corner brackets on the clip. Type ≥ 36 px; everything inside x 90–930, y 300–1430.
