# Jury: gta-budget, round 1 (2026-11-17)

Judged cold by the video-jury, who did not make the film. File judged: `/mnt/project-files/factory/gta-budget/gta-budget-vo.mp4` (38.2 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps). It is bit-identical in picture to `film/gta-budget/out/gta-budget-vo.mp4` (PSNR inf). `gta-budget-vo-upload.mp4` is the same cut, smaller: same 38.2 s, audio correlation 1.00, picture PSNR 42.9 dB (min 39.1), 9.7 MB against 16.9 MB. Method: frames every 0.5 s with contact sheets, full-res stills at 0, 1.0, 2.5, 4.5, 12, 15, 19.6, 21, 23.5, 27.5, 31.5, 34.5, 37, 38.1 s, 5 fps strips over 10.4–12.0 and 14.4–16.0 s. I read the poster, `out/pace.txt` and `mix.log`. I checked `events.json` against `vo/vad.js`. To measure the SFX I subtracted the voice-only track from the mix (lag 48 samples, gain 0.742, residual = SFX bed) and compared each hit's peak with the voice peak within ±0.6 s. I also ran ebur128 and blackdetect, and checked the facts at the sources myself.

## Looks generic or AI-made?

Not AI-made: the paper props, the string on the tag and the stamp textures read as hand-built, and there is no AI-template cliché. What reads as generic is the **house set itself**. Three Delta films in a row (outsold, storylength, budget) share the same wall, palm, sunset, floor, caption strip, chips and centred hero. A regular viewer will see the same video with a new prop. The next Delta film needs a different set (time of day, colour or location), not just a new hero object.
