# Treatment: "What Did GTA 5 Cost?" (Delta, gta-budget)

One hard idea: **GTA 5 reportedly cost about $265 million to make and market (a press report, not Rockstar's number), and it sold over $1 billion in its first three days.** One hero object: **a brass-and-paper balance scale**, cost pan against sales pan, under a hanging price tag.

## Three structures
1. **Scale (chosen).** Cost pan vs sales pan; the tip of the scale is the story, and the empty scale returns for GTA 6, so the end is frame 0 again.
2. A receipt printer counting costs. Rejected: would invite a cost breakdown nobody published.
3. A bar chart of budgets across games. Rejected: the infographic look, and other games' budgets are mostly estimates too.

## Native move
The scale tips as paper bundles land (4 for the reported cost, 15 for three days of sales: the ratio 3.75 matches 3.77). A tick per bundle; a thunk when the counter lands and the beam slams.
