## Description
How much did GTA 5 cost to make? In 2013 The Scotsman reported about 170 million pounds, roughly 265 million dollars, for making and marketing it together. Guinness World Records listed it as the largest videogame budget in 2013. That is a press report, not a number Rockstar or Take-Two published.

Then it launched. Take-Two says GTA 5 passed 1 billion dollars in retail sales in its first three days, almost four times the reported cost, faster than any entertainment release before it.

As of November 17, 2026, Take-Two hasn't given a number for GTA 6's budget. GTA 6: November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Did GTA 6 cost over a billion? Yes or no?
