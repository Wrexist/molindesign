**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- The GTA 5 cost is a **press report**, never Rockstar's or Take-Two's figure. The Scotsman (Sept 2013) reported a combined development **and marketing** budget of about £170 million. Every outlet that repeats it hedges it ("if this report … is to be believed", Kotaku; "supposed", MCV). The dollar conversion differs by outlet: $265M (most), $266M (Kotaku), $267M (Neowin), $276M (Guinness). The film says "about 265 million dollars", shows `≈ $265M` with `£170M · REPORTED` and `THE SCOTSMAN · SEPT 2013` on the same tag, says "making it and marketing it, together", and has a whole line for the dip: "But that's a press report, not Rockstar's number."
- I could not open The Scotsman's original article (not surfaced by search; its archive did not come up). The figure rests on four independent outlets that link or name it (Kotaku, MCV, GameSpot, Guinness) plus Guinness's own record page. Who at Rockstar (if anyone) gave the number is contested (some pages say art director Aaron Garbut; nobody quotes him), so the film names only the newspaper.
- The **$1 billion in three days** is Take-Two's own estimate of retail sales ("according to Company estimates"), so the screen says `TAKE-TWO · RETAIL SALES`. "Sales" is revenue at retail, not Take-Two's profit; the film never says "profit" or "made back".
- "Almost four times" is our arithmetic: over $1,000M / about $265M = 3.77. On screen `NEARLY 4×` with `THE REPORTED COST` under it. Never "4×" bare, never "paid for itself".
- **GTA 6's budget is not disclosed.** Take-Two's November 2026 investor presentation (read today) gives no budget; Zelnick has said only that it "was expensive" (reported). Analyst estimates ($1–1.5B, Business Insider; $2B LinkedIn post; $3B) are not used, not even as a rumor: the end question ("Did it cost over a billion? Yes or no?") asks, it does not claim.
- Evergreen: no "isn't out yet", "coming", "in X days". The GTA 6 tie is the date-safe "GTA 6: November 19, 2026." and a question.
