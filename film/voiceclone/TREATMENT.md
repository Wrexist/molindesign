# Treatment: How AI clones a voice from three seconds

Factory #18 · `voiceclone` · voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), every beat keyed to a word with `at(id, word)`.

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from:** incognito (our last pass, 7.5 / Truth 9.0). The hook is the source's own words, one mechanism runs from hook to end, the stake is dated and says "warned", and the tip comes from the same official page. The hero hold sits in a planned silence with its own caption.
- **Don't take:** math and wifi (parked). Don't freeze before the stamp, don't leave an empty stage under a wipe, don't run a dead tail, don't use coral on wipes or captions, and don't let the hook and the body explain different things.

## Three structures
1. **Tape (chosen).** One paper prop is the whole mechanism: sound, then numbers, then predicted numbers, then sound. The paper card proves it, the FBI folder dates the stake, and the folded card answers it.
2. **Phone call first.** Open on a ringing phone labelled LOVED ONE? and explain backwards. It's a stronger cold open, but frame 0 would show the scam (the answer), not the conflict, and it pushes the mechanism past 15 s.
3. **Side-by-side clips.** A real 3 s clip next to a cloned one. It needs real audio of a person and comes close to a how-to. Rejected.

## Native move
The voice tape is a cream paper roll with 8 rows of number tiles. It feeds out of a cut-paper mic, keeps feeding past a pencil line (PREDICTED) and plays through a paper speaker. Its digits are decorative (0–1023, the codebook size).
