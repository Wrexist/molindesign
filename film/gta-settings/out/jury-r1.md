## Truth findings (verified against the pages today)
1. **The blur verdict contradicts its own sources (main reason for the FAIL).**
   - PC Gamer's Ridley, in the sentence right after the one we quote: "I wouldn't blame anyone for turning it off entirely, especially on a more powerful console where the frame rate may not dip as much."
   - He also rejects the judder argument: "whoever says that is no friend of mine".
   - Gamerbolt's own top pick is "Motion Blur Strength: 0 for clarity" (judder is only its caveat).
   - The voice cuts "Zero can look choppy." straight to "A PC Gamer editor picked one." That implies PC Gamer chose 1 because zero is choppy, which is the reason he explicitly dismissed.
   - The FIX stamp and "Don't copy these" then count a setting both sources call fine as one the viewer shouldn't copy.
   - The quote chip also drops "on the standard PS5", which the treatment specified.
2. **HIS TV · ITS MAX 600 (18.9-21.5 s) is made up.** No source says the poster's TV peaks at 600. The `PER GAMERBOLT HDR GUIDE` chip sits under `HIS TV`, so it reads as if Gamerbolt said so. Gamerbolt only says: match your TV, "avoid copying an arbitrary brightness number from somebody else's setup". The TVs also don't carry the `ILLUSTRATION` tag that BRIEF §5 and TREATMENT require (only the blur 0/1 panels do).
3. Fine as is:
   - Default 3 / max 10 matches PC Gamer, and playday also says 3 default.
   - "Runs at 30 frames a second" is fair: Rockstar says "targeting 30 frames per second", PC Gamer calls it "30-fps-or-bust", and Gamerbolt says "fixed 30fps", with the chip saying TARGETING.
   - Brightness Reduction "ships off" matches playday ("off default") and gamer.org ("Off by default"). "Leave it off unless bright light bothers you" is close to gamer.org and is labeled OUR PICK.
   - Effects sliders at 5: the card carries a VIRAL TIP note, not a DEFAULT tag. Good.
   - Opinion is mostly labeled (OUR VERDICTS, OUR PICK, OUR DRAWING on every menu card at frame level).
4. SAFE-EDIT checklist:
   - No footage, no trailer or radio music, no leak.
   - Every number is in BRIEF §3 (3, 10, 30, 0, 5→2, 600/50/50).
   - The rumor-as-fact item fails only through finding 1: opinion stated as a verdict against its own sources.
   - Every drawn menu is labeled OUR DRAWING (except where the scorecard caption covers it, 26.3-28.2 s). Nothing passes as real Rockstar UI: own type and colors, no logo.
