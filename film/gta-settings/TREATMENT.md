# Don't copy these settings: treatment

Delta, launch week (day 2). A viral four-tip "make GTA 6 look insane" list, fact-checked against our sources, ending on a scorecard: KEEP · FIX · FIX · SKIP. Voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), no music, no footage: every menu is our own drawing and says so.

## Benchmarks
| Film | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| gta-heroes (Delta, case-file desk) | One hero object per beat, a physical native move on every stressed word (the redaction tear), hits only in the pauses the voice really leaves (vo/vad.js), a frame-0 object that drops back on at the seam. | The desk set, the manila cards, the stopwatch and the same-centred card on every beat (sibling jury: "small centred window, same layout every beat"). |
| gta-storylength remake (passed 7.3 after a new set) | Slammed values, never rolled; one stake; a hand-built prop that reads as made by hand. | The punch card and button box; three small props per beat. |

## Three structures
1. **Scorecard sheet + four close-ups (chosen).** Frame 0 is the printed settings sheet full width with a red tape strip DON'T COPY THESE SETTINGS; the camera dives into each row in turn, so each tip becomes a full-frame object (a giant slider, a flipbook, three stacked sliders, an HDR card, two TVs, a giant toggle). The verdict stamp lands on the close-up, and the sheet comes back for the scorecard and the loop.
2. Four sticky notes on a TV bezel, each torn off in turn. Rejected: the TV would be the same centred window on every beat.
3. A ranked tier list (S to F). Rejected: tiers imply a ranking the sources don't give; the brief's verdicts are KEEP / FIX / SKIP.

## Native move
**The slider slam.** A big hand-cut slider (track 760+ px, knob 130 px) whose knob slams to a value with a ratchet tick per notch, then a rubber verdict stamp drops beside it: KEEP (cream ink on teal), FIX (coral), SKIP (coral, struck). Toggles slam the same way.

## Set
A deep plum cut-paper wall lit by a TV-glow wash whose colour shifts with each tip (blue for blur, amber for HDR), two soft sunset paper bands drifting behind (periodic in the film's length, so the loop is exact). Every menu is a cream paper card with our own type (Archivo condensed, Geist Mono labels), never Rockstar's UI font, colours or icons, and carries a 36 px OUR DRAWING tag. Illustrations (the car frames, the TVs) carry ILLUSTRATION.

## Composition plan (vary it, never the same centred card twice in a row)
- hook: sheet full width, pushed in 1.08 and easing out; tape strip diagonal across the top half.
- blur: push into row 1; the slider card fills the width at y 1000, knob huge.
- fps: slider card slides up and shrinks to a strip at the top; a flipbook fills the lower two thirds and riffles.
- choppy: split screen, two illustration panels left/right, the slider back as a bottom strip moving 0 to 1.
- fx: three stacked sliders tilted, filling y 520-1300.
- hdr: HDR card offset left, the sticky note right; on "Don't" the note rips off and flies out.
- tv: two TVs, big one right, small one left low (the DM frame).
- br/off: a giant toggle low in frame, the ON note slapped on top, then peeled; sun icon high right.
- end: the sheet returns full width; stamps uncovered row by row; tape and covers drop back on for the loop.

## Sound
Ratchet ticks on each slam (one per notch), a stamp thump per verdict, tape rips, a page riffle for the flipbook, a toggle clack, a swish on each dive. Hits sit in pauses (vo/vad.js), dry, one per gap.
