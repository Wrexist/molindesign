# Treatment: GTA 6 lets aliens take you (gta-wildfinds)

## Benchmarks
- **@blockay, "Top 5 Coolest GTA 6 Easter Eggs" (Isac: "perfect content").** Learn: a fast countdown where each item is a new real gameplay picture inside 3 s, and the number is said before the clip lands. Don't take: his facecam, his one-word captions, his 62 s length, or long clip run-ons. We cap every creator use near 1.6 s and frame it inside our own card.
- **gta-eastereggs (Delta, same format).** Learn: phrase captions at 96 px, a ring that follows the thing in the clip, HE SAYS chips for game-side claims, a loop line that finishes in the hook. Don't take: its ink stripe backdrop, the corner-slam #N tile, the dated paper tickets, the drawn hero egg. This film keeps real Extended Look gameplay behind most frames (rule 11 plates) and has its own countdown object: a dial.

## Three structures
1. **Straight countdown 5 → 1 with the UFO teased at frame 0, loop line into the hook.** Number one is told first ("That's number one of five"), then counted back down to it. (chosen: the brief's script and the strongest hook)
2. UFO only, as a how-to (where, when, the trophy). One clip can't fill 30 s and stays under the "list" demand of the format.
3. Rank by "how real is it" (rumor → confirmed). Needs a verification we don't have for each item; the HE SAYS framing is more honest.

## Native move
**The beam drop.** Every clip card falls out of the top of the frame inside a coral cone of light, like something being lowered by the UFO, and lands with a knock on the countdown **dial** (a coral ring, big numeral, 5 tick marks that go out one by one). At #1 the drawn beam is replaced by the real one: the clip itself turns white. The dial is the one object that carries the countdown; it knocks down and re-prints at every number (no prop carries a number across items).

## Frame 0 and the loop
Frame 0: the night plate (dimmed real GTA 6 night road), a giant coral beam cone filling the middle (≥ 60 % of the frame) already sweeping, the tall `ufo` card inside it with Lucia under the light, and `ALIENS TAKE YOU?` (3 words). By 0.3 s the beam has widened and the card has started to rise. By ~1.0 s the clip reaches the white engulf (payoff on screen by 2.0 s). The last ~0.6 s rebuilds the same frame: beam, card, words, on the trailing "and…".
