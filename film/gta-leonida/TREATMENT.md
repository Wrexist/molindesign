# Leonida region by region (GTA 6, Delta)

Vertical 1080x1920 Short, about 36 s. One hard idea: **Rockstar names six places in Leonida on its own site; walk them on a map we drew ourselves.** Own graphics, own voice, no Rockstar footage, logo, key art or map art, no music. Positions on our silhouette are approximate (labeled on screen) except Mount Kalaga (northern border) and Ambrosia (heart of Leonida), which Rockstar's text places.

## Benchmarks
- **Kurzgesagt-style flat explainers**: learn flat hand-built shapes, one color per idea, icons that pop on a spring. Don't take the slow cosmic pacing.
- **Vice-City-era retro posters (striped sun, palms, neon outline)**: learn the palette and the striped sun. Don't take grid floors, lens glow or chrome text.

## Three structures
1. **Ranked list** (top 6 places): rejected, ranking is an opinion Rockstar didn't give.
2. **Fly-over camera** across a 3D-ish map: rejected, reads as Rockstar's map art and hides the one fact (six names).
3. **The spotlight tour** (chosen): one silhouette stays on screen; a disc of light lands on one region, its icon pops, its card quotes Rockstar's own words. Ends on the date and a one-word comment question; the last frame dissolves into frame 0.

## Native move
**The spotlight.** A colored disc of light lands on a region; the previous ones stay as a dim glow, so by the end the whole state is lit. A cream light runs around the coastline throughout, so the map never sits still.
