## Three structures
1. **The bowl, the page, the port, the dry shelf (chosen).** One bowl of rice opens and closes the film. Apple's support page lists what not to do; rice lands on the list; we push into the port to show why; then Apple's own fix, step by step, and the alert as the check.
2. **Two phones side by side** (rice vs air). Reads as a lab test Apple never ran: a Truth risk, and it locks one layout.
3. **A countdown of five don'ts.** A list, not one surprise (LESSONS: one stake, no lists).

## The native move: the grain pour
Rice falls as a stream of cut-paper grains. At `hook` the phone sinks into the bowl while grains pour beside it. At `twist` the move turns into the mechanism: one big grain cracks (on "bits") into fragments that drift into a 610 px port and lodge on the pins. At `tap` the phone lifts out of the rice and taps on a block; water drops fall, not grains. At `end` the grains still pour and the phone drops back into the bowl (the loop seam).

## Rules for this film
- Frame 0 ≤ 4 words (iPHONE IN RICE?), no UI, the bowl 800 px.
- Coral only on the bowl's rim and the one COULD DAMAGE IT stamp. YES / NO are cream.
- "Could" and "can", never "rice doesn't work". No RICE KILLS PHONES, MYTH BUSTED or DOESN'T WORK anywhere.
- The only drying-time number on screen is Apple's "…UP TO 24 HOURS TO FULLY DRY." (clipping at `day`); the clock has no numerals; no wait number anywhere.
- Apple's credit only on Apple's re-created UI: the page card, the page clippings (each a re-created strip of the same page with its own RE-CREATED tag) and the alert. Our own labels (CLASSIC FIX?, WHY NOT?, PORT DOWN, LATER, STILL WET / KEEP DRYING, YOUR HAND) carry no credit. No PORT label and no CHECK YOURS card (both cut for read time).
- One RE-CREATED tag per frame: search bar → page card → twist clipping (corrode lines join it) → airflow clipping → 24-hours clipping → alert, never two at once.
- Alert scope iPHONE XS, XS MAX, XR OR LATER at 46 px cream beside the alert; the alert is Apple's name, Liquid Detected, with no button tapped and no "blocked" state.
- Generic phone (punch-hole, no logo). No people: the hand is a plain cream block labelled YOUR HAND.
- Chips print on or after their word; BAG OF RICE and the stamp wait for their words.
