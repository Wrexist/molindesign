## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**Apple's own iPhone support page says don't put a wet iPhone in a bag of rice, because small rice particles could damage it; tap it out, let air dry it for up to a day, and don't charge it while the port is still wet.**

**Hook follows the source's strength (LESSONS wifibattery, incognito).** The hook is Apple's own sentence, shortened: "Don't put your iPhone in a bag of rice." → **"Don't put your iPhone in rice."** (6). It says *iPhone*, because the source is Apple's page about iPhone; the film makes no claim about Android phones. Apple doesn't say rice "doesn't work" or "makes it worse"; it says particles "could" damage it. The voice keeps "could".

**Scope in the voice, not only in small print (LESSONS restartphone):** "your iPhone" in `hook` and `twist`, "Apple" in `ask`, "Its own page" in `page`, "might" in `day`, "could" in `twist`, "can" in `dip`.
