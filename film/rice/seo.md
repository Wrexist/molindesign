Don't put your iPhone in rice. That's Apple's own advice: "Don't put your iPhone in a bag of rice. Doing so could allow small particles of rice to damage your iPhone."
So what should you do with a wet iPhone? Tap it gently against your hand with the connector facing down, leave it in a dry area with some airflow, and don't charge it while it's wet. Apple's liquid-alert page says it might take up to 24 hours to fully dry.

Apple's list of things not to do, from "If you see a liquid-detection alert on your iPhone": "Don't dry your iPhone using an external heat source or compressed air. Don't insert a foreign object, such as a cotton swab or a paper towel, into the connector. Don't put your iPhone in a bag of rice. Doing so could allow small particles of rice to damage your iPhone." Apple says rice "could" damage it; the page doesn't say whether rice dries a phone.

Why not charge it wet: "If you charge your iPhone while the Lightning or USB-C connector is wet, the pins on the connector or cable can corrode and cause permanent damage or stop functioning". In an emergency, Apple says "you have the option in an emergency to override the liquid detection and charge your iPhone."

How to dry it (same page): "Tap your iPhone gently against your hand with the connector facing down to remove excess liquid. Leave your iPhone in a dry area with some airflow." Apple's water-resistance page adds: "Placing your iPhone in front of a fan blowing cool air directly into the Lightning or USB-C connector might help the drying process."

How long to wait? Apple's two pages put it differently. The liquid-alert page (102643): "After at least 30 minutes, try charging with a Lightning or USB-C cable or connecting an accessory." and "It might take up to 24 hours to fully dry." The water-resistance page (108039): "Allow at least 5 hours before charging with a Lightning or USB-C cable or connecting an accessory."

Check yours: try charging later. Apple: "If you see the alert again, there is still liquid in the connector or under the pins of your cable." The alert works on "iPhone XS, iPhone XS Max, iPhone XR, or later".
