**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- The caller's hook, "Only one game has ever outsold GTA 5", **can't be said cleanly**: The Tetris Company says "over 520 million units have been sold worldwide", which beats GTA 5 if you add up every Tetris release since 1984. Reliable sources split on whether that sum counts as one game (Wikipedia's list puts Tetris and Minecraft jointly at #1 for that reason; Guinness gives the record to Minecraft). So the film **asks** instead of claiming ("Which game outsold GTA 5? Guess."), takes Tetris on openly as the viewer's likely guess, says what its number counts, and only then names Minecraft as the one **single** game that sold more. The caveat is spoken and on screen.
- **"Sold" only as each source says it.** Take-Two's November 2026 investor presentation says GTA V "Nearly 235 million units sold to date". Take-Two counts units **sold-in** (shipped to stores plus digital): its 10-Q filed the same day says "has sold-in over 230 million units". The voice says "nearly 235 million sold" plus "Take-Two's own count"; the screen carries `TAKE-TWO COUNT · UNITS SOLD-IN` small under the number. Minecraft is Microsoft's own "copies sold" count. Different companies, different counts: the comparison chip says `EACH COMPANY'S OWN COUNT`.
- **No GTA 6 sales prediction** anywhere. The end is a question only ("Can GTA 6 catch Minecraft?"). No analyst forecast, no pre-order number (Take-Two says pre-orders are "tracking far above our initial expectations", but gives no number; not used).
- **No trade dress.** No falling Tetris pieces in a well, no Minecraft grass block or pixel font, no Mario, no Wii logo. Each rival game is a plain paper game case with its name in our type.
