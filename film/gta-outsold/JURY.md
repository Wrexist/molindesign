## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **5.6–6.7 s and 24.4–26.4 s: counter noise (Truth blocker, repeats noise r2).** Both counters show random exact-looking values (`232,872,515`; `422,314,912` right over the Xbox CEO chip). Change `film.js` lines 541 and 574: drop the `hash(...) * 99999` term and roll in whole millions. For example, `Math.floor(v/1e6)*1e6` rendered as `232,000,000`, or better as `232 MILLION` in round steps, then land on `235,000,000` with `NEARLY`. Keep the grey, untagged roll. Re-render, then check frames 6.0–6.7 and 25.5–26.4 from `out/frames/`. No frame may show a non-round value.
2. **SFX hits level with or over the voice (Voice/sound, repeats password r2, gta-vicecity r1).** Measured pause hits: 30.2 s bracket snap −2.4 dBFS, 2.5 s and 7.1 s −2.8, 22.3–22.8 s rip −3.2, 27.0 s −3.1; voice peaks are −2.7 to −3.0. Lower the loudest hits (snap, thunks, rip) by about 3 dB in SCORE, or raise `duck.smr_db` from 23 to about 26. Re-measure on the final mix: every hit peak ≤ −6 dBFS, and ≥ 3 dB under the line before it.
3. **Wow, picture only, no new voice: make the gap undeniable (Share +).** In the 30.0–30.6 s pause, or across the `more` line, drop the two knocked-out Nintendo towers (72M + 82.9M = 154.9M, both on the scale) into the `190M+` bracket. They stack and still don't fill it. Add one ≥ 48 px chip, `BOTH NINTENDO GIANTS: STILL SHORT`, and list "72 + 82.9 = 154.9M < 190M" in BRIEF.md as derived. This is the DM moment BRIEF §1 promised ("that gap alone is bigger than any Mario Kart"), and the film currently never shows it. Keep it inside the existing beat (LESSONS: a fix round never adds a voice beat). If that feels too close to a new beat, do it as a poster-only hold.
4. **11–19.6 s: string clutter and floating Nintendo tags (Clarity, Craft).** Up to eight hanging strings run through the captions and towers, and the Mario Kart / Wii Sports tags hang at the 300–450M height above towers under 100M. The 235M dashed line also cuts through the Wii Sports tag. Tape each tag to its own tower top, with no strings, or keep only the string of the tag being spoken about. Each tag sits ≤ 40 px above its tower.
5. **26.5–30.5 s poster: `190M+` covers the `300M` ruler label, and the hero fills only y 580–1400.** Fade the ruler labels the bracket overlaps, or move `190M+ MORE` 60 px left/up off the ruler. Push the poster camera in about 1.2× so Minecraft's plate tops near y 420 and the towers' base sits near y 1430. That fills the empty band above, and the 520 px blank floor shrinks.
6. **22.4–26.5 s: the `#1 SINGLE GAME` stamp sits under `XBOX CEO · X POST · SEPT 2026`.** With the sound off it reads as part of her post. Give the stamp its own credit (`GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS`, ≥ 36 px), or move it below the counter, away from the quote chip, and only after the counter lands.
7. **10.5 s onward: `SOLD-IN` disappears.** From 10.5 s to the end the GTA 5 tag reads only `NEARLY 235M · TAKE-TWO · NOV 2026`, so the poster compares "sold-in" against "copies sold" without saying so. Add `SOLD-IN` as a third line on the hanging GTA 5 tag; `EACH COMPANY'S OWN COUNT` then has something to point at.
8. **0.0–2.4 s: frame-0 still.** The cover reads as a fluted pillar, and the GTA 5 stack has no plate until about 2.4 s. Put the `GTA 5` plate on the stack from frame 0. Give the sheet a visible draped top and a corner lifting about 15° (the "something is under here" cue) on "Guess." at 2.4 s. Keep the frame-0 words at 5.
9. **17.0–19.6 s: the Tetris fan covers the GTA 5 tower and the `?` cover and reaches about x 926.** Pivot the fan from the Tetris tower's own base so it stays right of the GTA 5 tower and ≤ x 900. Keep `EVERY TETRIS GAME, ADDED UP` from overlapping the cover.
10. **7.6–10.4 s and 31.5–35.5 s: soft stretches.** On "Take-Two's own count", underline `SOLD-IN` with a pen stroke and nudge the camera 1.0 → 1.08×. On the GTA 6 beat, let the calendar page flip from `NOV 15` to `19` on "nineteenth", so something moves in each 1.5 s window.

## Looks generic / AI-made?
No. The paper cases with grain, the kraft floor and the hand-cut tags read as hand-made, and the scale is honest. The one template smell is the hanging-tag-on-strings device used on every beat (fix 4). The Tetris fan uses a pastel rainbow of six case colours; that's fine, but don't push it closer to a tetromino palette.
