# Treatment: "Which Game Outsold GTA 5?" (Delta, gta-outsold)

One hard idea: **GTA 5 has sold nearly 235 million, and only one single game has sold more: Minecraft, at over 425 million. Tetris claims more, but its number adds up every Tetris game since 1984.** One hero object: **the paper sales tower**, a stack of plain paper game cases per game (a blank label strip on each spine, the name on a cream plate in our type). Hero sounds: the counter thunk, the sheet rip, the bracket snap.

## Benchmarks
| | Learn | Don't take |

| film/gta-longestwait (held) | Paper towers and a kraft floor read as hand-made to both juries. | A countdown that carries one prop's number onto the next item: every number here sits on its own game's tower or tag, with its owner in the same frame. |

## Three structures
1. **Sales floor with towers (chosen).** A covered tower waits beside GTA 5 from frame 0; guesses come and go beside it (two short Nintendo towers knocked flat, a Tetris tower that splits into a fan of single cases); the sheet rips off to show Minecraft; a bracket measures the gap; GTA 6 gets an empty spot.
2. A bar chart on a whiteboard. Rejected: the infographic look that held five Delta films.
3. A shop shelf with game boxes. Rejected: boxes invite box art (trade dress).

## Native move
The chart rescales: the pencil height lines on the wall (100M steps) slide as the zoom changes (2.0 → 1.5 → 1.15 → 1.25 → 1.55 → 1.3 px per million), while towers build case by case, so every new game lands in the same honest scale.

## Rules this cut is built to pass
- Frame 0: caption `WHICH GAME OUTSOLD GTA 5?` (5 words), the GTA 5 tower already building (45 % of its cases, all by 0.9 s), the covered tower with a pink `?`. The GTA 5 plate lands on "GTA".
- Every number has its owner on the same tag: `NEARLY 235M · TAKE-TWO · NOV 2026`, `TAKE-TWO COUNT · UNITS SOLD-IN · NOV 2026`, `72M · NINTENDO`, `82.9M · NINTENDO`, `520M+ CLAIMED · THE TETRIS COMPANY`, `425,000,000+ COPIES SOLD` + `XBOX CEO · X POST · SEPT 2026`. No GTA 6 number.
- Counters roll in grey with no tag and print `NEARLY` / `COPIES SOLD` only once they land.
- Tetris and Minecraft are plain cases with their names only: no falling pieces, no blocks, no green, no pixel font. No Nintendo or Rockstar art.
- `EACH COMPANY'S OWN COUNT` sits beside the `190M+ MORE` bracket.
- YES / NO identical; nothing on the GTA 6 spot hints at an answer.
