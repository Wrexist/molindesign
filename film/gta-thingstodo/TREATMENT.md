# Treatment: Nine things to do in GTA 6 (gta-thingstodo)

## Benchmarks
- **gta-eastereggs (Delta, jury 7.1 pass).** Learn: a fast countdown where every item is proved by a real gameplay shot inside 3 s, phrase captions at 96 px, a tracked circle on every clip, a knock in the pause after each number. Don't take: the different dated card per item (this film has no real-world context to add), the yellow second accent, the countdown tile that slams from a corner (our number lives in its own slot instead).
- **gta-sametrailer (Delta, six jury rounds).** Learn: full-width plates read as footage, not a dashboard; one clip per point. Don't take: split / two-window layouts (they capped Craft at ~4), the long unbroken footage stretches.

## Three structures
1. **The to-do grid fills itself (chosen).** Nine slots on screen from frame 0 at 0/9. Each item: its slot lights on the spoken number, the clip bursts out of that slot into a full-width plate, then the plate flips into our pictogram and drops back into the slot with a tick, and the counter rolls. The ninth item (BASE jump) gets two shots and the biggest hit. End on the full grid + WHICH FIRST?
2. A ranked top 9 (worst to best). Needs a ranking we can't source, and "best" is an opinion the jury reads as a claim.
3. A day-planner (morning gym, noon airboat, night race). Cute, but invents a schedule and spends words on clock times.

## Native move
**Slot → plate → slot.** The footage never just cuts in: it grows out of its own slot (the other eight slots slide down into a strip under the plate, so the count is always on screen), and when the line ends the plate flips over into our pink pictogram card and shrinks back into the slot. One move, nine times, from nine different places on screen, so no two beats share the same geometry. The grid is the goal-gradient: the viewer wants to see 9/9.

## Frame 0 and the loop
Frame 0: the BASE-jump freefall over the city, full width and 1080 px tall, already moving, under `9 THINGS TO DO` (4 words) with the GTA 6 pill and a `0/9` counter. At 0.8 s the plate shrinks into slot 9 and the empty grid is there by 1.2 s (payoff structure on screen before 2.0 s); "Count along" ripples the nine slots. The last 0.45 s: the full 9/9 grid un-ticks, slot 9 grows back into the same freefall plate at the same source frame, and the hook words return, so the last frame is frame 0.

## Sound
Voice + kit SFX only (no music). A pop on each slot as its number is spoken, a swish as the plate grows, a stamp-thud + tick as the pictogram lands, a ratchet on the counter. The BASE jump gets a long whoosh (wind) and a slam when the canopy opens; 9/9 gets a ding. Every hit sits in a silence of the voice track (vo/vad.js).
