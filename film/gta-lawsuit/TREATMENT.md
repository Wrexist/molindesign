# gta-lawsuit — treatment

**One idea:** Lindsay Lohan sued GTA 5 under a 1903 New York law and lost 6-0 ("not recognizable"), but the same ruling says a game character can count as a portrait. One loss, one twist.

## Three structures
1. **Case file (chosen).** The desk is the courtroom: one manila folder opens on a verdict box (WON / LOST), the evidence lands on top of it beat by beat (complaint, alley, statute book, courthouse, magnifier), the folder comes back for the 6-0 and the stamp, then the 1903 book comes back for the twist, the folder peels away to a gilt frame of nameless pixel tiles with its condition in big type, and the folder closes on the loop. (Remake 2026-11-18: no GTA 6 date beat, no Lacey card near the frame.)
2. Courtroom diorama (a paper court with judges and parties). Rejected: needs people, and the brief bans any likeness.
3. Timeline ribbon (1903 → 2014 → 2018 → 2026 on one long strip). Rejected: a list, not a story; no test for the viewer.

## Native move
**Covers lift off, papers land on top.** Every beat is a new piece of paper dropped onto the pile from above (folder, complaint, alley panel, the green book, the ruling page); covers (folder, book) lift off upward to show what's inside. The hero sound is the oxblood rubber stamp: the stamp tool comes down, slams, lifts, and leaves `DISMISSED` in bled ink. The brass gavel is the second sound (hook, court).

Exits go up (the top of the frame is free); nothing crosses x 90–930 or y 1430. The felt desk bleeds the whole frame; only texture lives outside the rail. No push-ins: the camera only drifts ±7 px.
