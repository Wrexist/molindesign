- **Only the court record.** Everything said about Lindsay Lohan and Karen Gravano is what the Court of Appeals opinion and the AP/Reuters reports say: who sued, when, what she claimed, what the court held. - **Exactly what the court held (Lohan, 2018 NY Slip Op 02208).** (1) "an avatar (that is, a graphical representation of a person, in a video game or like media) may constitute a 'portrait'" under Civil Rights Law article 5; (2) the Lacey Jonas character and the two loading-screen ("transition screen") images "are not recognizable as plaintiff", so the amended complaint "was properly dismissed". Voice says "She lost" (Reuters: "rejected Lindsay Lohan's appeal"). The stamp says `DISMISSED` with a small `AFFIRMED 6-0 · 2018` under it, which is exact.
- **6-0.** The opinion: Fahey, J., with DiFiore, Rivera, Stein, Garcia and Feinman concurring; "Judge Wilson took no part". Six voting, none against; Reuters: "By a 6-0 vote". If the director draws seven seats, the seventh is labeled `TOOK NO PART`, never "voted against".

## 1. One hard idea
**Lindsay Lohan sued GTA 5 over a character she called her look-alike, under a New York law from 1903, and lost 6-0 because the court found the character was "not recognizable" as her. But the same ruling says a video-game character can count as a person's "portrait".** One loss, one twist.
