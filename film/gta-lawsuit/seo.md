## Description
Lindsay Lohan sued GTA 5. Did she win?

In 2014 she sued Take-Two and Rockstar Games in New York, saying the GTA 5 character Lacey Jonas, an "actress slash singer" hiding from paparazzi, was her look-alike. Her weapon was a New York privacy law from 1903: use someone's name, portrait or picture to sell something without written consent, and they can sue.

In 2018, New York's top court, the Court of Appeals, ruled 6-0 against her (one judge took no part). It called Lacey "a generic artistic depiction of a 'twenty something' woman" who is not recognizable as Lohan, so the case stayed dismissed.

The twist: the same ruling says a video-game avatar can count as a person's "portrait" under that law. In New York, if a game character is recognizably you, the 1903 law can apply.
