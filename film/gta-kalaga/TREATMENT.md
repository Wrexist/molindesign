# A mountain · treatment (Delta, GTA 6)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-gators/** (Delta, held at 6.7–7.2): keep its engine (voice-first `at(id, word)` keying, the `vo/vad.js` SFX gate, `fontStretch` re-applied after every `ctx.font`, seeded paper pieces with grain, pale cut edges and baked shadows: the only Delta look both juries called hand-made). Don't take its story shape (a second region diluted one stake) or its layout (box rim, swamp panels, dip through water, workbench card). Nothing is reused from its scene.
- **film/qrcode/** (Tech, explained, passed at 8.0): one physical prop that keeps changing state carries the whole film. Here that prop is a pop-up book whose page keeps sprouting new pop-ups on Rockstar's words.

## Three structures
1. **Map card + quote cards** (Kalaga marked on a flat map, then quote slips). Rejected: the dashboard look that held five Delta films, and a marked map would invent a location Rockstar hasn't given.
2. **Height ladder** (Kalaga against real mountains). Rejected outright: Rockstar gives no height, so any comparison is our claim, and it would put the REAL WORLD number on the mountain.
3. **One pop-up book, one camera** (chosen). Frame 0 is the paper mountain already rising out of the fold; it snaps up on "mountain". The camera slides off the book to a separate cardstock ruler on the bench for the one real-world number, comes back, and every Rockstar phrase pops a small feature up out of the paper. It ends by folding flat and re-opening into frame 0.

## The native move
**The pop-up.** Three cut layers (orange back ridge, teal mid ridge with pines, pink front slope) stand up from the fold with a staggered lag, V-fold glue tabs at their feet, and a pull tab slides out of its slot on the map as they rise. The same mechanic answers each quote: deer on "hunting", fish on "fishing", dirt bike on "off-road ... trails", two paired cabins on "mystics" and "radicals", the vote panels at the end.

## Rules this cut is built to pass (LESSONS, Delta)
- Frame 0: the mountain is already rising (big, bright, moving), GTA 6 sticker + MOUNTAIN? panel = 3 words; "GTA 6" spoken at 0.2 s; visible change by 0.3 s (mean pixel diff 5.6 between 0 and 0.3 s).
- One place (Mount Kalaga), one stake, one hero number: 345 FT, on the ruler set only; the poster is taken in the written pause with the stamp landed and no caption.
- The REAL WORLD ruler is its own cardstock object on the bench, right of the book, with a REAL WORLD chip, FLORIDA'S HIGHEST POINT · BRITTON HILL tag and SOURCE: USGS chip. Nothing touches or measures the mountain; nothing says Leonida is Florida; nothing gives Kalaga a height (the tape at the end is blank, with a ?).
- Rockstar's words on torn newsprint strips, byte-exact from the Kalaga page (curly quotes and ’, … at every cut), never highlighted, never mixed with our words; one source chip; every strip peels off before the next lands, and all three stay on the one place.
- Everything is clipped to x 90–930, y 300–1430, so no camera scale can push a shape over the rail; pixel-scanned on every rendered frame.
- Sound only in written pauses, gated on `vo/vad.js`; each hit ≥ 3 dB under its neighbouring words on the final mix. No music.
- End vote: MOUNTAIN and HILL panels identical (same paper, size, ink), popped together.
