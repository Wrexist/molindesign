## 1. Topic and one hard idea
**GTA 6's state has a mountain, by Rockstar's own name for it: Mount Kalaga.** Rockstar's page calls it "A national landmark up against the state’s northern border" with "prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails", and "hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals" in the backwoods "far from the prying eyes of the government." The juxtaposition the viewer completes: **in the real world, Florida tops out at 345 feet** (Britton Hill, USGS). One place (Mount Kalaga), one stake (a Mount, in a game everyone pictures as flat swamp and beach), one hero number (345).

**Lead with the surprise, not the context** (LESSONS gta-editions, gta-gators): the mountain is the first spoken noun and pops up on frame 0's paper; the REAL WORLD number lands by ~8 s. No "Leonida is a state with…" set-up.

**The Florida decision (why "In the real world, Florida tops out at 345 feet" is safe enough):** Rockstar never writes "Florida" on its GTA 6 site (grep of both pages 2026-11-01: 0 hits), so no line, chip or picture may say or show that Leonida *is* Florida. The line is a true real-world fact, scoped twice: in the voice ("In the real world") and on screen (REAL WORLD chip, USGS source). It sits on its own prop (a cardstock ruler on the bench, **outside** the Leonida pop-up map, never touching or measuring Mount Kalaga; LESSONS gta-gators: a theory card must not look like a reveal), and no line says "Leonida", "based on" or "like Florida". The viewer draws the line themselves; that is the hook. Rejected: "Real Florida? Its highest point is 345 feet." (the bare "Real Florida?" right after "Mount Kalaga" reads as "the real version of this place", i.e. it asserts the link).
