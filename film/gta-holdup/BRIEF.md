**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- **We never say it is the same scene.** Trailer 1's holdup (Dec 2023) and the Extended Look's holdup montage (Aug 2026) are different shots: different outfits (black tank top vs white tank top and a cap). What matches, and what we say: a **red bandana**, a masked partner behind, a low camera looking up the aisle, and **the same signs** in the aisle (the e-Cola "SPREAD THE FIZZ" banner and the Pißwasser $14.99 card, both shots, see §3 F5). We say "same sign", never "same store" or "different store": whether it is the same store location is not established. Our claim is "showed holdups again", never "the same holdup" or "a remake".
- **No names in voice or on screen.** The footage strongly suggests Lucia and Jason, but neither video names them in these shots, so the film says "red bandana" and "partner". (gta-cast's rule: a name on a face costs Truth unless checked; here we don't need it.)
- **"HUD" is what the frame shows**, not a feature claim: Extended Look 706–711 s shows, top right, six wanted stars, five icons, an ammo readout (13 / 80, later 20 / 73) and a pistol icon, over a third-person view of a robber in a store. We say "the HUD is on: wanted stars and an ammo count". We do **not** say "you can rob any store", "it's a mission", "it's playable" or how robberies work (guides describe that; Rockstar hasn't in its own words).
- **"In-game footage" is Rockstar's sentence** about the whole Extended Look ("Captured entirely from in-game footage on the PlayStation 5"). We don't say it about Trailer 1 (Rockstar's Trailer 1 copy says nothing like it), and we don't say "gameplay" for the whole video.
- No story: who they are robbing or why, what happens after, any mission names. The Extended Look's narration over this montage is an unrelated story (a carousel); we don't quote it.
