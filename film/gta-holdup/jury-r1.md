- A leak or ending shown: **pass.** Official trailers only, and the cut stops before the VI logo.

## Looks generic or AI-made
- The centred caption-pill → monitor → chip → rail stack on every beat, and a mono `CLIP · ROCKSTAR · …` kicker on every panel: the dashboard kit LESSONS keeps flagging.
- The NO SIGNAL SMPTE bars and TV snow as transitions are a stock "retro" template move. They only earn their place if they carry sound and cover a real cut, not a 1.3 s gap.
- The HUD redraw is a generic grey card with clip-art stars and a T-shaped gun. It reads less real than the actual HUD in Rockstar's frame. Show the real one big first.
- The red circle plus the yellow arrow plus the yellow-highlighted keyword in each caption: three emphasis systems on one frame (0-2.5 s).
