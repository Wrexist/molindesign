# GTA 6: Trailer 1 ended on this (treatment)

Delta, Trailer vs Reality Short (VIDEO-PLAN format 2), launch week. Ming US_James seed 7, no music, official Rockstar clips on our own drawn monitors.

## Benchmarks
| Film | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| "GTA 6 Trailer vs Gameplay Graphics" (469k, launch week Shorts list) | Two moments side by side read in under a second with the sound off; the dates do the explaining. | A plain split with no point beyond "looks similar": ours has one claim per beat (date, same bandana, the HUD, Rockstar's capture line). |
| gta-cast (Delta, previous film) | Footage inside a drawn object (cards), plates under our boards, captions on tape, the frame-0 layout returning for the loop. | Its lineup board, flap tiles and ballot. A new set per film. |

## Three structures
1. **Security monitors (chosen).** The topic is a store holdup, so every feed is a store's CRT security monitor: CAM 1 is Trailer 1 (DEC 04 2023), CAM 2 is An Extended Look (AUG 27 2026). The split puts CAM 1 over CAM 2; the vote is two monitors, 1 and 2.
2. Film strip vs game screen (a 35 mm strip for the trailer, a TV with a controller for the game). Rejected: it pre-judges "cinematic vs gameplay", which the film shouldn't claim for Trailer 1.
3. A calendar wall from Dec 2023 to Aug 2026. Rejected: a list of dates is the dull part; the footage is the story.

## Native move
**The channel switch**: static snow and a rolling bar, a TV-static fizz with a CRT thunk, whenever a monitor changes feed (Trailer 1 → NO SIGNAL test card → CAM ? → CAM 2 → static → split → HUD zoom → redrawn HUD → vote cards). A 2023 → 2026 rail along the bottom fills on "August".
