## Description
The GTA 6 trailer song? In trailer 1 it was Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road", a 1989 track from Full Moon Fever that was never released as a single, just a B-side. After the trailer came out in December 2023, Spotify told BBC Newsbeat that streams of the track rose 36,979% compared to the same time the week before. Spotify said soundtracks had spiked before, but never like this. Then trailer 2 (May 2025) did it again with the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" (1986).
