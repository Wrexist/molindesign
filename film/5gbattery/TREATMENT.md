Engine, gates and sound come from `../phonelistening/` (#33, best start yet: `CLIP_RUN`, `LABELS_RUN`, safe box, quote plates, peel/label/hold patterns, captions, SFX gate). ## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `phonelistening` (#33, r2 7.5, 411 views at 6 h):** one hero object with a physical fail and a physical pass, a summary drawn on the hero hold that becomes the poster, every quote cut with "…", scope in the same frame as every number, a punch-in on the payoff. **Don't take:** its black box and phone-with-ear (the hero prop changes every film) and its "?" flap; this film's frame 0 is the 5G icon itself next to a draining battery.
- **Learn from `darkmode` (#31, r2 7.4):** a lab meter can carry a hard number if the needle agrees with the voice on the word and never rests on a tick that reads as a claim. **Don't take:** its locked phone-left / meter-right layout held for 20 s. Here the layout changes twice in the middle third (bench at 5 s, settings at 22 s), and the meter itself is redrawn (two needles, printed tape, chalk chart over it).

## Three structures
1. **The lab bench (chosen).** The 5G icon peels off the status bar and rides a paper lead to a big bench power meter wired to a phone lying on its back. A trickle of data makes the 5G needle swing past the ghost 4G needle into the red and the meter prints a tape (fail, 79 %); a flood of data makes the 4G needle race past the calm 5G one and a second tape prints (pass, 5×). The two results become one chalk chart over the bench (hero hold, poster). Then the settings screens, where the 5G tile flips to LTE.
2. **Two batteries racing.** A 5G phone and an LTE phone draining side by side. Clear, but it turns an efficiency result into battery-life minutes the paper never gave (a Truth trap, BRIEF §5.1–5.2).
3. **The data pipe.** Data as water through a 5G pipe and an LTE pipe, a pump that costs more to keep running. Pretty, but abstract; the lab meter is a real object from the study (Monsoon power monitor) and reads as not-AI.

## The native move: the 5G tile
The "5G" glyph from frame 0's status bar is a two-sided paper tile. It peels off at `test` and rides the meter's lead as its label; at `auto` it is back in a status bar and flips to **LTE** on "drops to LTE" (Smart Data) and back on "won't help"; at `on` it stays stuck on 5G while the battery loses a notch; at `end` it is in frame 0's status bar again.

## Layouts (two changes across the middle third)

## Rules for this film
- Frame 0: four words (the status-bar **5G** plus **5G DRAINING IT?**, "?" coral), the phone top (≈ 780 × 880 px) gliding ≥ 330 px in 0.55 s, a battery icon with no number whose last segment blinks. The search card **DOES 5G DRAIN BATTERY** types in at ~1.5 s. The end card is the same picture.
- Every **79%** and **5×** shares its frame with **ONE PHONE · VERIZON · 2021** (≥ 36 px) and says **EFFICIENT**, never battery. No number inside or next to a battery icon. The meter face reads **ENERGY PER BIT**, so the needle shows efficiency, not charge; it rests on a peg outside the scale (darkmode lesson).
- The chart is **OUR SUMMARY · SIGCOMM 2021**, drawn without axis numbers.
- Apple and Google quotes word for word, "…" at every cut, tagged with the source. Settings: RE-CREATED, generic rows, no checkmark, no switch, neutral chevrons, MENU NAMES VARY.
- Coral only on frame 0's "?", the 5G line of the chart and YES / NO.
- Props stay whole inside x 92–928, y 302–1428; backgrounds bleed full width.
- SFX: dry hits in the written voice gaps only, end of line + 0.3 s: battery tick (hook), needle thump (slow), tape slap (twist), one dry chart hit (barely), pop (on).
