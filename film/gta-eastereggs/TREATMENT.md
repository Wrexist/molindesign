# Treatment: GTA 6 hid an actual egg (gta-eastereggs)

## Benchmarks
- **@blockay, "Top 5 Coolest GTA 6 Easter Eggs" (Isac: "perfect content").** Learn: a fast countdown where every item is proven by a real gameplay shot, each item a new picture inside 3 s. Don't take: his facecam, his one-word captions, his order, his 62 s length, his clip run-ons (we cap each use under 5 s and keep our own graphics between them).
- **gta-lootrun (Delta, jury 7.0 pass).** Learn: full-bleed footage band, phrase captions at 96 px, a tracked circle on every clip, a chip that says whose claim it is. Don't take: one centred hero that rides every beat (jury docked the same-centred layout), the small window, the dark static frame-0 backdrop.

## Three structures
1. **Straight countdown 5 → 1, egg last, loop.** Each item: number slam, clip with an annotation, one dated real-world card. The literal egg is #1, Atari's 1980 coinage closes, "forty-six years later" loops into the hook. (chosen)
2. Egg first, then "four more". Spends the payoff in the hook and needs the extra `five` line (+2.5 s, over the 32 s target).
3. Timeline 1980 → 2026 (Atari, F&F 2001, GTA 5 2013, Dexter 2009). Reads as a history lesson, the flop pattern in LESSONS.

## Native move
The **countdown number slam**: a huge numeral (250 px) drops onto each item's footage from a different corner, the number's paper tile in a new colour, a knock and a stamp sound in the pause the voice leaves after "Five." / "Four." / "Three." / "Two." / "One.". The **dated card** (a torn paper ticket with a big year) is the second recurring object and sits in a different place each item, so no two beats share a layout.

## Frame 0 and the loop
Frame 0: our drawn Easter egg in the in-game egg's colours (purple, magenta and yellow stripes) fills the middle and is already turning; `A REAL EASTER EGG?` (4 words) and the GTA 6 pill. By 0.9 s the egg flies up and the clip of the real egg thrown off the tower takes the frame, circled (payoff on screen by 1.2 s). The last 0.6 s rebuilds the same frame (egg back, same words), so the loop has no bump.
