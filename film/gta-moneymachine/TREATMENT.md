# GTA 5 is 13 years old. Why is it still selling? · treatment (Delta weekly long video, 16:9)

## Benchmarks
- **film/cables/** (our only 16:9 long-form): take its 1920x1080 page, word-keyed scene list, burned-in phrase captions at the bottom with stressed words coloured, `FILM.CHAPTERS` for YouTube. Don't take its one-character stickman look or its 5-minute single-set pacing; long-form on a new channel lost viewers there (21 views), so this one changes picture every 2-4 s.
- **film/gta-price/** (latest Delta Short): take the engine whole (voice-first `at(id, word)`, the `vo/vad.js` SFX gate, paper props with grain and pale cut edges, `fontStretch` re-applied after every font, quantized counters whose tags appear only once the number lands, foley in written pauses, no music). Don't take its backdrop: the synthwave sun, spotlights and flecks the jury called a generic template.

## Three structures
1. **One long ledger tape as the whole film.** Rejected: one prop for 3.7 minutes is the "single prop for 30 s" stall the juries called slow, times seven.
2. **A chart that grows for the whole film.** Rejected: a dashboard (held five Delta films) and the viewer has to read axes.
3. **Six named chapters on one ledger set, one hero object per line** (chosen). A heist told in reverse: the big score (3 days), the crash (the drop), the machine that took over (GTA Online, three console generations), the count (the split-screen), the hand-off (GTA 6). Each chapter opens on a full-frame paper card with a heavy clunk and closes on its quotable line built from word tiles.

## The set (new, not the synthwave template)
A dark accounting ledger: teal ruled lines drifting up, a pink double margin, column rules, a giant outlined chapter numeral; a stock-ticker crawl across the top carrying only sourced facts for the current chapter; a receipt-printer tape down the right edge.

## The native move
**The receipt tape prints every money number as it is said** (DAY 1 $800M+, DAY 3 $1B+, FY2015 −$1.3B, in-game 78.1%, the three copy counts, GTA's 68.9% and 12.4% share), feeding down with a ratchet in the pauses. It steps aside for chapter 5, where the split-screen takes the whole frame.

## Rules this cut is built to pass
- GTA on screen at frame 0 (a big blank `GTA 5` case gliding ~480 px in the first second, a `SEPT 2013` tag on its string); 4 words on frame 0; the search chip `HOW MUCH MONEY HAS GTA 5 MADE?` at 1.9 s.
- Type ≥ 36 px everywhere (captions 48 px, labels 40-64, hero numbers 150-330).
- Every number from BRIEF.md §5; our own arithmetic marked `OUR MATH`; `RE-CREATED` on the 10-K pages; quotes exact with "…"; nothing highlighted inside a quote.
- Split-screen (chapter 5): left pink `LAUNCH YEAR · FY2014` rolls to $1.6B and stops; right teal `EVERY YEAR AFTER · FY2015-FY2026` keeps rolling to about $9.0B while year chips stack; they slam together into `$10B+` with `NOT GTA 5 ALONE`.
- Chapter cards 2-3 s with a heavy clunk in the pause before the card line; sound only in VAD gaps; no music.
- End card from the `next` line to the end (≥ 10 s): `WHAT GTA 6 HAS TO GET RIGHT` end-screen slot and a subscribe slot.
