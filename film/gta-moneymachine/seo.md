What the video says, in short:
- Day one: over $800 million in sales (Take-Two's estimate, not counting Japan and Brazil). Day three: more than $1 billion. Take-Two said no game or movie had reached that milestone faster.
- The next fiscal year, Take-Two's GTA sales fell by about $1.3 billion, because the year before had the launch.
- GTA Online came free with every copy two weeks after launch; inside it, players could buy game cash with real money (Rockstar calls them Shark Cards).
- GTA 5 was sold again on three console generations: PS3 and Xbox 360 (2013), PS4 and Xbox One (2014), PC (2015), PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (2022).
- Over 10 billion dollars from GTA games to Take-Two from fiscal 2014 to fiscal 2026, and the launch year was less than a sixth of it.

Our own math (not Take-Two's): every year, Take-Two's annual report (10-K) says what share of its net revenue came from "Grand Theft Auto products". We multiplied that share by the year's total net revenue, for each fiscal year from FY2014 (April 2013 to March 2014) to FY2026 (April 2025 to March 2026), using each year's own 10-K. That gives about $1.6 billion for the launch year (68.9% of $2,350.6 million) and about $9.0 billion for the twelve years after, about $10.65 billion in total. 1.6 out of 10.65 is about 15%, less than a sixth. Scope: this is all GTA games (mostly GTA 5 and GTA Online, but also older GTA titles and the 2021 Trilogy), it is Take-Two's revenue after stores take their cut, not what players spent, and it is not "GTA 5 alone". Also our math: $1 billion in 72 hours is about $14 million an hour, and "before its first Friday" is our calendar check (September 17, 2013 was a Tuesday).

The spending-inside-games numbers (about a quarter of revenue in FY2017, over three quarters in FY2026) are for all of Take-Two's games, not GTA alone; half of Take-Two's FY2026 revenue came from mobile games, and it bought Zynga in May 2022.

Unit counts are as of Take-Two's August 2026 report (the 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, filed August 7, 2026: "over 230 million units" sold-in). Take-Two's November 9, 2026 release (fiscal Q2 2027) gives no new unit count and keeps GTA VI on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm "Sold-in" means copies sold to stores and digital storefronts, as Take-Two counts them.
