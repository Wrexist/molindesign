## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 5 did not make its money at launch: the record launch year ($1.6B to Take-Two, by our math) is less than a sixth of the over $10 billion GTA games brought Take-Two in the 13 years since, because the game kept being re-sold (three console generations) and kept selling inside itself (GTA Online, Shark Cards).**

Story angle: a heist in reverse. Chapter 1 is the big score everyone remembers ($1B in 3 days). Chapter 2 is the crash that should have ended it (the next year GTA sales fell about $1.3B). Chapters 3-5 are the machine that took over. Chapter 6 hands the keys to GTA 6. A person who never read a GTA article gets it: "how does one 2013 game still make money?"

**Main search phrase:** `how much money has GTA 5 made` (show as a chip on frame 0-5 s and in the YouTube title/description). Secondary: `GTA 5 sales`, `GTA 5 revenue`, `GTA Online Shark Cards`, `GTA 6 release date`.

### Our own arithmetic (marked "by our math" / "our math" in voice and `OUR MATH` on screen)
- **≈ $14 million an hour** (c1f): $1B ÷ 72 h = $13.9M. Uses F2's "more than $1 billion", so "about 14" holds.
- **GTA products revenue per fiscal year** = F12 share × F12 total ($M, rounded): FY14 1,620 · FY15 303 · FY16 770 · FY17 680 · FY18 712 · FY19 686 · FY20 710 · FY21 985 · FY22 1,083 · FY23 781 · FY24 786 · FY25 710 · FY26 825. **Sum FY14-FY26 = $10,652M → "over 10 billion dollars from GTA games"** (c5g). Launch year FY14 = $1,620M → "1.6 billion" (c5c). Years after (FY15-FY26) = $9,032M → "about 9 billion more" (c5f). Launch share 1,620 / 10,652 = 15.2% → "less than a sixth" (16.7%) (c5x).
  - Scope, said in the voice: these are **GTA games** (Take-Two's "Grand Theft Auto products": GTA V and GTA Online mostly, but also older GTA titles and the 2021 Trilogy), **Take-Two's revenue** (after stores take their cut), **as each 10-K first reported it** (later 10-Ks restate some years under new accounting; we use each year's own filing). It is not "GTA 5 made $10B" and not consumer spending. On screen: `GTA GAMES · TAKE-TWO REVENUE · FY2014-FY2026 · OUR MATH`.
  - FY2014 starts Apr 1 2013, five months before GTA V; that pre-launch GTA revenue sits in the "launch year" bar. It makes the launch bar slightly bigger, and the bar is still under a sixth, so the claim holds.
- **"Fell off a cliff" / −81%** (c2x): FY14 1,620 → FY15 303 is −81% by our math; the spoken number is Take-Two's own "$1,315.3 million" decrease (F8). Don't print −81% unless as `OUR MATH`.
- **13 years**: Sept 17 2013 → Nov 19 2026 = 13.2 years; FY2014-FY2026 = 13 fiscal years.
- **69% → 12%** (c6a, c6b): F12 FY2014 68.9% ("approximately") and FY2026 12.4%. "Take-Two got much bigger" = net revenue $2.35B → $6.66B (F12).
