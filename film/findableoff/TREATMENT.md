# Treatment · Can you find your iPhone when it's off?

Factory #41 (`findableoff`), the director's plan. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json` (Ming US_James, seed 7, no music, no AI tag). The engine, gates and sound come from `../rice/` (#40, r2 7.5 / Truth 9.0): `CLIP_RUN`, `LABELS_RUN`, the safe box, the peel and slap patterns, the torn-paper roll, captions keyed to the frame time, the SFX gate from `vo/heard.js`, the loop seam at t = 1/60. The look is this film's own: a dim sofa (tufted cushions) at night, and one big generic paper phone is the thread from frame 0 to the end card.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `rice` (#40, r2 7.5 / Truth 9.0).** One hand-made hero object in motion on frame 0 and back on the end card; Apple's page as a re-created card that prints on its words; one coral stamp; RE-CREATED on every re-created UI. **Don't take:** its bowl, grains, port close-up or kitchen tiles.
- **Learn from `trackeralert` (#38, r2 7.6 / Truth 9.0).** Find My explained with objects, scope in the voice, the maker's real setting names on the re-created screen. **Don't take:** its tracker tag, bag or stranger story (this film is own-phone only, no people).

## Three structures
1. **The phone, the page, the crowd, the switch (chosen).** The power-off slider on frame 0; the phone gets lost in a sofa and goes dark; Apple's guide card says up to 24 hours; the camera pulls back to a room of helper devices that relay a blip to an approximate-location map; one switch kills it all; the re-created Settings screen turns both switches on; back to frame 0.
2. **A 24-hour clock as the spine.** Strong number, but it puts the hero on frame 0 (the answer) and leaves the mechanism abstract.
3. **Map first (where is it?).** Reads as tracking someone; the brief bans exact-location pictures.

## The native move: the beacon ring
The dark phone sends out a cream paper ring every ~1.2 s. In the room the rings reach helper devices, which tick once and light up; one passes a coral blip up to the map card. When the switch flips off at `dip`, the rings freeze and fade and the helpers grey out. On the Settings screen the switches turn on; the end card returns to frame 0's power-off slider.

- **Change 2 at `network`, the crowd.** The camera pulls back: the sofa shrinks to the middle and 14 helper devices (laptops, tablets, phones on tables and in a bag, no people) light up in a wave; "…OVER A BILLION APPLE DEVICES…" · APPLE. At `relay` a ring reaches the laptop and a coral blip hops to a paper map card, landing in a soft circle APPROXIMATE LOCATION · APPLE; BLUETOOTH · ANONYMOUS · ENCRYPTED.
- **C at `dip`.** A big paper switch FIND MY NETWORK flips OFF: rings stop, helpers grey out, the circle empties; ONLY IF FIND MY NETWORK IS ON.
- **D at `path`/`toggle`, the check.** A torn wipe to the re-created Settings screen (RE-CREATED): breadcrumbs SETTINGS › [YOUR NAME] › FIND MY on their words (NOW card), then the Find My iPhone page; both switches flip on on "both switches"; Send Last Location sits untouched.
- **End.** A wipe back to frame 0's phone and title; YES / NO (cream, 112 px) on "on?"; they shrink away and the knob rides into frame 0's pose.

## Rules for this film
- No model names; no "iPhone Findable After Power Off" string; the slider says POWER OFF only.
- "Up to" on every number; 5 hours only with "in power reserve mode".
- The pin sits in a soft circle, never on an exact address; no live tracking line.
- Never show the network working with the switch off; the check screen ends with both switches on.
- Coral only on the UP TO 24 HOURS stamp and the relay blip.
- RE-CREATED on the power-off screen, the search bar, the guide card and the Settings screen.
