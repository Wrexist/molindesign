## Rulings on the director's worries
**(a) The re-created listing at 13.3–15.5 s.** It is accurate. The editions page and PS Store have 0 "chapter", and the item names and quote match. It can't pass as Rockstar's page: there is no Rockstar logo, R mark, font, key art or colour scheme, just a cream card with our own drawings, and a red `RE-CREATED` tag (about 36 px) sits on the header. So there is no branding problem under SAFE-EDIT. Two weaknesses remain. The find bar and `0 MATCHES` claim a dated search but never show the date (pages change after launch patches). And `PS + XBOX PAGES: 0 TOO` is cryptic: "0 what?". Neither blocks posting. Both are in fix 1.

**(b) "Jason's pickup goes when Chapter 1 ends."** This is a minor progression spoiler, not a story spoiler under SAFE-EDIT rule 9. It shows no cutscene, gives no reason, names no event and has nothing to do with the ending. Game Rant and TheGamer publish it as a spoiler-light tip. The whole film's premise (Chapter 1 items lock) already implies a move. Keep it. Optionally add "Minor Chapter 1 spoiler-free tips" to the description. Do not add SPOILERS to the title: it would hurt more than it protects.

**(c) "Rockstar's own page doesn't say that."** It is true of the page the picture shows (the ROCKSTARGAMES.COM editions page, verified 0 matches). It also holds for both console store pages. But the voice says "Rockstar's own page" without scoping it. Search snippets of the unreadable Rockstar support article reportedly talk about bonuses "behind each chapter", so a commenter could answer "Rockstar's support page does". The on-screen scope saves Truth at 8.5, but this is the line most likely to draw a "wrong" comment. Re-voice it as fix 2.
