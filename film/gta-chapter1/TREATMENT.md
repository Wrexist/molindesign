## Three structures
1. **The garage door (chosen).** Jason's safehouse garage at night, cut paper. A roll-up door is the clock: it rolls up on frame 0, drops a notch on the question, another when the pickup "goes", and slams on "Chapter 2". Every item wears a paper claim tag. One beat leaves the garage: the re-created listing, searched for "chapter", 0 matches.
2. Countdown calendar: a tear-off pad from Chapter 1 to 2 with the items pinned to pages. Rejected: a pad is a list, and pages read as real time.
3. Inventory screen: a drawn loadout grid with timers. Rejected: invented game UI is a claim (LESSONS gta-pc r2).

## Native move
**The roll-up door.** Slats roll up and down as rigid paper bands; each notch lands with a metal clang in a pause the voice leaves. The film starts and ends on the door rising, so the loop is one continuous roll.

## Composition (one hero per beat, never the same layout twice)
hook wide garage + headline · items: the bike, then the kayak lift out of the garage to full-width hero size · build: the side-view pickup rolls across the full width · q: wide, calendar pad + door notch · catch: VGC sticky note big over the door, slow push · store: the re-created listing fills the frame · pickup: the front-view pickup lifts out under a dark scrim · do: checklist card left, calendar right, door slam · end: door rising into frame 0.
