## Three structures
1. **Lights out, then the meter.** One dark room: a phone, a workbench power meter (% LESS POWER) and a wall switch. The switch flips and the room goes ink. A black photo glows gray on LCD, and the X-ray flap shows the backlight burning behind closed blinds. A re-created toggle is flipped and the needle only jitters off its peg. The LCD and OLED phones then stand side by side (gray vs pitch black). The OLED bulb grid switches off row by row while the needle creeps. Lights come back up, the meter grows into the hero, and the needle swings into 3–9. The window light falls across the meter and the needle slams into the coral 39–47 band. Frame 0 again, with YES/NO.
2. **Two phones side by side** for the whole film. Clear, but there's no room for a big meter. (Kept as a 2.5 s beat inside structure 1.)
3. **Start with Purdue's numbers**, then explain why. The DM-able test would come after the payoff.

**Chosen: 1.** The viewer's own test drives the first half; the meter is the spine and becomes the hero.

## The native move: lights out
A cut-paper wall switch flips on "lights" and the room drops from warm dim to near-ink in 0.3 s. Props keep cream rim lights. What's left is the phone's black: a gray glow with a halo on LCD, and on OLED nothing but the phone's cream rim. The needle answers every switch:
- It rests on a brass peg 8° left of 0, so at rest it points at no number.
- It only jitters for LCD.
- It creeps toward the 3–9 band as OLED rows switch off.
- It swings into 3–9 indoors.
- On "thirty-nine" it sweeps to about 41, then on the thump it slams to 47, overshoots about 3° and springs back to about 45. The coral band snaps on in the same frame.

## Rules for this film
- Coral only on the meter's 39–47 band and YES/NO. Frame 0's SAVES BATTERY? is a cream sticker on the phone, with ink type.
- Every Purdue number shares the frame with the sticker PURDUE · 2021 / 4 ANDROID OLED PHONES (two lines, 50 px). The bands are labelled INDOORS · AVG 3–9% and FULL SUN · AVG 39–47%. Purdue's own sentence appears as a strip: "…SAVES AN AVERAGE OF 39%-47% BATTERY POWER." · PURDUE, 2021.
- No 0 %, ZERO, ✓ or OK anywhere. The needle never rests on a number.
- RE-CREATED only on the re-created dark-mode toggle. Quote strips are the sources' own sentences, credited, with "…" at each cut.
- Generic phones (round punch-hole, no logo). No clip art: the sun is a window-light wedge, the hand is cut paper, and the bulbs are irregular paper discs with grain.
- Props stay whole inside x 92–928, y 302–1428. Only the wall, desk and wedge light bleed.
