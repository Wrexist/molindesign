# “One second”: how a watch counts time (treatment)

A 38.5‑second vertical explainer (1080 × 1920, 30 fps) for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, written entirely in code like `film/` (Held). Same contract, same renderer (`node render.mjs video --film escapement`).

## Benchmark

- **Macro watch‑movement videos** (watchmaker channels filming a balance wheel under a loupe): learn the hypnotic real‑speed beat and the reveal of the jewels. Don't take: footage, branding, loupe vignettes.
- **Clean motion‑graphics explainers** (one claim per caption, numbered steps): learn one read at a time and the number on each step. Don't take: their characters, music or colours.

## Three structures considered

1. **Glossary**: name each part as it appears. Rejected: a list, nothing to wonder about.
2. **History of timekeeping** (sundial → pendulum → quartz). Rejected: too much for 40 s, and the object is the interesting thing.
3. **Take it apart, put it back (chosen).** Hook on a ticking dial with no battery. Lift the dial and the regulating organ away, show what the spring does alone (dumps its power in a second), then put back the escapement in slow motion and the balance wheel, and speed back up to real time as the watch ticks again. The turn: a second is not measured, it is *rationed*.

## Reads, in order

| # | Time | Shot | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–4.7 | Macro on the small seconds ticking at real speed (8 steps a second), pulling back to the whole dial by 2.3 s. | “No battery. So what counts the seconds?” |
| 2 | 4.7–6.2 | The dial lifts toward camera, the fork and balance lift with it. | We are going inside. |
| 3 | 6.0–9.4 | Push in on the barrel, cut away to show the coiled mainspring. | “1. A wound spring.” |
| 4 | 9.4–12.0 | Release: the frame punches in, the spring unwinds, every wheel spins out in a blur. | “Let go: gone in a second.” |
| 5 | 12.0–19.4 | The fork and balance drop back in; the camera locks; time slows to 0.06×; the fork rocks and the escape wheel slips one tooth at a time. tick / tock land on each lock. | “2. A fork lets it move one tooth at a time.” |
| 6 | 19.4–26.3 | Pull back to the balance wheel and its breathing hairspring at 0.25× (the beats fall on 120 BPM). | “3. A balance wheel sets the beat. 4 round trips a second. Always.” |
| 7 | 26.3–33.0 | Speed ramps to 1×, the whole movement runs, 28 800 beats an hour; eight dots pulse each second. | “Spring pushes. Balance counts.” |
| 8 | 33.0–38.5 | The dial drops back on with a click; the seconds tick on. Ends where it began, so it loops. | “That's a second. A spring and a wobble.” |

**Native move:** the time ramp. Speed is the teaching tool: the same mechanism at 0.06×, 0.25× and 1×, and the sound slows with it, so each beat's three clicks (unlock, impulse, drop) become audible one by one in slow motion.

**Signature shot:** the escapement at 0.06×, rubies locking teeth with tick / tock.

## Look

Paper `#eeeae2`, rhodium‑grey parts with ink outlines and soft shadows, perlage on the plate. One accent, ruby `#c8102e`, reserved for the jewels and one key word per caption. Archivo 800 for captions, Geist Mono for labels and readouts. Captions sit in the upper third, labels stay left of the right‑hand button column and above the bottom caption band (platform safe zones).

## Sound

Hook: real‑speed ticking only (ASMR open). Spring section: soft pluck in D, a whirr and ratchet on the release, then a cut to a low‑passed pad in slow motion where the clicks are pitched down. At 0.25× the beats land on quarter notes at 120 BPM and a bell answers them; at 1× the ticks become sixteenths and the groove arrives. The dial lands with a thud, then only ticks, so the end runs into the start.
