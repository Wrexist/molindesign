## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **4.5–7.0 s: replace the drawn sunset slab with the real race shot (Truth + Craft + Retention).** Run the race clip full-bleed (1080 wide, crop to 9:16 around the car, about 1222.2–1224.6 s, so it isn't reused from the hook or count). Keep `SUNSET` as a sticker on the real sky. Move the quote onto the source card itself, written `"…CAPTURED ENTIRELY FROM IN-GAME FOOTAGE…"` with both ellipses, ≥ 36 px, under an `AN EXTENDED LOOK · ROCKSTAR` credit. That gets rid of the clip-art and the quote on our own drawing, and fills the 5 s drop-off with real motion. Expected: Truth +1, Craft +0.5.
2. **13.15–16.5 s and the strip: scope the montage count, or drop it (Truth).** Either say "one of six shots in a row of night shots" and label the strip `20:14 → 20:31 · OUR CUT` with the later skyline shots drawn as greyed segments past 20:31, or cut the "six / seventeen" line and make the beat "And the same trailer shows where the night goes", with no count. Rewrite the description too: "the shot opens at 6 of 6 with its timer at 00:04.79" (not "it starts"), and "a run of night shots" in place of "17-second night montage".
3. **0.0–2.0 s: make the stake mean something (Hook + Share), the wow fix.** Make the real race fill the frame (bleed it full-frame behind the HUD zoom, at least 1080x1100, not an 810 px card). Turn the race into a felt stake: a bold 4-car leaderboard stack that physically reorders under the car on each place (6→5→4→3→2), with each overtaken car's slot swinging out of the stack, and `4 CARS · 3.47 S` as the single hero stamp at 1.8 s. Cut frame 0 down to headline + chip + the ringed `6 / 6`: hide PROGRESS, the timer and the `HUD·ZOOM×4.6` tag until 0.6 s, so the frame has at most 5 readable words. That gives the viewer a DM-able moment (a leaderboard flipping four times in under four seconds) instead of a static number.
4. **7.25–9.75 s: give each place its own hit (Voice/sound).** Re-voice the count with written pauses of 0.45 s, as "Six of six. … Five. … Four. … Three. … Two." (about 3.6 s, steal the time from the 13–16.5 grid beat). Put a 1–3 kHz beep in each gap at about −18 dB RMS, ≥3 dB under the voice, and make the place digit flip on the beep frame. Also put a gap of ≥0.45 s after "second" (0.12–1.9 s) so the `2ND` sticker gets a real thump.
5. **16.65–20.8 s: kill the empty interstitials and the clashing counter (Clarity + Retention).** Drop the full-frame `3 / 6`, `5 / 6` and `6 / 6` boxes on bare ink (17.2–17.6, 18.6–18.9, 19.6–20.8 s). Cut shot to shot under the checkered sweep, with the next shot drawn in full under its first frame. Give the montage counter a different look from the race-position box (e.g. `SHOT 3 OF 6` in a plain white chip, no checker), so sound-off viewers never read a tower as "position 1".
6. **13.15–16.5 s: the grid is too small (Craft + Retention).** Show the six tiles at about 380 px for at most 1.0 s, then push into the race tile up to 1.6× (about 600 px wide) on "seventeen". Never hold the full 3x2 for 3.3 s. Remove the 0.3 s of empty dashed slots at 13.0 s by landing the tiles on the sweep's first frame.
7. **All footage scenes: bleed backgrounds full frame (Craft, LESSONS repeat).** Behind each card, use the same clip blurred (radius ≥ 30 px, dimmed to 35 %) across all of 1080x1920 instead of flat navy ink, so the frame stops reading as a small window. Keep shapes and text inside x 90–930 and above y 1450.
8. **22.85–26.95 s: make the last line loop (Loop).** End on "…pool or dance floor? Or a race you start dead last?", so the final words lead into the frame-0 "Last to second". Keep the three equal buttons, and add `RACE` back as the fourth word only if it stays unhighlighted.
9. **Mix: lift the transition hits (Voice/sound).** The wipes at 4.4, 9.6, 16.45 and 18.5 s measure −21 to −34 dB RMS. Raise them to about −18 dB RMS with a 1–3 kHz layer (LESSONS gta-lucia two-lock note: lower `duck.smr_db` rather than one hit's LEVEL), then re-check each one ≥3 dB under the neighbouring words on the final mix.
