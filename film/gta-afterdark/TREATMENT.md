# Treatment: GTA 6. Last to second.

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from — gta-fridge (Delta, jury r2 7.2 / Truth 9.0):** Rockstar's own UI zoomed at source pixels as the proof (the ×3 loupe), a ring on each thing the voice names, a frame-0 that holds the source frame still and returns at the seam. **Don't take:** the berry-ink house template with cream tape captions (juries called it a template twice), blank full-frame door wipes, the hard card over flat ink with an empty lower third.
- **Learn from — "N details you missed" / "Trailer vs Gameplay" Shorts (VIDEO-PLAN §1):** one detail read off the real screen beats a list. **Don't take:** uncommented montage runs with music. Here the montage is counted, not played: every shot on screen is the thing the line is about.

## Three structures
1. **Montage tour in order** (tower, selfie, pool, race, dance floor, balcony): honest, but a list; frame 0 would be a purple building. Fails the frame-0 rule and LESSONS "no lists".
2. **Race only** (the HUD read 6 → 2): one stake, but 4 s of footage can't carry 25 s without stretching or repeating.
3. **Race HUD hook, then zoom out to the night run** — **chosen.** Frame 0 is the conflict in 5 words (`GTA 6` + `LAST → ?` over the real race full frame, the HUD zoomed at `6 / 6`, PROGRESS and timer masked until 0.6 s); by 1.75 s the HUD reads `2 / 6`, the board has swapped four times and `3.47 S · 4 PLACES` stamps in. Then the timer, the source card on the real shot, the count with a beep per place, progress, the run of night shots as a timeline strip, three full-frame shots, the balcony, and a vote whose last words ("…the race in last place?") loop into "Last to second." (r2 after jury r1.)

## The native move
**The leaderboard swap (r2).** Six fixed place cells in the race HUD's checkered style (pink edge bar, sampled #f67bb2); the cars' rows move. On each HUD place change our row (`YOU`, hot pink) climbs one slot and the passed car swings out of the stack and drops in below, on the beep. The checkered N / 6 box is only ever a race position now; shots carry plain white timecode chips (`20:14`, `20:18`, `20:27`, `20:29`). Scene changes: a checkered-flag band sweeping left to right; inside the tour, hard cuts.

## Look
Night navy-violet ink with a faint HUD checker. Footage bleeds full frame (the race in the hook and count, the tour shots); the balcony card sits over the same clip blurred 30 px at 38 % brightness; the race beat, the run beat and the end run over dimmed full-frame plates. Captions sit in a dark HUD box with a pink edge bar, white condensed type, the stressed word in hot pink. Our own drawings: the HUD boxes, the leaderboard, the timer readout, the source card with the Extended Look scrubber, the progress track, the night-run strip (eight shots to scale, 20:14–20:34, the race pink), the vote buttons (waves, mirror ball, checkered flag). Dropped in r2: the drawn sunset slab, the 3x2 grid, the lone N / 6 interstitials.
