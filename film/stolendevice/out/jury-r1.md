## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **Sound, 22–36 s (repeat).** All 10 hits measure −21.3 dB. Lower `duck.smr_db` (58 → about 50) or raise the hit gains equally until the max 50 ms RMS is −18.5 to −19.5 dB, and re-measure each hit so it stays ≥ 3 dB under the voice median (−16.7). Move the stamp thump from 23.99 s to the stamp's ink frame (~22.25 s): either ink the stamp in the 1.1 s pause after "another scan" (~23.7 s) and slam it there, or keep the ink on "hour" and drop the hit into the closest gap. Add two hits after 30 s: a switch click at the Settings ON (~33.4 s, in the 0.4 s pause after `check`) and a soft slap on YES/NO (~35.3 s). The payoff currently plays in silence.
2. **Truth, 13.6–16.2 s.** "But not with Stolen Device Protection on." is absolute until `away`. Print the chip **AWAY FROM FAMILIAR LOCATIONS · APPLE** (≥ 40 px) under the switch on the word "on" (~15.8 s), not at 16.5 s with the map. Or change the line to "But not away from home, with Stolen Device Protection on." (+4 words; check the 38 s cap). This also fills the empty y 1000–1430 under the switch.
3. **Truth/clarity, 33.3–35.2 s.** MIGHT BE ON BY DEFAULT is about 22 px inside the settings row. Lift it out as a standalone chip **MIGHT BE ON BY DEFAULT · APPLE** at ≥ 44 px caps, at y 1300–1420 under the Settings phone, from "Passcode" (~32.6 s) to the wipe. Add **MUST BE ON BEFORE IT'S STOLEN · APPLE** (≥ 40 px) on "before the theft" (~34.2 s), as BRIEF §3b.7 asks.
4. **Loop, 35.2–36.17 s.** The frame-0 end card holds ~0.7 s and is only clean for ~0.17 s. Hold PASSCODE SEEN + YES/NO for ≥ 0.8 s after "yours" ends, then cut to frame 0. Move YES/NO to y ~1480→1400 (centre x 330 / 750) so YES no longer covers the coffee cup. Match the end's filled dots to frame 0's.
5. **Wow, 18.0–19.0 s (the native move).** The key bouncing off the lock, the film's whole argument, is a small upside-down key at y ~1360 that most viewers won't notice. Make it the beat: a 360 px PASSCODE key drives into the SAVED PASSWORDS keyhole, the tumbler refuses (2-frame shake, a dull clack at −19 dB), the key rebounds 120 px, and the keyhole morphs into the face-frame/fingerprint scan glyph at 200 px. That is the contrast with 9.5 s, where the same key turned. It gives the film a second shareable shot that mirrors the first.
6. **Poster / DM frame.** "Away from home, it takes an hour." reads as "the thief just waits". Change it to **"A thief knows your passcode? Away from home, they still need your face."** Or keep the hour: **"…they need your face, an hour, then your face again."** Put the FACE ID OR TOUCH ID chip on the poster (it is hidden under the stamp now).
7. **Craft, 18.6–21.5 s.** FACE ID OR TOUCH ID and NO PASSCODE FALLBACK sit over the dial and the second keyhole. Stack them under the lock (y ~1290 and ~1350, centred, x 160–920), and take the map card off before the lock grows (no 0.3 s blur smear at 19.6 s).
8. **Retention, 25.2–28.0 s.** The clock, laptop and LOST phone are each under 250 px and almost static. Make the laptop 520 px wide at y 900–1250 with a pin that drops on "mark", and slam the LOST tag (cream, 150 px) onto a 420 px phone on "lost" with a hit at the line end (+0.3 s). Drop the small clock; the +1 HOUR card already says it.
9. **Craft, 4.4–7.4 s.** The `ask` caption wraps to three lines with "out?" orphaned, 10 px above the scope plate. Force the break "Can a thief with your passcode / lock you out?" (2 lines) and keep ≥ 24 px between the caption and the plate.
10. **Craft/truth, 28.3–30.2 s.** Take RE-CREATED off the Apple quote card (it reads as "quote re-created") and put it on the phone/pay scene. Under fix 1, also make the switch-only stage at 13.3–14.4 s carry the version card from the first frame, so the 0.9 s pause isn't a single prop on flat green.
