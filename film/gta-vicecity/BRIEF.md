## 1. Topic and one hard idea
**GTA 6's Vice City first appeared in 1997: four Vice Cities on one timeline (1997, 2002, 2006, 2026) make it 29 years old by release year.** The film walks the timeline card by card and ends on a one-word vote: which Vice City was best.

Truth rule for this film: "29 years" is **release year to release year** (1997 to 2026). The exact 1997 release day is disputed (21 Oct vs 28 Nov 1997, see fact 2), so the film never claims a birthday, an exact age on a date, or "turns 29 on…".

Main search phrase: **GTA 6 Vice City** (spoken in line 2 at about 3.5 s, "GTA 6" spoken in line 1 at 0.2 s). Secondary: "GTA Vice City 1986", "original GTA 1997".
