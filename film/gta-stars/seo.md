## Description
GTA's crooked cop? Samuel L. Jackson.

Samuel L. Jackson voiced Officer Frank Tenpenny, the corrupt Los Santos cop in GTA San Andreas (2004). Two years earlier, Vice City starred Ray Liotta as Tommy Vercetti, and Burt Reynolds was in it too.

Reynolds argued with Rockstar's writer Dan Houser over a scene. It ended with Reynolds yelling, "Get the limey out of here." (Houser is from London; "limey" is old slang for a Brit.)

In 2018, Houser told Vulture that Rockstar doesn't "bring in name actors anymore because of their egos and, most important of all, because we believe we get a better sense of immersion using talented actors whose voices you don't recognize." Those are his words from 2018, about Rockstar at the time.
