# Treatment: "GTA's Crooked Cop Was Samuel L. Jackson" (Delta, gta-stars)

One hard idea: **GTA once hired Hollywood, and its own writer said in 2018 that Rockstar stopped bringing in name actors: partly egos, most of all because voices you don't recognize pull you in.** One hero object: **the paper dressing-room door with a gold star** (our own generic star, no Walk of Fame design: no sidewalk, no brass ring, no emblem). One hero sound: the door slam after "Get the limey out of here."

## Three structures
1. **Backstage corridor (chosen).** A hand-cut paper backstage hallway bleeds the full frame; the camera trucks right along it from door to door. Each Hollywood name gets a dressing-room door with a gold paper star and a name card (type only, no faces). Reynolds' door is the recording booth: it slams on "limey". After Houser's rule, the stars come off the doors and the name cards flip blank; the corridor ends at two doors that carry only Rockstar's character names, JASON and LUCIA. The camera walks on into the frame-0 door for the loop.
2. Sidewalk of star tiles (the brief's first idea). Rejected: a star pressed into a sidewalk is the Walk of Fame's trade dress (BRIEF §6.2).
3. A casting board with pinned name cards that get unpinned. Rejected: a flat board is the dashboard/infographic look that held five Delta films, and the slam has nothing to do.

## Native move
The corridor truck. The camera's speed tells the story: a slow glide on the hook, a quick walk between stars (why, liotta, burt), a held push into the booth door during the fight, a pull-back at "voices you don't recognize" that shows the three doors at once going blank, a walk to JASON and LUCIA, then on to the frame-0 door. The wallpaper and floor repeat with a period that divides the corridor length, so the last frame is frame 0.

## Rules this cut is built to pass
- Frame 0: caption tape `GTA'S CROOKED COP?` (3 words) + the door's name card `SAMUEL L. JACKSON` (3) = 6, GTA first. The door fills ~65 % of the frame, cream and gold against the ink wall; the cop cap on the hook is already swinging and the camera already gliding (≥ 300 px in the first second).
- No likeness: stars are name cards; characters are objects only (cop cap and generic star badge, pastel jacket on a hanger, a script). No Rockstar logo, art, footage or music.
- Houser's 2018 words carry `DAN HOUSER · VULTURE · 2018` on the same piece; the `2018` card and the NO MORE NAME ACTORS stamp leave before `gta6`. The GTA 6 pill never sits on a star door, a Houser card or the quote.
- JASON and LUCIA doors: character names only, plain stars, no actor name, no `?`, no UNKNOWN.
- The limey quote is printed exactly with its teller: `"GET THE LIMEY OUT OF HERE."` · `REYNOLDS, AS TOLD BY DAN HOUSER · VULTURE 2018`.
- Every quote strip peels off under the move that leaves its beat. No highlight on quoted words.
- YES / NO end tags identical.
