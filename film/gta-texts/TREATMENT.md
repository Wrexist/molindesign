# GTA 6: Lucia texts Jason (treatment)

Delta, "It has X, shown" Short (VIDEO-PLAN format 5), launch week. Ming US_James seed 7, no music, official Rockstar clips plus our own drawn phone.

## Benchmarks
| Film | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| "GTA 6 Has Actual Jail Time Now CONFIRMED" (551k) | One feature, named in the title, shown in the first second. | "CONFIRMED" with no source on screen: ours tags Rockstar's video and the timestamp. |
| gta-trunkstream (Delta, previous film) | The in-game overlay re-drawn big and tagged RE-DRAWN; footage between drawn beats so no stretch runs past 5 s. | Its cut-paper street and stream overlay. New set: night glass and a party-light sweep. |

## Three structures
1. **The thread (chosen).** Our phone is the hero object; her two texts land in it on the words that read them. Footage beats (gunmen, Lucia typing, the gunman close) open from a slit between phone beats.
2. Split screen Jason / Lucia the whole way. Rejected: two half-height 16:9 plates are too small on a phone, and it would run footage unbroken past 5 s.
3. A countdown of "things her text says". Rejected: a list format for one idea.

## Native move
The footage plate opens from a horizontal slit and closes into one; the phone rises from below and drops away.

## Reads (what the viewer must read, and for how long)
