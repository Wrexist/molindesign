## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **0.0–2.0 s, frame 0 (Hook).** "GUYS EVERYWHERE" alone doesn't say what the video is about, and it isn't a question or number. Change the title to 4–5 words that state the feature: `LUCIA TEXTS JASON` (white, 120 px) over `MID-HEIST?` or, better because it is true, `GUNMEN IN THE ROOM` (teal). Keep the in-game bubble ringed at frame 0, not at 0.45 s. Scale the footage band to at least 1.6× (crop to about 1200 px wide around Jason and the bubble, full height about 1000 px) so the real bubble reads at 60 px or more. Move our re-drawn bubble in at 0.6 s at 72 px type. Frame 0 then holds the topic, the real text and a stake.
2. **6.6–8.7, 12.7–15.1, 18.6–20.6 s: footage beats at 600 px tall with y 1000–1430 empty (LESSONS repeat, full Craft point).** Crop each foreground clip to 4:5 or 1:1 (1080x1080 or larger, centered on the ringed object: the masked face, her phone, the gunman) so it fills y about 280–1400. Or keep 16:9 and put the consequence object under it: the RIGHT THERE beat gets a distance chip `~2 STEPS` only if you can measure it, otherwise the `10+ GUYS / AUTO WEAPONS / EXPLOSIVES` stack enlarged to 48 px at y 1150–1400. This is also the wow fix: a full-height, ringed shot of the gunman walking past her glowing bubble at 18.6 s is the screenshot moment the film lacks.
3. **20.9–24.6 s, static end card (Retention, Loop).** 3.7 s with YDIF 0.62. Cut it to 2.0 s. Show the dilemma instead of a still phone: a split of the gunman's ring (top) and her bubble (bottom), a SEND button pulse at 0.5 s intervals, a thumb hovering, a ding when the cut back to Jason lands. Change the line to one that fits what happened: "Would you have sent it? Because again," and lift the read (Ming speed 1.1 on that line).
4. **9.5–12.7 s, the lock screen `11:47 · JASON'S PHONE` (Truth).** The clock is the video timestamp passed off as in-game time, and the source shows Jason holding a radio, not a phone. Drop the clock and the "JASON'S PHONE" label. Show the bubble alone, floating over the jplate exactly as the game draws it (avatar, "Lucia", the text), tagged `RE-DRAWN · 23:47`. Truth goes to 9.
5. **4.2–6.5 s, the `YOU'RE INVITED · GRAND OPENING · NEW HQ · TONIGHT` card (Truth, Craft).** It is a made-up in-game artifact, and its tumble-out with motion blur at 6.6 s looks cheap. Replace it with a label chip on the real plate: `OPENING PARTY · NEW HQ` (from F2) plus a ring on the host on the stairs. Raise the plate dim from about 60 % to 40 % so the party actually shows. Cut the card instead of flinging it.
6. **15.2–18.5 s, the unreadable 5-line message on the intel phone (Clarity).** At about 22 px nobody reads "After Czr, he's in saferoom..." in 3 s. Enlarge the phone 1.4× and type only the three hit words at 44 px or more as they are said (`10+ guys` / `auto weps` / `explosives`). Keep the full original text as one dimmed line above for honesty. Stamps land with a hit SFX on each word.
7. **18.2–18.6 s and 20.2–20.8 s: empty stages under the slit wipes (LESSONS repeat).** Build the next scene under the wipe's first frame: the gunman clip already open behind the slit at 18.2 s, and the end-card phone already drawn under the closing slit at 20.2 s. Check every wipe frame on the render.
8. **Whole film: no "so what" (Clarity, Shareability).** Add one 4–6 word line or chip by about 12 s saying why this is notable, sourced only to what is shown, for example `TEXTS SHOW UP MID-SCENE` (on screen) with "no menu, it just pops up" in the voice. Don't claim how the player uses it.
9. **0.0–25.4 s: dark footage.** The source is dark and plates are dimmed on top. Lift foreground clips +0.15 gamma (not the plates), so Jason's face and the bubble read on a phone at 30 % brightness.
