# Treatment: Four locks, one bag (gta-lootrun)

**Benchmarks.** Learn from `film/gta-leaks/` (clip inside a card, an annotation on every clip, own drawing for the context) and from escape-room explainers that track progress with a visible lock counter. Don't take: the leaks film's centred card on every beat with nothing carrying over; the "?" towers and paper sets other Delta films already used.

**Three structures.** (a) Countdown 4 → 1 of locks. (b) Chain: one object (the bag) that is locked four times and opens one lock per line, riding a rail under each beat. (c) Before/after: empty-handed vs bag. Chosen: **(b)**, because the object carries the story across every beat and gives the loop: the bag comes back up and re-locks into frame 0.

**Native move.** A padlock's shackle popping open on the stressed word, with an OPEN stamp, four times; at the end the chain drops off the bag.
