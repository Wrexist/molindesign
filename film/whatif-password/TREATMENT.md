# Treatment · What if your password were 20 letters long

Factory #23 (`whatif-password`), director's plan before the scenes. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json`.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `energy` (#22):** one hand-made hero prop, the stamp lands in a written silence, the end card is frame 0 at full opacity with YES / NO. **Don't take:** its empty lower third (2.5–9.5 s) and a hero stamp that shared a frame with the next source.
- **Learn from the parked `password` (#11):** the ladder of lengths is the right picture. **Don't take:** a field whose letter count disagreed with its stamp for 2.5 s, six coral stamps, and a quote scoped wider than its source.

## Three structures
1. **Ladder walk** (8 → 9 → 12 → 18 → 20 on one field, then the dip, then the advice). One mechanism, one prop, escalating numbers: the what-if shape.
2. **Two fields side by side** (8 vs 20) from frame 0. Faster to the payoff, but frame 0 would show the answer.
3. **Cold open on INSTANTLY**, then rewind. Strong dip, but the hook then explains a second mechanism first.

**Chosen: 1.** Frame 0 asks the viewer's own length (HOW LONG IS YOURS? over 8 masked dots), the field grows to 20, the hero is the number at 20, and the dip flips the same field to a reused, real-word password.

## The native move
**The typed tile drop.** One paper tile per letter falls into its cell with a knock. The tag above carries the count and the crack time on one piece of paper; **the count and the stamp change on the same frame, or both are blank** while tiles are typing.
