## Three structures
1. **The gate on the data lane (chosen).** The cable has two lanes: amber power dashes that always flow, and a cream data lane that stops at a paper gate at the phone end. The fear (the FBI's words, a paper bug on the port) never gets past the gate; the twist is an empty CASES slot; the lab is a dashed box where the bug does get through; the phone's prompt is the gate; the viewer's own "Don't Trust" slams it.
2. **The courtroom.** The kiosk in the dock, the FBI post as the charge, the empty evidence bag. Clear, but it turns a hedged "probably" into a verdict, which §3b forbids.
3. **The map of an airport.** Ports lit across a terminal map, none flagged. Pretty, but nothing for the viewer to do and no "check yours" moment.

## The native move: the gate
A cream paper gate (two posts and a barred arm) sits on the cable's data lane at the phone end. It is shut from frame 0 while the power dashes flow past it. At `asks` the camera goes inside the cable: the gate is huge, a sign board rises on its posts and becomes the prompt's two faces at `os` (iPhone, then Android). At `never` a paper finger hovers over Trust, slides to Don't Trust, presses; the arm slams down (knock), the data packets bounce off it, the power dashes keep flowing (CHARGING STILL WORKS), and the camera punches 1.00 → 1.06 on the slam. That is the wow beat, in the open, with no wipe near it.
