## YouTube Short

**Title:**
Why Walmart pulled GTA San Andreas off its shelves

**Description:**
In July 2005 the ratings board (ESRB) took away GTA San Andreas's M rating and made it AO, Adults Only. A hidden adult scene had been left locked on the disc, and a fan-made mod unlocked it. Walmart, Target and Best Buy pulled every copy from their shelves. Rockstar cut the scene, and the new version got its M rating back. GTA 6 is out November 19, 2026. Should hidden content change a game's rating?
