# Treatment: How to tell if a video is AI

Factory #21 · `detect` · voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), every beat keyed to a word with `at(id, word)`. Engine copied from `film/vision/` (torn-edge wipes, word-keyed captions, the SFX gate, the safe-box clip), scenes new.

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from:** noise (1,319 views: a task on frame 0, "Find the cat") and incognito (7.5, Truth 9.0: the sources turn the belief over, one mechanism hook to end, a check the viewer can do). Here frame 0 hands the viewer the test (REAL OR AI? on a playing clip with a draining ring) and the whole film is one move: the loupe leaves the pixels for the name.
- **Don't take:** vision (7.2 × 3: one small print on a dark stage; here the paper phone is 820 px tall for the whole film and 780 px in the hero), wifi/gps (empty stage under a wipe: every incoming scene is drawn in full above the torn edge), password r2 (stamps everywhere: one coral stamp, everything else cream), incognito r1 (a label on a bracket over a group: every label sits next to the one thing it names, and the clean redraw carries ALSO MADE UP in big type for its whole line).

## Three structures
1. **One phone, one loupe (chosen).** The paper phone plays a cut-paper street clip (a waving paper hand, a shop sign, a lamp post). The loupe hunts the pixels, circles three drawn tells, the clip flips to a clean redraw that is still made up, a torn edge opens the 2024 study card and a coin toss, a second tear brings the phone back, and the loupe leaves the picture for the poster's name: coral WHO POSTED IT? and the hold. Then the check, on the same phone: tap the name, the uploads list, the first upload; label chips; the chips pulled off and the METADATA corner torn away. The end card is frame 0 sliding back.
2. **Real vs fake side by side** (two clips, guess which). A stronger quiz, but it puts the answer (the pixels) at the centre, the opposite of the film's point, and a two-panel frame halves the hero.
3. **The study first** (open on the coin toss). A clean shock, but frame 0 would be a card, not a test, and "your feed" would land after 10 s.

## Native move
**The loupe that leaves the picture.** A brass paper loupe magnifies the clip 2× inside its lens (the clip is drawn a second time, scaled, inside the lens circle). It hunts like the viewer does, circles the tells, finds nothing on the clean redraw, shows blocky pixels on "pixels", lifts on "stop", and on "Check who posted it first" slides off the picture onto **@ ? ? ?**, which it magnifies. The argument is that one move and it reads with the sound off.

## Motion personality
Paper on a desk: springs at ζ ≥ 0.85, slides along one axis, two torn edges falling top to bottom, a paper flip for the redraw, no zooms out of the hero, nothing bounces. One heavy sound (the stamp thump); the rest are paper (ticks, a coin ping, a tap).

## Sound
No music. Paper hits placed only in pauses the gate passes (`vo/heard.js`, base.en + small.en on the mix and the voice-only energy): THE stamp thump in the silence after "first." (body 250 → 180 Hz with a 1.6–2.5 kHz paper slap), a coin ping in the 0.6 s silence after "half the time.", soft loupe ticks on the tells, frame 0's play click, a soft rip on the second torn wipe and the FIRST UPLOAD tick, each only where the gate passes it (the ALSO MADE UP slap, the first wipe's rip and the hero-exit rip have no gap and are left out). Room tone about −60 dB so the seam is never digital zero.
