# Treatment · Airplane mode isn't only for planes

Factory #28 (`airplanemode`), director's plan before the scenes. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json`. Engine, gates and sound: `../coldbattery/` (the latest passing film); the look is its own.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `coldbattery` (#27, fastest start since incognito):** a phone-myth with a thing the viewer owns on frame 0, the maker's own words as proof, one hero stamp in a written silence, every scene built in full under the transition, the end card is frame 0 at full opacity. **Don't take:** its hero device (a coral stamp over a drawn phone, third film in a row) and its frost-peel transition. Here the hero is a re-created FCC rule page, the big prop a cut-paper airliner, and scenes change under a bank of paper clouds.
- **Learn from `incognito` (#2 by views, Truth 9.0):** the hook is the source's own idea ("isn't only for planes" = AC 91.21-1D's "ground-based cellular stations"), so hook, proof page and the end vote all explain one mechanism. **Don't take:** a talking-head-free film that only shows documents; here the mechanism (one phone up high, many towers) is drawn, not quoted.

## Three structures
1. **Two agencies, two face-down cards.** Frame 0: a huge airplane-mode tile, signal arcs pulsing out of it, NOT ONLY FOR PLANES. "Who makes you turn it on?" lays two face-down cards. The FAA card flips over the cut-paper airliner (2013: most airliners tolerate phones; low-visibility exception; crew may ask). The cloud bank wipes back to 1991 and the FCC card flips over a paper map of towers (the ring falls wide and lights them all). OCT 2026: a drone rises into the same ring. The hero: the FCC's own rule page with the new drone paragraph inked in, STILL BANNED next to (a). Frame 0 again, YES / NO.
2. **One flight, gate to landing**, a beat per phase (boarding, cruise, approach in fog). Concrete, but the FCC half (towers on the ground, drones) has no phase of its own and the twist would land late.
3. **Cold open on the drone vote.** The DM beat first ("drones can, you can't"), but it is a riddle before the band is explained (BRIEF §2 rejected it as the opener).

**Chosen: 1.** The face-down cards turn "who" into a sound-off picture and give the film a midpoint flip.

## The native move: the signal ring
Cream paper arcs (flat, opaque, a small drop shadow; never a glow or a shockwave) step out of whatever is transmitting. On frame 0 they pulse out of the airplane-mode tile; on "planes." the tile toggles on and the arcs fold back into it with a click. On the tower map the ring from a phone on the ground reaches one tower; the same ring dropped from the plane falls wide and lights a whole field of towers, each lighting as the ring reaches it, with a tick per tower. The drone's ring lights them too (DRONES: OK). On the hero the passenger phone's ring is cut by the rule.

## Transitions
A cut-paper cloud bank (opaque, scalloped, a soft shadow) sweeps across the safe box; the old scene leaves on its leading side, the next is already built behind it. Right → left going forward; **left → right for the 1991 flashback**, so the direction carries the time jump with the sound off. Time cards: 2013 (from the right), 1991 (from the left), OCT 2026 (from the right), TODAY on the end.

## Rules for this film
- Coral only on STILL BANNED and on YES / NO. Every other chip, card, stamp and tick is cream or ink.
- Quotes word for word with "…" at every cut, each with its source and date under it: Huerta (FAA, Oct 31, 2013), 47 CFR 22.925, FCC 26-72 (Oct 2026). The low-visibility hedge quoted in ≥ 48 px. Every re-created page carries RE-CREATED from its first frame.
- "most" and 2013 on the tolerate claim; "FAA PANEL" (the committee), never "the FAA proved". 1991 is the rule (never "since 1984"). The drone change is **VOTED**, never "in effect". "Cell signal still banned", never "your phone is banned": WI-FI: IF YOUR AIRLINE OFFERS IT and BLUETOOTH ACCESSORIES: OK sit under the hero page with FAA · 2013 on them.
- Nothing says or implies phones crash planes, or that they are perfectly safe.
- Generic phone, plane, drone, tower and control tile, drawn by hand: no airline livery, no logos, no Apple or Android UI, no agency seals.
- Lower band y 1000–1430 filled every second (tile, the face-down cards, the airliner, runway, the map, the page).
