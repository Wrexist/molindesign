**Title:** **How AI Pulls a Cat Out of Static 🤔** (7 words, Title Case, "How…", one emoji, no colon or channel name, per the LESSONS viral study). The playlist is "How AI works".

**Description:**

> How do AI image generators work? Find the cat in this static. You can't, because there's no cat in it.
>
> Image generators like Stable Diffusion are trained by adding static (random noise) to real pictures and learning to guess exactly which noise was added. To make a new picture, they run that backwards: start from pure noise and wipe a little away, often about 50 steps (Stable Diffusion's default), while your text prompt steers every step. Same starting noise, a different word, and you get a different picture. The cat was never hiding. It was painted in.
>
> Fine print: Stable Diffusion's starting noise is a small compressed grid that's decoded into pixels at the end, shown here as static. "460,000,000+ training looks" is 225,000 training steps x a batch of 2,048 in Stable Diffusion v1-4's last training round alone (our arithmetic from the model card). Not every AI image tool works this way: ChatGPT's image generator is reported to use a different method. The cat and dog are hand-drawn stand-ins, not real model output.
>
> Sources:
> - Ho, Jain & Abbeel, "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (2020): https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239
> - Rombach et al., "High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models" (Stable Diffusion, 2022): https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752
> - Hugging Face diffusers, Stable Diffusion text-to-image (50 steps default; reusing the same noise with a different prompt): https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/api/pipelines/stable_diffusion/text2img
> - Stable Diffusion v1-4 model card (training steps and batch size): https://huggingface.co/CompVis/stable-diffusion-v1-4
> - Sohl-Dickstein et al., "Deep Unsupervised Learning using Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics" (2015): https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03585
