## Three structures
1. **The peephole check.** The browser is a front door seen from inside; every site is a visitor who shows a dated ID card (CERTIFICATE · shop.example · VALID AUG 2026 – FEB 2027) under a string tied to your wall calendar. Right clock: the marker lands inside the window, the chain slides off. Wrong clock: the calendar flips back to APR, the same card fails, the warning pops out of the peephole. Study plate (1 IN 3). Wi-Fi clerk's clipboard in front of the card. Lights drop: a stranger with a pasted-over card. The two checks on their own props. Hero: YOUR BANK door, coral chain snaps taut. Frame 0 again, YES / NO.
2. **A browser's checklist** ticked line by line (clock, Wi-Fi, site). Clear, but a checklist is a list, and ticks are banned (no ✓ chips).
3. **Start at the airport** (a traveller on hotel Wi-Fi). Timely, but the clock cause, the DM-able fact, would come second.

**Chosen: 1.** One physical set explains all three causes as visitors at the same door, and the hero is the same door's chain.

## The native move: the dated card under the calendar's string
A cream string runs from the tear-off calendar to a marker on the card's date ruler. The window (AUG → FEB) lights warm when the marker sits inside it. When the calendar flips back, the marker slides out of the window with it: same card, wrong clock.

## Rules for this film
- Coral only on the hero door chain and YES / NO. Frame-0 HACKED? is an ink chip, struck in cream.
- No ✓, SAFE, OK or FALSE ALARM anywhere. "fails", never "expired"; the calendar flips backwards.
- Every warning screen (frame 0, the peephole pop-ups, the Proceed link) carries RE-CREATED; quotes word for word with "…" at each cut.
- Generic site name `shop.example`; YOUR BANK on the hero; no browser logos or colours.
- Props whole inside x 92–928, y 302–1428; the wall and floor bleed full width; ink in y 1000–1430 every second.
