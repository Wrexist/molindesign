# Treatment: "GTA Online Outlived Its Launch Consoles" (Delta, gta-online)

One hard idea: **GTA Online launched rough (Rockstar warned it would be "temperamental"; day one brought lags and complete outages), its PS3 and Xbox 360 versions were shut down in 2021, and it is still running thirteen years later.** One hero object: **the paper server rack** in a server closet, cabled to the two launch consoles. Hero sounds: the two plug pops (the unplug) and the STILL RUNNING stamp.

## Three structures
1. **One closet, one rack (chosen).** The rack never leaves; the story is told by what plugs into it (units added, consoles unplugged, new boxes plugged in) and by its lights (green, red, green).
2. A timeline of console generations. Rejected: an explainer list, and console shapes invite trade dress.
3. A birthday cake with 13 candles. Rejected: says "13" but not "outlived".

## Native move
The unplug: in the pause after "shut down", both cables whip out of the rack's ports, arc and drop onto the shelf; the consoles' power lights die, the boxes sink behind the cabinet lip; on "kept going" newer boxes rise and their plugs click into the same ports.
