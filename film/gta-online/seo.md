## Description
GTA Online outlived the consoles it launched on. Before launch, Rockstar said it was working around the clock to buy and add more servers, and it even warned the first few days would be even more temperamental than usual. On October 1, 2013 it went live on PS3 and Xbox 360, and day one brought lags and complete outages.

On December 16, 2021, Rockstar shut down the PS3 and Xbox 360 versions of GTA Online. The game kept going on newer consoles and PC, and thirteen years after launch it's still running. Take-Two's CEO Strauss Zelnick said in February 2026: "I have every reason to believe we'll continue to support GTA Online."
