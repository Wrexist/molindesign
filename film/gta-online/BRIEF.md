- **The hook is scoped by line 6.** "Outlived its launch consoles" means: GTA Online launched on PS3 and Xbox 360 (Take-Two, fact 1), Rockstar shut the PS3 and Xbox 360 versions down on Dec 16 2021 (fact 5), and the game kept running on newer consoles and PC (fact 6) to this month (fact 7). - **Zelnick is quoted, not paraphrased into a promise.** The voice keeps his hedge ("has every reason to believe"); the strip prints his full sentence. No claim about GTA Online's form, transfers or price after GTA 6.
- **Evergreen.** No "coming", "isn't out", "in X days", no "last month". "Thirteen years later" (Oct 2013 → Nov 2026) stays true. The end question is a pick-one between two games that both exist.
- **No overlap with gta-13years** (GTA 5 PS3 → GTA 6 PS5 wait). This film is GTA Online's launch and survival, not the GTA 5 release gap.
