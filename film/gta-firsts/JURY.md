## Generic or AI-made?
The isometric city (cube buildings, grid windows, a green park square, a navy gradient with a blueprint grid) is the most stock-illustration-looking set Delta has used. Without visible paper grain, cut edges or shadows on the board, it reads as a flat-shaded vector template, not hand-built paper. Push the paper: visible fibre, slightly uneven cut edges and a hard lamp shadow under each building. The torn newsprint caption tape and the stamp are the most hand-made parts. Keep those.
