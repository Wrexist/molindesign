# Treatment: "GTA 3 Didn't Invent Open Worlds" (Delta, gta-firsts)

One hard idea: **GTA 3 didn't invent open worlds (the 1997 GTA was already a city, seen from straight above). It took GTA into 3D in 2001, and it was the first 3D open world to go massive.** One hero object: **the paper city**, a single board of cut-paper buildings on a drafting table, filmed by a real (projected) camera. Hero sounds: the swoop swish with a tick per building standing up, the two stamps.

## Three structures
1. **One city, one camera (chosen).** The same paper city is the whole film; history is told by where the camera stands (above for 1997, street level for 2001) and what fills the streets (a crowd for "massive", traced copies for "a model").
2. A timeline of game boxes 1997 → 2001 → 2026. Rejected: box art invites trade dress and a timeline is an explainer.
3. Two side-by-side screens (2D vs 3D). Rejected: would need screenshots or a look-alike of Rockstar's art.

## Native move
The camera pitch: 30° on frame 0 → 89.5° (straight down) on "1997", while every building folds to 4 % height so the city becomes a map of roofs → back to 24° on "GTA 3" while the buildings stand up from the middle out, one tick each.
