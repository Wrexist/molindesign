GTA 3 WASN'T FIRST

## Description
GTA 3 didn't invent open worlds. The first Grand Theft Auto came out in 1997, and you saw its city from straight above. In 2001, GTA 3 took the series into 3D: Rockstar calls it the first three-dimensional, open-world Grand Theft Auto title.

But it wasn't the first 3D open world. The Strong museum, which runs the World Video Game Hall of Fame (GTA 3 was inducted in 2016), says it wasn't the first 3-D sandbox game. Its real first: it was the first of its kind to achieve massive mainstream popularity, and it became a model for many other open-world games that followed.

Twenty-five years later comes GTA 6, dated November 19, 2026. Which was the bigger leap?
