## Generic or AI-made?
The isometric block city (box buildings with grid windows, a green park square, a navy gradient with a blueprint grid) is still close to stock "isometric city" illustration. At full size the new grain, cream cut edges and hard lamp shadows read as paper, and that is a real step up from round 1. At phone size it reads as clean 3D vector. The torn newsprint captions, the quote strips with ragged edges and the inked stamps are the most hand-made parts. The pegs read as a placeholder crowd. Nothing reads as Rockstar trade dress, and nothing reuses the old Delta stage.

## seo.md packaging
Good. The title "GTA 3 Didn't Invent Open Worlds 🤔" has 6 words, starts with GTA, carries the search phrase and matches frame 0. The cover text `GTA 3 WASN'T FIRST` has 4 words and matches. The description is accurate and scoped, with the platforms removed, every source listed, the "our arithmetic" note and the non-affiliation line. The hashtags are relevant and no more than 5. The pinned comment matches the end vote. One weakness: the keyword "first open world game" will bring searchers who want a name the film never gives. That's acceptable, but don't lead the description with it.
