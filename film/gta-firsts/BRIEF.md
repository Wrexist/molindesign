**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- The caller's topic, "GTA III changed open-world games / what GTA was first to do", is a myth trap: GTA 3 is often called "the first open-world game". The Strong National Museum of Play, which runs the World Video Game Hall of Fame, says the opposite in one sentence: "It wasn't the first 3-D 'sandbox' game to allow players to freely interact with an open virtual world, or even the first game in its juggernaut franchise." The film is built on that belief-break, then gives the two "firsts" reliable sources do state, each in the source's own scope:
  1. **Rockstar's own first, scoped to GTA:** "It was the first three-dimensional, open-world Grand Theft Auto title" (Rockstar press release, Oct 13 2011). Stamp: `FIRST 3D GTA` (not "first 3D open world").
  2. **The museum's first, scoped to its kind:** "the first of its kind to achieve massive mainstream popularity and widespread critical acclaim". Stamp: `FIRST 3D OPEN WORLD / TO GO MASSIVE` ("of its kind" = 3D sandbox games, the sentence before).
- **No cause the sources don't state.** The script never says GTA 3 is in the Hall of Fame *because* of X; it says "The video game Hall of Fame says so" (the "so" is the popularity first, which is the Hall of Fame page's own sentence). "It became a model for later open worlds" is the museum's "became a model for many other open-world games that followed" ("a model", not "the model"; the voice says "a model").
- **Dates only as the sources give them.** GTA III: Rockstar says "October 2001"; press splits Oct 22 vs Oct 23, so only the year 2001 is spoken and printed. First GTA: Time Extension (Nov 28 2022 anniversary piece) gives PC on November 28th, 1997; spoken and printed only as 1997, labelled `FIRST GTA · PC`.
- **Evergreen.** No "coming", "isn't out", "in X days". The GTA 6 line is "Twenty-five years later: GTA 6." (2001 → 2026), and the date card prints Rockstar's date `NOV 19, 2026`, true before and after launch. End question is a pick-one between two games.
- **No trade dress.** The city is a generic paper grid (no Liberty City map, no landmarks, no logo, no box art, no characters). The car is a plain yellow paper block. People are plain paper pins.

## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 3 didn't invent open worlds (even the first GTA, 1997, was a city seen from straight above). What it did first: it took GTA into 3D in 2001, and it was the first 3D open world to go massive.** One hero object: **the paper city**, one board of cut-paper buildings on a drafting table, with a real camera. Native move: the camera goes straight up for 1997 (the buildings fold flat into a map) and swoops to the street for 2001 (they stand back up one by one, a tick each).

Main search phrase: `GTA 3` / `first open world game` (frame 0 caption `GTA 3 DIDN'T INVENT OPEN WORLDS.`, "GTA 3" spoken by ~0.6 s).

DM-able moment: the crowd flooding the paper streets under `FIRST 3D OPEN WORLD / TO GO MASSIVE`, with the museum's sentence above it. It's what you send the friend who says GTA 3 was the first open world.

## 2. Hook options

2. "What did GTA 3 really do first?" A question with no stake; weaker.
