**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- **Whose number.** $3.79 billion is **Take-Two's own estimate** (the release's footnote: "*based on company estimates through end of day November 19th worldwide"). The voice says "by Take-Two's own estimate"; the screen carries `TAKE-TWO ESTIMATE` on the hook and on every GTA 6 number beat. Never "confirmed", "official total" or "audited".
- **What it measures.** "Day one **retail sales**", in dollars. Not units, not profit, not revenue booked by Take-Two (retail sales are what stores and storefronts took in). The voice says "sales" and "made" (same sense as gta-money, which passed Truth 8.5); never "profit" or "earned".
- **"One day", never "24 hours".** The window is "through end of day November 19th worldwide", which across time zones is longer than 24 hours, and the release does not say how much of it is pre-order money counted at launch. Title, voice and screen say **"one day" / "day one"** only. Do not use RUN.md's example title "in 24 Hours".
- **The comparison is like for like.** Same company, same kind of number: GTA V "first day sell-through of more than $800 million worldwide … according to Company estimates" (Take-Two, Sept 18 2013) and "worldwide retail sales of more than $1 billion during its first three days on sale, according to Company estimates" (Take-Two, Sept 20 2013). Screen labels both `TAKE-TWO ESTIMATE · 2013`.
  - GTA V's day one **excluded Japan and Brazil** (launched later there). Screen fine print on the GTA 5 receipt: `EXCL. JAPAN & BRAZIL`. It makes GTA 5 look smaller than its full launch, so it must be shown.
  - Both are **"more than"**: screen `$800M+` and `$1B+`. The voice says "over $800 million".
  - **Nominal dollars**, no inflation adjustment. Screen fine print `2013 DOLLARS` on the GTA 5 receipt. No inflation number in the film (I did not fetch a CPI source today).
- **No ratio in the voice.** 3.79 / 0.80 = 4.74, but GTA V's figure is "more than $800 million" with no upper bound, so "nearly five times" could overstate it. The film never says "times"; the two receipts sit side by side and the viewer sees it. (Also: gta-streampeak already used "more than four and a half times"; a second "times" line would feel like a template.)
- **"GTA 5 hit one billion in three days"** = the Sept 20 2013 release, word for word ("more than $1 billion during its first three days"). We do not say "needed" or "took" three days (day two's total is unknown; it may have passed $1B before the end of day three).
- **"GTA 6 passed three billion in a single day"** = $3.79B ≥ $3B, from the same release. True, and it rounds down, so no "over" fuzz.
- **"The biggest entertainment launch in history, Take-Two says"** = the release: "setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history." Attributed in the voice ("Take-Two says") and on screen (`"BIGGEST ENTERTAINMENT LAUNCH IN HISTORY" · TAKE-TWO`). It is Take-Two's claim; we did not check it against every movie or album.
- **No movies.** The release names no film. A box-office comparison would set ticket grosses against retail sales (different measures, different windows); left out (see Not used).
- **Not the 99.** The Metacritic score is in the release's subhead but is gta-99's film; not mentioned.
- **Dates.** No "today" or "yesterday" in the voice. The number is a dated company estimate; if Take-Two revises it before posting, re-voice the hook.
