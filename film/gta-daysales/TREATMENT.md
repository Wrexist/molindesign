# gta-daysales — treatment

**One idea:** GTA 6 took in $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales (Take-Two's own estimate, through end of day Nov 19 worldwide). GTA 5's famous launch, by the same company's same kind of estimate, was over $800 million on day one and over $1 billion after three days. One picture: two cash registers on one counter, a short 2013 receipt and a 2026 receipt that pours off the counter.

## Three structures
1. **Checkout counter (chosen).** A pink cut-paper cash register is the hero; its receipt is the measure. The GTA 5 register (grey, 2013, boxy) prints a short tape over three days; the GTA 6 register's tape shoots out in one day and runs off the counter. The viewer compares two lengths of paper, no ratio spoken (BRIEF: "more than" has no upper bound).
2. Calendar race (day pages tearing off while money stacks). Rejected: rolling dated counters with labels attached is a LESSONS mistake, and calendar pads were the gta-longestwait set.
3. Bar chart with three bars. Rejected: the dashboard template juries dock, and it would invite reading the GTA 5 bar as an exact $800M.

## Native move
**The tape run.** Receipt paper feeds out of the printer slot. GTA 5's tape ticks out slowly, one printed day at a time; GTA 6's pours continuously, loops over the register and down past the counter edge, its printed lines streaming. It settles once, never bounces. Hero sound: the register *ka-ching* (bell + drawer thunk) on the hook stamp and on `fast`.

## Truth notes
- Every GTA 6 number beat carries `TAKE-TWO ESTIMATE` (hook chip, est receipt line, fast chip).
- GTA 5 numbers always show `+` and carry `EXCL. JAPAN & BRAZIL` and `2013 DOLLARS`; day 2 has no number on the tape (unknown).
- "One day" / "day one" only, never "24 hours". No ratio on screen.
