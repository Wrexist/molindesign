## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **17.85-21.5 s, the tape run (wow).** Make the GTA 6 tape really pour. On "passed" it should shoot up out of the slot, fold over the register top, then run down the counter front and pile into loose loops on the kraft from y 1300 to 1440 (keep it inside x 90-930 and above y 1450). It should settle once by 19.0 s. Add 4-6 printed line ticks a second streaming down it and continuous printer chatter under it (gain 0.6), ending in the ka-ching on "single day". This fills the dead lower third (fixes the repeat) and makes the DM frame.
2. **20.0-24.8 s, DM frame clutter.** Shrink `PAST $3B` to about 70 % and move it onto the tape pile at y ~1350, so it stops covering the GTA 6 register. Peel the `3 DAYS` tag when the quote plate lands (21.45 s). At most 4 reads on screen: quote, GTA 5 receipt, GTA 6 tape, one `1 DAY · $3.79B` chip.
3. **0.0-2.0 s, hook for sound-off.** Change the display or a small line under the headline to `DAY 1 SALES` so the search phrase "GTA 6 sales" reads by 2 s. Keep the headline at 4 words, for example headline `GTA 6: $3.79 BILLION` and the display `DAY 1 SALES` instead of a second `$3.79B`. Move the hero ka-ching (bell plus drawer pop, gain 1.3) onto the `IN ONE DAY` stamp at 1.08 s, in the gap between "six" and "three point", and keep a lighter tick at 3.14.
4. **7.0-9.4 s, the empty ask beat.** The register is about 420 px and there is an empty wall from y 500 to 880. Scale the GTA 6 register to 0.8 (not 0.6), put the `?` card at about 260 px in the dashed slot at y ~700, and print an empty GTA 5 receipt stub in the slot so something prints into it (LESSONS #34/#35 slot rule). The dashed slot must stay up for 1 s or more before the 2013 register drops in.
5. **9.4-17.4 s, the grey GTA 5 stretch.** Add hits: a slam on the `$800M+` display on "million" (~11.9 s, gain 1.1), a print tick for each of `DAY 2` and `DAY 3` (~15.6 and ~16.6 s), and a stamp on the `$1B+` flip on "three days". Re-pace the display text so `$800M+` and `$1B+` fit inside the display window with at least 12 px of padding (it overflows on the left now).
6. **Truth polish, 18-24 s.** Add a `NOT TO SCALE` note (36 px or more) at the foot of the GTA 6 tape, or make the tape length exactly 3.79/0.80 of the GTA 5 day-1 tape and label it `LENGTH ∝ $`. Optionally change the voiced "made" in `old` to "sold" ("GTA 5 sold over $800 million on day one"). That matches "sell-through" and avoids reading as revenue.
7. **25.0-27.4 s, end chips.** Use `YES · DAY ONE` / `NOT YET` (as the brief says) at 44 px or more, with equal colour. Bring the seam diff under 2 (match the encoded last frame to the encoded first frame, LESSONS #34).
8. **All beats, set life.** The three lamps are identical and static on every frame. Give them a slow 1-2° sway with offset phases, and let the receipt-paper curl or the display flicker once every 4-6 s. Without that, the paper set looks like a template between hits.
9. **4.0-6.9 s, est receipt.** The receipt header lines are about 30 px at full res on the GTA 6 slip (`DAY ONE / RETAIL / SALES / $3.79B`). Bring them to 44 px or more as the brief planned, and print them on their words ("day one sales" → `DAY ONE RETAIL SALES`, "estimate" → the `TAKE-TWO ESTIMATE` chip highlight).

## Generic or AI-made tells

- Symmetric triple pendant lamps on every frame: decoration with no job.
- The `PAST $3B` grunge rubber stamp is the house stamp from gta-99 and gta-streampeak. A third film in a row with the same stamp texture is starting to feel like a template.
