# Treatment: "Is GTA 5's city real?" (Delta, gta-losantos)

One hard idea: **Los Santos is Rockstar's re-imagined L.A. (its art director: "all of Los Angeles", 250,000+ images; Edge: the Hollywood sign became Vinewood, Santa Monica's pier became Del Perro, Santos = saints for the angels), but it is not a copy.**

## Benchmarks
| | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| film/gta-outsold (7.8, Truth 9.0) | Every number taped to its owner; counters roll in round steps; quotes on torn strips with the owner chip under them; SFX in the written pauses, VAD-gated. | The curtain stage, paper towers of cases and the covered `?` on frame 0: juries now call that a template. |
| film/gta-vercetti (held) | Use the source's own verb ("homage", "incorporates", "starting point"). | A negation split across caption cards. |

## Three structures
1. **Light table with vellum overlays (chosen).** Each beat lays a new sheet: the real place in cobalt ink; the game's version on orange vellum slides over it and snaps onto register pins. The dip is the vellum landing off-register: NOT A COPY.
2. A split-screen postcard rack (real left, game right). Rejected: static halves, a dashboard look.
3. A road trip on a drawn map. Rejected: invites a map-accuracy claim we can't source.

## Native move
The vellum slide-and-register: orange sheet glides in from the right, decelerates, clicks onto the pins (a click in the pause). Off-register once, on "copy".
