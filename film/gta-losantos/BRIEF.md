**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- **Only links the sources state.** Rockstar's own words are "a re-imagined, present day Southern California" (Rockstar North's GTA V page). The direct "Los Angeles" link comes from Rockstar North's art director, Aaron Garbut, on the record (BuzzFeed 2013: "a world that incorporates all of Los Angeles"), and from Edge (GamesRadar+). The voice never says "Rockstar says Los Santos is L.A."; it says Rockstar sets it in a re-imagined Southern California, and that *its art director* says it takes in all of L.A.
- **Landmarks: only two, both stated by Edge.** "Hollywood and its signage transmogrify into Vinewood" and "Del Perro pier (a lovingly rendered homage to Santa Monica's pier)". Griffith Observatory / Galileo Observatory was dropped: only fan wikis, a small blog (Splice Today) and a GameSpot page we could not open state it.
- **Not a copy.** Edge: "The city isn't an exact recreation of Los Angeles"; Garbut to Edge (2014): "we're still a long way from being dictated by reality. Instead, we use it as a starting point." That is the dip. The voice attributes it to "Rockstar's art director".
- **The name.** Edge: "The 'Santos' of its name means 'Saints' in Spanish ... sidling up against the 'Angels' of real-world Los Angeles." Spanish: *santos* = saints, *Los Ángeles* = the angels. On screen with `SPANISH · VIA EDGE MAGAZINE`.
- **GTA 6:** (cut in round 2; was) Rockstar's own place name only ("Vice City, Leonida", rockstargames.com/VI). Nothing about Florida, no date, no "coming".
- **Drawings are ours and generic:** a skyline, a coast, block letters on a hill in our own type, a pier with a wheel. No screenshots, no box art, no trade dress.
