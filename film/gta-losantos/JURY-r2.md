## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **28.2–32.0 s: make "not a copy" a new picture, not a fourth showing of the hook (Retention, Clarity, Share; the wow).** At 29.3 s the sign overlay is the same VINEWOOD-above-HOLLYWOOD frame as 1.2 s and 15.5 s, so it says "copy". Replace it with one sheet that holds the real drawing (cobalt: coast, sign hill, pier, about 820 px wide). Slide the whole orange Los Santos vellum over it, and let the pins **miss by about 60 px**: the vellum lands visibly off-register, the shapes don't line up, thud. Stamp `NOT A COPY` at 30.4 s rather than 31.3 s. Under it, tape Garbut's sharper line from the same Edge interview, `"I'D NEVER WANT TO REBUILD A CITY"` (exact source words, `GARBUT · EDGE 2014`), and add an `OUR DRAWINGS` chip so the invented differences are scoped. Cut the copy pause from 1.9 to 1.2 s. That frame is the second poster.
2. **0.0–2.4 s: hold the question and fix the hero word (Hook, Sound, Craft).** Slow the vellum so it registers at 2.15 s, right after "real?" (Whisper ends "real?" at about 2.1 s), and fire the click there instead of 2.38 s (now 1.2 s late). Keep at least 300 px of glide over 0–1 s, so frame 0 stays fully printed and moving. Fix the VINEWOOD kerning (the I overlaps the V by about 15 px) on the hook, sign, copy and loop frames. Then re-check that frame 0 still reads `HOLLYWOOD` in full.
3. **Whole film: break the window (Craft, −1 for repeating LESSONS 288).** The sheet is an 835x890 square at y 540–1430 in every frame, with 45 % empty mat. Make the sheet full-bleed (x 0–1080, y 330–1450) for at least the hook, sign, copy and end. Keep props and type inside x 90–930 and above y 1450, then pixel-check the render. Pull the caption to sit on the sheet's top edge (y about 300–420), not floating on the mat.
4. **35.7–39.7 s: the end card is a static 3.3 s with tiny drawings (Retention, Loop).** Stack SIGN and PIER as two ≥ 420 px panels, VINEWOOD lettering ≥ 90 px. Move the two taps out of "Vine-" (35.65 and 35.79 s, masked by the voice) into a pause: write 0.35 s after "Del Perro Pier:" and tap each panel there. End within 0.5 s of "first?" (38.9 s → film at about 39.4 s with the wipe).

On the poster frame the dashed LOS ANGELES ghost is wider than the tag, and an "L" and an "S" stick out at x about 190 and 800. Fade the ghost to 0 within 0.2 s of the tag landing.
