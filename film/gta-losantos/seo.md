## Description
Is GTA 5's Los Santos a real place? Rockstar sets GTA 5 in a re-imagined, present day Southern California, and Rockstar North's art director says the world incorporates all of Los Angeles. His team shot over 250,000 images for it.

The Hollywood sign became the Vinewood sign. Santa Monica's pier became Del Perro Pier. Even the name plays on it: Santos means saints, Los Angeles means the angels.

But it is not a copy: Rockstar's art director calls the real city a starting point.

Vinewood sign or Del Perro Pier? Which would you visit first?
