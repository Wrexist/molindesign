# GTA 6 has a trunk streamer (treatment)

Delta, "It has X, shown" Short (VIDEO-PLAN format 5), launch week. Ming US_James seed 7, no music, official Rockstar clips on our own drawn phone.

## Benchmarks
| Film | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| "GTA 6 Has Actual Jail Time Now CONFIRMED" (551k, launch-week Shorts list) | One feature, named in the title, shown in the first seconds. | "CONFIRMED" with no source on screen: ours carries Rockstar's own capture line and the video's timestamp. |
| gta-holdup (Delta, previous film) | Footage inside a drawn object, a HUD re-drawn big and tagged RE-DRAWN, the frame-0 layout returning for the loop. | Its security monitors, channel switch and rail. A new set per film. |

## Three structures
1. **A phone feed (chosen).** The subject is a stream, so the hero object is a phone: the footage plays on our phone's screen (cover-fitted to vertical), and the stream overlay is re-drawn as a transparent layer over our own cut-paper version of the 18:24 chase frame (jury r1). Native move: the swipe up between beats.
2. A chat log that scrolls the whole film. Rejected: the chat is the punchline, not the setting; it would bury the footage.
3. A police scanner / chase map. Rejected: pulls toward the wanted angle Delta already covered.

## Native move
**The swipe up**: every beat leaves upward and the next rises from below, like a vertical feed, with a swish in the pause.
