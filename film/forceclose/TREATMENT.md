# Treatment · Closing apps won't save your battery

Factory #25 (`forceclose`), director's plan before the scenes. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json`.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `incognito` (#21, 1,281 views, 6/6 packaging):** the hook is the maker's own sentence, the proof is the maker's own page (re-created, tagged), and hook, page and payoff explain one mechanism. **Don't take:** a label sitting over a group it doesn't name (ON YOUR DEVICE), and a shredded prop that read as tidy at 540 px.
- **Learn from `whatif-password` (#23):** one hand-made hero prop, the hero stamp inks on its word and the thump presses it in a written silence, the end card is frame 0 at full opacity, every hit sits in a planned pause. **Don't take:** a 10.4 MB upload (keep under 9.5 MB) and stale events.json in the SFX gate.
- **And from `embeddings` (#24, parked at 7.1):** a strip left beside a new subject reads as a claim about it. Every strip and stamp peels before the next subject lands.

## Three structures
1. **The switcher, then the letter.** Frame 0 is the viewer's own app switcher (paper cards) with one card swiped away; Apple's page; what really drains it; the swiped card rebuilt from scratch; the 2016 email stamped NO AND NO; what does drain it; the settings check; the one card worth closing; frame 0 again. One mechanism, the card is in every beat.
2. **Cold open on the email.** NO AND NO on frame 0 is the strongest image, but it shows the answer on frame 0 (strawberry lesson) and puts a 2016 story before the viewer's own habit.
3. **A battery gauge race** (swipe vs no swipe, two gauges). Clear, but it would need a number for the relaunch cost that no first-party source gives (BRIEF: kept out on purpose).

**Chosen: 1.** The viewer's own habit on frame 0, the maker's page as proof, the story as the turn, a check they can do tonight.

## The native move
**The card flick.** Every app card is a hand-cut paper card. Swiping flings a card up through the top of the safe box. Every scene change is the same move: the whole scene shrinks into a card and is flicked up, with the next scene already built under it (no empty stage under a wipe). The one "good" swipe at the end (the frozen, hogging app) is the same flick.

## Rules for this film
- Coral only on the hero stamp “NO AND NO.” and the YES / NO chips. The ≠ on frame 0 and the underline on Apple's sentence are cream.
- Quotes on screen are only words the voice says at that moment: Apple's “…close an app only if it's unresponsive.” on `apple`, and “No and no.” on `answer`. No Lockheimer chip (never spoken). The fan's question is unquoted and printed as it is spoken.
- Nothing on screen says "drains" about closing, or "more battery"; `work` shows MORE WORK (Android Developers, app startup), not battery.
- Settings paths only for iPhone and Pixel. Generic phone screen and app switcher drawn by hand: no logos, no real UI, app cards carry generic icons (note, pin, bubble, mountain) and no brand names.
- Every strip and stamp peels before the next subject lands; the hero hold (stamp, letter, source line) is the only still ≥ 1.0 s.
