## Description
Every main GTA hero, from GTA 3 to GTA 6. Can you name them before the name shows up?

Main console games since GTA III (2001). Spin-offs, handheld games, the GTA IV episodes, GTA 1, GTA 2 and the London packs are left out.

2001, GTA III, Liberty City: Claude. He never speaks, and GTA III never names him; his name was revealed later, in San Andreas (2004).
2002, Vice City: Tommy Vercetti.
2004, San Andreas, Los Santos: Carl "CJ" Johnson.
2008, GTA IV, Liberty City again: Niko Bellic.
2013, GTA V, Los Santos: one hero wasn't enough. Michael, Franklin and Trevor.
2026, GTA VI, Vice City, in the state of Leonida: Jason and Lucia. That's 24 years after Tommy's Vice City.

Best GTA hero? Drop the number in the comments: 1 Claude, 2 Tommy, 3 CJ, 4 Niko, 5 Michael, 6 Franklin, 7 Trevor, 8 Jason, 9 Lucia.
