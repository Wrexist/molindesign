**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- **Scope: "main GTA hero" = the lead(s) of the six main console games since GTA III (2001):** III, Vice City, San Andreas, IV, V, VI. Left out on purpose: GTA 1, GTA 2 and the London packs (2D era, pick-a-character), Advance, Liberty City Stories, Vice City Stories, Chinatown Wars, the IV episodes (Johnny Klebitz, Luis Lopez). The voice says "main", and frame 0 carries a chip `MAIN GAMES · SINCE 2001`. Never say "every GTA hero" without "main" (LESSONS gta-editions r2: scope the voice, not only the chip).
- **Claude.** He is unnamed and silent in GTA III; Wikipedia: "Claude is unnamed in Grand Theft Auto III … his name is not officially revealed until his appearance in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004)" and "He is a silent protagonist, never speaking throughout his appearances in the series." Rockstar's own GTA III blurbs (rockstargames.com/games/grandtheftauto3, Rockstar Store Trilogy page) don't name him. So the voice says "Claude, who never speaks" (true across the series) and **the card carries fine print `NAME GIVEN LATER · SAN ANDREAS, 2004`**. Never write "in GTA 3 his name is Claude".
- **"One hero wasn't enough"** describes the move from one lead to three in GTA V; it's our line, not a Rockstar quote. No "first game with three protagonists" claim (true per Wikipedia's "featured three playable protagonists" but not needed; Rockstar has not been checked on "first").
- **"Now it's two: Jason and Lucia."** Rockstar: "Vice City, USA. Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them" and "Jason and Lucia's story". Voice keeps Rockstar's order (Jason first). **No "first female lead", "first woman", "first couple", "first time two"** (LESSONS gta-lucia r1/r2). No word on how switching works, who is "the" protagonist, or anything past the page's own synopsis.
- **"Back to Vice City. 24 years after Tommy."** Vice City first release Oct 29 2002 (NA PS2) → GTA VI Nov 19 2026 = 24.06 years (OUR MATH). Rockstar: GTA VI is set in "Vice City, USA" across "the state of Leonida". The card says `VICE CITY · LEONIDA`. Never say Leonida = Florida, never say it's "the same" Vice City (Wikipedia calls it a third version).
- **Years are first-release years** (NA PS2 for III/VC/SA, worldwide PS3/360 for IV/V, PS5/Series for VI). Cards print the year only, no month, no platform.
- **No spoilers.** Each hero gets name + game + year + city only. Nothing from any story beyond the opening premise; no endings, deaths, betrayals.
