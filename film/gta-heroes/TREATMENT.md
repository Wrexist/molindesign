# Every main GTA hero: treatment

Delta, pillar 3 (GTA history), "Every X" catalog run as a quiz. Voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), no music, own graphics only.

## Three structures
1. **Card deck (chosen).** One manila case-file card per hero, dealt onto a stack on a dark desk. Year and city are printed, the name is under a black redaction bar; the bar tears off on the spoken name. GTA V's card tears into three, GTA VI's into two. Ends on a numbered ballot card and the case-file cover drops back on for the loop.
2. Timeline rail: a horizontal string of years with hero tags hanging off it. Rejected: a rail of nine tags in a vertical frame is small type and reads as a chart.
3. Line-up wall: silhouettes against height lines. Rejected: silhouettes edge toward likenesses (brief §6.5) and repeat the "?" tower look.

## Native move
**The redaction tear.** Every name is hidden under a black bar that peels left to right on the spoken name (tear sound in the breath before it), the torn strip flies off, and the name is underneath. GTA V and GTA VI break the pattern: the card itself tears into narrow cards, one per lead.

## Set
A walnut desk at night, top-down, a green leather blotter bleeding the frame, a warm lamp pool drifting, a brass stopwatch bottom right whose red hand makes exactly one turn per film (so the loop is seamless), a pencil that jumps when a card slaps down. Cards: manila 820 x 660, typed mono labels (GAME, YEAR, CITY, NAME), condensed values, coral case tab, paper clip. Type at least 36 px; everything inside x 90-930, y 300-1430.

## Sound
Card slap on each deal, a paper tear in the breath before each name, a rubber stamp for NO LINES, a swish when the stack is swept, a string twang on 24 YEARS, a click as the cover lands at the seam. Hits sit in pauses (vo/vad.js).
