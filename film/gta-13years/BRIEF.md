# gta-13years: 13 years since GTA 5 (Delta, Short #1)

Channel: Delta (GTA 6 line). Rules: `delta/SAFE-EDIT.md`. No Rockstar footage, logo or key art, no music, rumors labeled (none used).

## One hard idea
GTA 6 took thirteen years and two months after GTA 5, more than double the longest gap before it, and the date was moved twice on the way.

## Hook options
1. "GTA 6 took thirteen years." (5 words; chosen: says GTA 6 in the first second, a number, a contradiction with how fast other sequels come.)
2. "GTA 5 came out in 2013." (backlog hook, 6 words; used as line 2 so it lands in the first 3 s.)
3. "Thirteen years. One game." (4 words; weaker, no GTA 6 in the first second.)

Main search phrase: "GTA 6 release date" / "GTA 5 to GTA 6 gap" (said in lines 1 to 3, shown as the date cards).
