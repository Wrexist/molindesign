# gta-13years: treatment

## Benchmarks
- `film/nextword/` (learn: voice-first timing with `at(id, word)`, word-highlight captions, the loop dissolve into frame 0; don't take: the caret and cream/coral look).
- `film/memory/` (learn: one hero object owns the frame and the payoff stamp comes once; don't take: chat bubbles).

## Structures considered
1. Pure countdown: a calendar page flips from 2013 to 2026. Clean, but the delays have nowhere to live.
2. Versus: GTA 5 left, GTA 6 right with bars. Good for the "more than double" beat only.
3. **Chosen: one year ruler and a sun.** The striped sunset disc sits behind the giant 13 on frame 0, rolls along a 2013 to 2026 ruler, and the same ruler later holds the two crossed-out dates, so the whole film is one object travelling one axis.

## Native move
The striped Vice City sun: stripes slide through it continuously (something moves in every frame), it carries the "wait" along the ruler, and it sets on "almost over".
