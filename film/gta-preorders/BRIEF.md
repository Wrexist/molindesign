- **No ratio, no difference voiced.** "3.5 million more" would need GTA V's exact figure (it is "nearly", so the gap is a bit more than 3.5M); not voiced or shown.
- **"A record across all platforms, Rockstar says."** Rockstar's claim, attributed. Not "the biggest launch ever" (that was the $3.79B claim, yesterday's film).
- **Plates are GTA footage of the right game.** The two 2013 beats play GTA 5's own 2013 Gameplay Video and must carry a `GTA 5 · 2013` chip; every GTA 6 beat plays GTA 6 footage. Never a GTA 5 plate under a GTA 6 number or the reverse (LESSONS gta-money: a pill read against the caption beside it).
