# gta-preorders: treatment

GTA 6 sold 32.5 million before launch (Rockstar, Nov 20 2026), more than GTA 5 had shipped to stores six weeks after its 2013 launch (nearly 29 million, Take-Two, Oct 29 2013). Delta launch-week news Short, ~25 s, Ming US_James, no music.

## Benchmarks
| Film | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| gta-daysales (yesterday, jury 7.2 / Truth 9.0) | The number big on frame 0 with the hero prop already up; every hit in a voice pause (vad.js); a source chip on every number; last frame = frame 0. | Its empty lower third (jury repeat −1), the DM frame stacking six reads, the pendant lamps that never had a job, the same grunge stamp three films running. |
| gta-wildfinds / gta-thingstodo (Delta clip films) | Real GTA 6 gameplay on screen most of the time reads as "the game", not an infographic. | Footage as the hero: here plates stay dimmed under our props, never the subject. |

## Three structures
1. **Departure board (chosen).** One split-flap board: GTA 6's 32.5M already up; GTA 5's row drops in empty, flips one blank flap per week for six weeks and lands ~29M; GTA 6 re-slams under it "before day one". The comparison is two rows of the same board, labeled with what each row counts.
2. Calendar race: two tear-off calendars, GTA 5's ripping 42 pages while GTA 6's sits on DAY 0. Rejected: page-ripping is time, not units; the numbers would be small chips.
3. Stacked boxes: copies as boxes piling up. Rejected: a pile implies a scale ratio the brief does not voice.

## Native move
The **flap slam**: every cell flips blank flaps for a third of a second, then lands its final character left to right (60 ms stagger). The counter never rolls through other numbers (LESSONS). GTA 5's row is the slow version: one blank flap per week tick, a WEEK 1 to 6 flap beside it.

## Layout
- Frame 0: `GTA 6:` (pink sticker) / the 32.5M board (cream flaps, 810 px wide) / `BEFORE LAUNCH` with a pink underline drawing in, on an ink backdrop. No plate until 1.0 s (frame 0 all ours, cleaner hook).
- An ink shelf with a pink lip bleeds the full width at y 1300; the plate label (`FOOTAGE: GTA 5 (2013)` / `FOOTAGE: GTA 6`) and the stamp live on it, so the lower third is never empty.
- Middle: two rows, GTA 5 above (chip + week flap / grey board / SHIPPED tag), GTA 6 below (chip + DAY 0 / pink board / SOLD tag).

## Truth calls carried on screen
- "sold" (GTA 6) vs "shipped to stores" (GTA 5) on the two tags; `~29M` never `29M+`; no ratio, no difference.
- Every number has its source and date on screen; the quote has its leading "…" because the sentence is cut.
- GTA 5 plates are desaturated and labeled `FOOTAGE: GTA 5 (2013)`; every GTA 6 beat plays GTA 6 footage.
- Plate swaps from the brief: P1 skyline 1545.2 is now used by gta-wildfinds, so the hook plays the mural street 1026.2-1029.7; P2 (store robbery: people cowering, a body at 711 s) replaced by the boat 1550.8-1552.4; P6 trimmed to the bike jump 48.3-50.0 (hidden cut at 48.2); P7 trimmed to the marina 4.4-6.2 (cuts at 4.33 and 6.27).
