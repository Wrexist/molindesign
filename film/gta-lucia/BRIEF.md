GTA 6 launches 2026-11-19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (rockstargames.com/VI).

## 1. Topic and one hard idea
**Since GTA III in 2001, every lead Rockstar wrote was a man. Women were playable before (1997) and beside it (GTA Online), but never as a written lead. Lucia is the first woman Rockstar wrote to lead a GTA story, sharing it with Jason.** (Reframed on "written" after jury round 1; see the Log.)

- **Stake:** a 25-year streak, and GTA 6 ends it.
- **Contradiction (the turn):** "But you could play a woman in 1997." The viewer who knows that is answered right away: the pick was only cosmetic, and GTA Online's woman is a blank you build yourself: no name, no past.
- **Hero number:** **25 YEARS** (2001 to 2026, derived). It is spoken in the hook, shown big on frame 0 and on the poster. No other number gets a stamp (LESSONS Delta: one hero number). The 13 men and 11 games are shown as a picture (a lineup of cards), never as a spoken or stamped count.
- **Main search phrase:** `GTA 6 Lucia`. "GTA 6" is spoken at 0.2 s (hook) and "Lucia … GTA 6" at about 3.5 to 6 s. On screen: GTA 6 by 0.3 s, then LUCIA from "Meet".

**Why not the backlog wording.** "GTA has never done this before" and "first female protagonist" are false as stated. The 1997 original let you choose from eight characters, four of them women (fact 5), and GTA Online lets you create a female character (fact 9). Several outlets ran "Lucia is not the first female protagonist" pieces (LevelUp, Dot Esports, Sportskeeda), so a "first ever" claim would be called out in the comments and cost Truth. The claim that survives checking is **"the first woman Rockstar wrote to lead a GTA story"**, and the film says the qualifier out loud ("Rockstar wrote", "lead", "story") and on screen. (The first cut said "the first woman to lead a 3D GTA story"; jury round 1 retired it, see §3.)

**Tense note:** posted 2026-10-31, before launch. "Ends" is a headline present. If the jury reads it as claiming the game is out, the fallback is "GTA 6 breaks a 25-year streak." (same length). "Is ending" would be 7 words.

## 3. The claim, exactly
**Spoken:** "Since then [GTA 3, 2001], every lead Rockstar wrote was a man." … "GTA Online lets you make one. But she's a blank: no name, no past." … "The first woman Rockstar wrote to lead a GTA story."
**On screen, same frame as the claim, big (≥ 64 px):** `FIRST WOMAN WRITTEN TO LEAD A GTA STORY` with the chip `CO-LEAD · WITH JASON`. On the wall: `WRITTEN LEADS · 2001–2025`, each card plated with its release year. On the Online creator: `NO NAME · NO PAST`.

**Why "written", not "3D" (jury round 1):** the first cut counted silent Claude (GTA III) as a lead but ruled out GTA Online's woman because "she never speaks", a double standard, and Wikipedia calls Online's character a "protagonist" too. What separates the wall from Online is not speech but authorship: every card is a named character with a story Rockstar wrote (Claude has a name and a written betrayal plot even though he never talks); Online's character is player-made, with no set name and no written backstory (fact 8: "players create a new character specific to the online world"). "3D" is gone from every claim because the wall includes two top-down games (Advance, Chinatown Wars); `three` keeps "GTA 3 took the series 3D in 2001" as history only, and its chip now reads THE STREAK STARTS.

**Why it is true:**
- Every GTA game released from 2001 to 2025 has a male story lead. That covers the 3D games (III, Vice City, San Andreas, Liberty City Stories, Vice City Stories, IV, The Lost and Damned, The Ballad of Gay Tony, V) and the two top-down handhelds of the same years (Advance, Chinatown Wars). It is 13 leads in 11 games, all men (facts 10 to 20). So "since then, every lead Rockstar wrote was a man" is true for every game on the wall, top-down handhelds included, which is why the claim carries no "3D".
- GTA Online (2013) lets the player create a female character, but Online's player character is a "customisable silent protagonist" the player builds (fact 8): the player names her, and Rockstar writes her no backstory. The film says so ("But she's a blank: no name, no past"). It does not claim Online has no women, and it no longer leans on "silent" (Claude is silent too).
- The 1997 original's women were selectable, but "the choice is purely aesthetic" (fact 5). The film says "Same missions. Only the look changed." It does **not** say Lucia is the first woman you can play, and it does **not** say "first ever".
- "Lead" for Lucia rests on Rockstar's own words: "Jason and Lucia's story" and "Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them" (facts 1 and 2), plus her own character page (fact 3). Rockstar's text never uses the words "protagonist" or "playable" (checked by text search of the page source, 2026-10-30), so the script never says "play as Lucia" either. "Co-lead with Jason" is shown on screen so nobody reads "lead" as "only lead".
