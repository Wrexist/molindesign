# Treatment: Lucia breaks the lineup (gta-lucia)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-songs/** (Delta #5, held at 7.2): learn the voice-first keying with `at(id, word)`, the slanted pink and teal cut, the SFX gate fed by what Whisper heard in the mix (`vo/heard.js`), `duck.smr_db` 56, and the end vote with neither option highlighted. Don't take its card scenes that sit high and leave y 1000–1430 empty, or its four silent wipes: here one object (the wall) fills y 470–1340 in every scene, and the hero sound is written into the script's pause before voicing.
- **A police lineup photo (the genre, not any one picture):** learn the height-chart stripes behind a row of people, the number boards and the flash that a lineup shot implies; everyone recognises it in a frame. Don't take any real mugshot, any Rockstar art, or a traced character: every figure is a generic head-and-shoulders silhouette built from an ellipse and a rounded trapezoid.

## Structures considered
1. History walk: one card per GTA game, 2001 → 2013. Clear, but a list (gta-vicecity, held at 7.2, taught that a walk across games reads as a list), and per-game cards would make 13 cuts.
2. Two-column split (men | women) with bars per era. Needs a chart and a second number; turns one surprise into a statistic.
3. **One wall, filled and broken (chosen).** A police lineup wall with 13 grey slots and one double-width pink slot is on screen from frame 0 to the last frame. It empties, refills one lead per spoken name with a clack, the counter in the pink slot lands on 13, then Lucia (and Jason beside her) fill the shared fourteenth slot. The turn (1997) rewinds the wall into a pixel character-select strip, and the loop comes back to the wall.

**Director's changes from the brief:** the names line reads "Claude and Tommy, CJ and Niko, Michael, Franklin and Trevor." because Ming paused ~0.8 s after every bare name (4.6–5 s for seven names, over the slot); the pairs read in 3.8 s. Also: the brief's two rows of seven give 114 px slots, too narrow for 36 px name placards. The wall is three rows (5 · 5 · 3 + one double-width slot), 164 px per slot, so every placard carries the lead's name and game at 36 px or more. The double-width fourteenth slot holds the counter during the count and later holds Lucia and Jason together, which shows "she shares GTA 6 with Jason" literally.

## Native move
The lineup wall: cream height stripes, three rows of slots, a grey silhouette per lead with a name board (name over game, 36 px). A slot fills with a drop and a **clack** plus a short white flash on the slot; the counter in the pink slot rolls up a digit per fill. The cut is the slanted pink and teal edge (shared with the other Delta films) with the next object already drawn under it.

## Frame 0
The wall already full (13 grey silhouettes, no name boards yet) drifting in a slow dolly, the pink slot with a pulsing "?", and a GTA 6 pill. Three words on screen: GTA 6, ?. A flash pops at 0.15 s and the "?" pulse is visibly moving by 0.3 s. The 13 numeral first appears with its scope (SINCE GTA III · 2001) on line 2, so the hero number is never on screen unscoped.
