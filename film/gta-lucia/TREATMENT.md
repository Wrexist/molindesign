One hard idea: **since GTA III in 2001, every lead Rockstar wrote was a man; Lucia, co-lead with Jason, ends that streak.** One stake (a 25-year streak), one contradiction (you could pick a woman in 1997, and build one in GTA Online, but neither was a lead Rockstar wrote), one hero number (25 YEARS, the only stamp, and the poster).

## Three structures
1. **Game-by-game history walk (III → V → VI):** rejected. LESSONS (gta-vicecity, gta-songs): a list across games is not one surprise, and both films stalled at 7.2.
2. **Before/after split screen (all the men left, Lucia right):** rejected. It answers the question on frame 0 and has no turn for the "but you could play a woman in 1997" viewer.
3. **The streak and the lineup (chosen):** frame 0 is the streak itself (a huge 25 over 25 tally strokes); the native move is the lineup wall that fills up with male story-lead cards and then lights its one empty slot pink. The turn (1997 select screen, GTA Online creator) answers the know-it-all before the payoff, so the payoff can be exact: FIRST WOMAN TO LEAD A 3D GTA STORY, CO-LEAD · WITH JASON, 25 YEARS.

Row three has two empty slots; on "Now Lucia" the first one lights pink and a case folder slides into it. The same wall comes back on the payoff with the 13 cards dimmed and LUCIA lit.
