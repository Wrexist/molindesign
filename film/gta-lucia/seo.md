**Title:**
GTA 6 breaks a 13-man streak. Here's who.

**Description:**
Count every GTA story lead since GTA 3 in 2001: Claude, Tommy, CJ, Mike, Toni, Vic, Niko, Johnny, Huang, Luis, Michael, Franklin and Trevor. That's 13 leads, and every single one is a man (our count, story modes only). GTA 6 changes that: Lucia Caminos shares the story with Jason Duval, and Rockstar says she's fresh out of prison. But women were playable in GTA way earlier. The very first GTA in 1997 let PC players pick from eight characters, and four were women; the choice was looks only, the story stayed the same. Since 2001, Lucia is the first woman to lead a GTA story. Lucia or Jason: who breaks it for you?
