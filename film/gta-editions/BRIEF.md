## 1. Topic and one hard idea
**GTA 6 has two US prices, $79.99 and $99.99, so the whole buying decision is one number: a $20 gap.** Both prices are printed by the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store today and match Rockstar's own June 24, 2026 announcement, so the stake needs no hedge. The film shows what that $20 does and doesn't change, using only Rockstar's own edition list: both editions get the Vintage Vice City Pack before November 20; the $20 adds Rockstar's 16 Ultimate-only items, five of them shops that are "only open for business with the Ultimate Edition"; and only Standard comes boxed (with a download code inside). It ends by handing the choice back: Standard or Ultimate.

**Hero number: $20** (derived, exact: $99.99 − $79.99 = $20.00; the Xbox Store also sells a "Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade" at exactly $20.00). The poster carries **+$20**. Everything else is secondary and quieter: 16 is a small receipt footer, five is a count of door signs, not a stamp.

**Backlog angle check:** "$79.99 / $99.99 (verify)": verified on two official stores (PlayStation Store US, Xbox Store US) plus Rockstar's press release as reported by Game Developer, Engadget and Game Informer (all 2026-06-24). The backlog hook "Which GTA 6 edition should you buy?" is 7 words; it becomes line 2 ("So which one should you buy?") behind a 5-word hook.

**Main search phrase:** `GTA 6 price` (on screen from frame 0 as the GTA 6 pill plus the two tags; "GTA 6 has two prices" spoken from 0.3 s). Secondary for SEO: `GTA 6 Ultimate Edition`, `GTA 6 editions`, `GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it`, `GTA 6 physical copy`.

**DM-able moment:** five shops in Leonida stay shut unless you bought Ultimate (Rockstar's own wording: "Only open for business with the Ultimate Edition"). Backup DM: the GTA 6 box holds a download code.
