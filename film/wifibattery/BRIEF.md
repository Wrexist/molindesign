## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**Apple says Wi-Fi and Bluetooth draw minimal power when they aren't connected and to keep them on, and on iPhone the Control Center Wi-Fi button doesn't even turn Wi-Fi off; Wi-Fi uses less battery than cellular, so check Settings › Battery instead of tapping the toggles.**

**Backlog hook softened.** "Turning off Wi-Fi won't save your battery." (7 words, house limit 6) is stronger than the sources: Apple says "minimal power", not zero, and Google tells you to turn **Bluetooth** off to stretch a dying battery. "Won't save" would overclaim. Chosen: **"Turning off Wi-Fi barely saves battery."** (6). "Barely" is our word for Apple's "minimal"; it is about Wi-Fi only, the radio both makers tell you to use (Apple: "Use Wi-Fi when you can"; Google: "Use Wi-Fi instead of mobile data"). Bluetooth is handled honestly in `bt`.

## 3b. Truth rules for the director and jury (check every frame)

1. **Strength.** "Minimal power" (Apple's word) and "barely" are the strongest claims allowed. Never "zero", "no power", "doesn't matter", "useless", "myth", "never turn off". No ✓ or SAVED/NOT SAVED stamp with a number. Neither source gives a percentage or mAh figure, so **no numbers on any battery gauge**; a battery icon may not visibly change level when the tile toggles.
2. **Apple's reason is "the best experience", not battery.** The keep-it-on advice sits in the battery section of 105105, but its stated reason is the experience. Don't print "KEEP IT ON TO SAVE BATTERY".
3. **The twist is iPhone Control Center only.** Show **iPHONE · CONTROL CENTER** with the twist. Settings › Wi-Fi really turns it off (fact 2); show that path as a small chip at `avail` or `fiveam` so the viewer knows how. Don't claim Android's Quick Settings tile behaves the same. Android's twin (fact 8: apps can scan "even when Wi-Fi is turned off" if scanning is always available; Google calls it Wi-Fi scanning under Location) may appear only as fine print: **ANDROID: "WI-FI SCANNING" IS A LOCATION SETTING · GOOGLE**, never as "Android keeps Wi-Fi on".
4. **"Available", not "using".** After the tap, Wi-Fi is available for AirDrop, Location Services and the rest; don't draw data flowing or a battery draining while it's in that state.
5. **Auto-join, not Wi-Fi.** At 5 a.m. Apple says auto-join comes back. Draw the clock bringing back the **auto-join** chip, not the Wi-Fi tile lighting up by itself.
6. **When turning radios off does help (the honest exceptions, keep on screen where noted):**
   - **Dying battery, Bluetooth:** Google (facts 5, 6) says turn off Bluetooth to stretch a low battery. Voiced in `bt`; credit **PIXEL & ANDROID HELP · TO STRETCH A DYING BATTERY**.
   - **No signal:** Apple says the phone spends battery "searching for a Wi-Fi or cellular signal" and to "enable Airplane Mode" where there's no coverage for some time (fact 3). Google: airplane mode "if you don't need your mobile network". Not voiced (it's about searching for signal, close to forceclose's "weak signal"); optional fine print at `cell`: **NO COVERAGE FOR A WHILE? APPLE: AIRPLANE MODE**.
   - **Pixel auto-on:** Google lists "Set your Wi-Fi to not turn on automatically near saved networks" under high-drain features (fact 7). That is the auto-turn-on feature, not Wi-Fi itself; leave it out of the voice, don't contradict it on screen.
   - **Low Power Mode** does not turn off Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (fact 10 lists what it limits). Don't claim the opposite.
7. **Quotes word for word, "…" at every cut.** Apple's verbs: "draw minimal power", "disconnects", "continue to be available", "uses less battery power than cellular networks". Google's: "Turn off Bluetooth", "Use Wi-Fi instead of mobile data".
8. **Re-created screens only.** The Control Center tile, Pixel Quick Settings tile and both Settings › Battery screens are re-created in the house look and tagged **RE-CREATED** ≥ 36 px. No Apple, Google or Bluetooth logos (the Bluetooth rune is a trademark; draw a plain "BT" or the word). Generic phones only.
9. **No 5G.** Apple's drain page mentions 5G Auto; leave it out (5gbattery did it).
