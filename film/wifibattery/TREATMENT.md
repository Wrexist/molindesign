## Three structures
1. **The dim that isn't off (chosen).** One giant re-created Control Center Wi-Fi button. Apple's quote and advice frame it; the viewer's finger taps it off anyway; the camera pushes in and the gray top layer peels back like a paper flap: the fan arcs are still there underneath, dashed (available, not connected, not "using"). Strings tie it to AIRDROP and LOCATION SERVICES tags while the home-network tag unhooks. A paper alarm clock reaches 5 and pulls the AUTO-JOIN tag back onto the still-dim button. Then a balance (Wi-Fi vs cellular), the honest Bluetooth exception, and Settings › Battery.
2. **The battery jar.** A glass of "battery" with taps for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, each dripping. Clear, but it needs drip rates (numbers) the sources never give, and it would draw Wi-Fi draining while off, which §3b rule 4 forbids.
3. **The switchboard.** A wall of paper switches flipped off one by one. Pretty, but it only shows switches, not the twist (the button doesn't turn Wi-Fi off), and it has no "check yours" place.

## The native move: the dim that isn't off
The Wi-Fi button is blue paper with cream fan arcs. On the tap (`dip`) a gray paper layer drops over it with a thunk, but the arcs stay faintly cut through it. On `twist` the camera pushes in 1.00 → 1.18 and the gray layer peels from its top-right edge like a flap: the cream arcs are still there underneath, drawn dashed. NOT OFF (the one coral stamp) lands with **iPHONE · CONTROL CENTER** at 52 px under it. On `avail` the dashed arcs carry the word AVAILABLE; the home-network tag's string snaps and the tag slides away (DISCONNECTED), AIRDROP and LOCATION SERVICES stay tied on. No dots flow along any string, the battery icon never moves.

## Rules for this film
- Frame 0: three words (WI-FI OFF? and RE-CREATED). The lit blue button with the fan icon fills about 450 px and rises to 600 px; nothing tapped.
- Coral only for the NOT OFF hero stamp and the dying-battery sliver. The Wi-Fi button is blue (it reads as "on"), the dim state is gray paper. YES / NO cream.
- Apple's quotes word for word with "…" at every cut; credits say which Apple page. Google's credit says PIXEL & ANDROID HELP · TO STRETCH A DYING BATTERY.
- Scope: the twist carries iPHONE · CONTROL CENTER in big type; the real off switch is shown as fine print FULLY OFF: SETTINGS › WI-FI. Nothing says Android behaves the same.
- After the tap, Wi-Fi is "available": dashed arcs, strings to AIRDROP and LOCATION SERVICES, no flowing dots, no draining battery.
- At 5 a.m. the clock brings back the AUTO-JOIN tag; the button stays dim. A 5:00 AM card with OR A RESTART under it (the voice says "5 in the morning": Whisper heard "5 a.m. And" as "5am. M" through the mix).
- No numbers on any battery; the small battery icon never changes level when a button toggles. The dying battery is the premise of `bt`, drained on "dead?", before the tap.
- Re-created screens tagged RE-CREATED ≥ 30 px; no Apple, Google or Bluetooth logos; generic shapes only.
- Props whole inside x 92–928, y 302–1428 except during camera moves; the wallpaper bleeds full width; lower third filled every second.
- SFX only in the written gaps (line end + 0.3 s, margin 0.2), gated ≥ 3 dB under the voice from `vo/heard.js`: tick (hook: the search card lands), thump (dip: the gray layer drops), slap (twist: NOT OFF re-pressed), tick (fiveam: the clock's hammer, no ring), pop (cell: the tower pan lands), click (bt: the Bluetooth pill), click (check: the Battery row).
