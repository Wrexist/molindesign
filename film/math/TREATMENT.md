2. **Commas first** ("Commas make AI better at math"): the best DM fact, but dated (GPT-3.5, 2023) and a puzzle without a stake on frame 0 (BRIEF §2, hook 3).
3. **Error gallery** (wrong answers screenshotted): rejected, the brief has no current failure rate and shows no wrong answer (BRIEF §3, kept out).

## Native move
