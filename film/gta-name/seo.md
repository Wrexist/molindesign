## Description
GTA is a real crime. Grand theft is the more serious kind of theft, and California's Penal Code lists an automobile by name (section 487(d)(1)). Lawyers call that charge grand theft auto.

But the game wasn't called that at first. DMA Design's founder Dave Jones says the original name was Race'n'Chase, the design they pitched to BMG, and the name changed soon after the publisher came on board. It came out in 1997 as Grand Theft Auto, and GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI, November 19, 2026) still carries the charge.

Better name: Race'n'Chase, or GTA?
