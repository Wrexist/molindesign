## 1. Topic and one hard idea
**In GTA 6's swamp, Rockstar itself says the gators are not the deadliest thing.** Rockstar's own Grassrivers text: "The gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators and weirder discoveries among the mangroves." That is the whole stake, in Rockstar's words, with no hedge. Rockstar does **not** say what those predators are, so the film never names one as fact; it says "Rockstar won't say", quotes "you never know what lies beneath", backs up "don't swim" with Rockstar's own warning about the Keys' "dangerous waters", gives **one** labeled guess (THEORY · OUR GUESS: the real Everglades hides invasive Burmese pythons, per the National Park Service), and ends on a question the picture does not answer.

**Lead with the surprise, not the context** (LESSONS gta-editions): the stake is spoken by 2.4 s and the paper gator plus the big unclear shape under the water are on frame 0. No "GTA 6 has a swamp called…" set-up line.

**Backlog angle check:** "Everglades-style wildlife; only confirmed items." Confirmed by Rockstar: gators (named in the Grassrivers text; Rockstar's own screenshot alt texts also show alligators and airboats in the Grassrivers) and mangroves.
