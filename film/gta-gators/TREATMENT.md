# Don't swim · treatment (Delta #14)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-editions/** (Delta #11, held at 6.8–7.0): learn its engine (voice-first `at(id, word)` keying, the VAD gate in `vo/vad.js`, `fontStretch` re-applied after every font change, the hand-made paper props juries finally called hand-made). Don't take its story shape: a stake everyone already knew on frame 0 ("two prices") and four separate sets (window, printer, street, window) that each had to be re-introduced.
- **film/qrcode/** (Tech, explained, passed at 8.0): learn that one physical prop that keeps changing state carries a whole film. Don't take its single static camera: here the camera itself (push, dip, slide, pull back) is the change every ≤ 1.2 s.

## Three structures
1. **Map card + quote cards**: Grassrivers on a map, then quote slips. Rejected: gta-leonida already put this exact quote on a map card, and cards on a flat background are the dashboard look that held five Delta films.
2. **Monster countdown** ("5 things deadlier than gators"): rejected outright: Rockstar names none, so every item would be a rumor stated as a list.
3. **One paper diorama, one camera** (chosen): a hand-built cut-paper swamp in a cardboard box lives the whole film. The camera pushes toward the water, dips through the vellum water line, slides along the box's paper track to a second panel (the Keys), and pulls back out of the box onto the workbench for the one guess, so "real world" literally sits outside the game box. It returns to frame 0 for the loop.

## The native move
**The dip.** On "beneath" the camera drops straight down through the vellum water line (one axis, no radial sweep): the gator's paper legs, the root ends and the huge soft shape under the water get bigger and blurrier, and it never resolves into an animal. A muffled swell and bubbles play in the written pause after "beneath."

## Rules this cut is built to pass (from LESSONS)
- Frame 0: DON'T SWIM sign + GTA 6 label = 4 words, fully printed; gator and shape already moving; nothing rolls.
- One hero stamp (NOT THE DEADLIEST), beside the gator, never on it or on the quote strip. Everything else is a tag, chip or strip.
- Quotes exactly as Rockstar prints them (mixed case, curly ’, the em dash, … at every cut), no highlighting inside quotes, a source chip under each.
- THEORY · OUR GUESS on a field card that sits on the workbench outside the game box; the shape in the box stays unresolved.
- Everything clipped to the box x 90–930, y 300–1430, so a push or dolly can never cross the rail; pixel-scanned on every rendered frame.
- Sound only in written pauses, gated on `vo/vad.js`; each hit ≥ 3 dB under its neighbouring words, measured on the final mix.
