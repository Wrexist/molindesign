# Treatment: GTA 6 cops remember 4 things (gta-wanted)

## Benchmarks
- **gta-thingstodo (Delta, trailer mode).** Learn: every countdown item is proved by a short real clip inside 2 s, a ring on the exact thing, phrase captions at 96 px, one native move repeated from a different place each time. Don't take: the pink to-do grid and its slot-to-plate geometry (this film needs its own set), nine near-identical beats.
- **gta-storylength remake (Delta, passed 7.3).** Learn: a hand-built prop the whole film hangs on (stopwatch, punch card) reads as made by hand and carries the loop. Don't take: an all-graphics film; Isac asked today for real GTA footage playing behind the graphics (SAFE-EDIT rule 11).

## Three structures
1. **The HUD lifted out (chosen).** Rockstar's own chase plays; a magnifier pulls the real wanted HUD out of the corner and its four icons fly down into our own icon tray. Countdown 4 → 1, each tile flies up out of the real HUD again with its number. Then Rockstar's fix: the stars turn red, Lucia leaves the car and the real car icon blinks out in the same frame our car tile cracks and falls; she burns the car; three tiles left.
2. A "how to lose the cops" tutorial (steps 1-2-3). Takeaway is clear, but it claims a full escape the video never shows (the stars never clear). Dropped for Truth.
3. A myth card first ("GTA 5 cops forgot you") then the HUD. Spends the hook on a game the viewer isn't playing and needs Rob Nelson's paraphrase on screen, which the brief forbids.

## Native move
**HUD → tray.** The real icons are about 17 px at phone size, so every proof beat opens a magnifier straight over the HUD corner of the clip (a sharp HUD crop of the same source range, cut at 2× with clipkit), rings the icon, and our own big cut-paper disc of that icon flies out of the lens into a four-slot tray under the clip. The tray is the film's memory: four discs on 2.0 s, three at the end. The payoff runs the move backwards: the lens shows the real car icon vanish (source 957.07 s, frame-checked) and our car disc cracks and drops out of the tray on that frame.

## Frame 0 and the loop
Frame 0: Rockstar's chase full-bleed (Jason firing from the car, four white stars, 851.7 s) under `COPS REMEMBER 4 THINGS` with the GTA 6 pill; the plate shrinks into the clip card at 0.25 s, the HUD magnifier opens by 0.9 s and the four icons land in the tray by 2.0 s. The last 0.55 s: the van card grows back to full-bleed and cuts to the same chase frame (from 851.7 s at DUR), the tray drops away, the hook words slam back, so the last frame is frame 0. "Now they only remember three" hands straight into "four things".
