## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **18.13-18.71 s, the "red" line (Truth).** "Lose them, and the stars turn red" overclaims and is circular after "how do you shake them?". Re-voice it as "Get out of sight, and the stars turn red." (the brief's line, 9 words; it fits the 18.13-20.9 slot at speed 1.0-1.05). Change the 18.1-18.6 headline from LOSE THEM to OUT OF SIGHT. Optionally add a 0.4 s white-blink on our star row before it turns red, with a 34 px chip FLASHING = LOST SIGHT OF YOU · GAMESPOT, so the white → flashing → red ladder is honest.
2. **11.8-13.5 s, the chip MASK WAS ON DURING THE CRIME (Truth).** This is an unsourced story claim. Replace it with MASK ON IN THIS SHOT (same 34 px mono). Keep the red ring on the bandana and STILL LIT HERE on the icon.
3. **1.9-2.4 s, the star row over the source credit (Craft, LESSONS repeat).** Move the six-star row to y ≥ credit bottom + 16 px, or slide the ROCKSTAR · AN EXTENDED LOOK tag up 70 px before the stars land. Scan 1.5-3.0 s at 0.1 s steps afterwards to confirm no overlap.
4. **23.3 s, CAR ICON: GONE prints 1.0 s before "gone" (Clarity, LESSONS repeat).** Hold the BEFORE/NOW split without the stamp, and slam the stamp on 24.28 s (the word "gone") with the hit, or keep the hit on the vanish (22.9 s) and stamp on "gone". The picture can show the vanish early; the words go on with the voice.
5. **0.0 s and 29.2 s, the pink translucent rounded box at bottom-left (x 0-110, y ~1570-1870) (Hook/Craft).** It looks like a stray layer, probably the CLIP card's edge. Remove it from the frame-0/end composition. It is on the most-seen still of the film.
6. **25.0-26.9 s, the burn beat (Clarity/Craft).** Four chips sit on the fire. Drop the second CAR ICON: GONE (already said) and move ALSO: WEAPON · CCTV ICONS / SOURCE: DEXERTO to the loop beat (27-28.5 s, where it already is) only. Keep MOLOTOV and let the flame fill the card for 0.4 s with nothing on it.
7. **22.9 s, the vanish flash washes the lens grey (Shareability, wow).** Make it the poster frame. Hold the clean lens for 0.15 s with the car icon visibly gone *before* the flash. Then have our car disc fall through the tray and burst on y 1440 into 6-8 paper shards that tumble into the empty bottom band (y 1450-1700) and settle there until the burn. The empty band gets a job, and the screenshot frame carries both the real HUD and our broken disc.
8. **0.0-2.0 s, the search phrase (Hook).** The GTA 6 WANTED LEVEL chip arrives at ~2.0 s at ~20 px. Put it on frame 0 at ≥ 34 px under the headline (it does not count toward the 5 hook words as a chip, but keep it in a smaller weight).
