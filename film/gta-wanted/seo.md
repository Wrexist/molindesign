## Description
GTA 6's wanted level isn't just stars. Under them, icons show what the cops know about you, and in Rockstar's own getaway chase four are lit: that there are two of you, your clothes, what you look like, and your car. Masks on, the description icon is still lit. To shake one: get out of sight and the stars turn red (red means they don't know where you are), then leave the car. Its icon disappears the moment Lucia steps out, and she burns it. The cops still remember the other three. There are also weapon and CCTV icons, not lit in this chase.

How to lose the cops in GTA 6, read straight off the real HUD.
