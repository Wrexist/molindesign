## 1. Topic and one hard idea
GTA 6's wanted HUD shows what the cops know about you as icons under the stars; in Rockstar's own getaway four are lit (two of you, clothes, description, car), and the film counts them down 4 → 1 on the real HUD, then shows Rockstar's fix knocking one out.
**Native move:** the **icon tray**. Four of our own big icon tiles (drawn in our style, matching the real icons' shapes: two heads, hanger, head-and-shoulders, car) sit in a row from 2.0 s. Each countdown item flies its tile up out of the real HUD (zoom from the clip's top-right corner, 1:1 to our tile), stamps its number, and sits back in the tray. In the payoff the car tile cracks and drops out of the tray in sync with the real icon vanishing in the clip; the last frame is the tray with three tiles, which loops into frame 0's four.
