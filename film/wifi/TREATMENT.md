# Treatment · wifi ("How Wi-Fi goes through walls")

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from agent (7.4, Truth 9.0) and phonephoto (first cut 7.5):** one hand-made prop carries the whole film and the mechanism is shown on the prop itself. Here the prop is the folded paper wave ribbon: its height is how much signal is left, its fold count is the frequency.
- **Learn from qrcode / battery (8.0):** a frame the viewer can act on (the floor plan with four placement labels) held still long enough to screenshot.
- **Don't take from phonephoto r2 / password / gpus / agent r1:** the hero leaving on a zoom; six coral stamps; a frame 0 that barely moves (agent r1 measured a ±4 px wiggle); labels sitting on the prop they name. Here: one coral stamp (SAME BAND), the ribbon glides ≥ 300 px over the first second, every label is measured against every prop box on the rendered frames, and the plan leaves on the torn edge.

## Three structures
1. **The ribbon's journey (chosen):** contradiction on your own wall → the camera pans back along the ribbon to the router → it is radio, 2.4 billion waves a second → the lab rack (most materials let it through) → same wall, tighter 5 GHz ribbon comes out narrower → foil stops it, a fish tank thins it → the twist: the oven card and the router card, SAME BAND → the floor plan, what to do → the cabinet question → frame 0. One prop in every scene.
2. **Microwave first** ("Your microwave shares your Wi-Fi's band"): a stronger stake but it overclaims as an opener (BRIEF §2, hook 3), and the film would be about one appliance.
3. **A signal-bar countdown** (bars dropping per wall): rejected, it needs dB numbers the sources don't give for 2.4 GHz (BRIEF §3, kept out).

## Native move
The ribbon. A strip of card stock folded accordion-style; every material it passes leaves it narrower, foil crumples it, water tints it. Fold spacing is the frequency (5 GHz folded twice as tight). Scene changes are the house torn edge along one axis, the next scene drawn under it; the first change is a camera pan back along the ribbon.
