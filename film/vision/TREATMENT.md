# Treatment: Your photo? Claude shrinks it first.

Factory #20 · `vision` · voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), every beat keyed to a word with `at(id, word)`.

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from:** stepbystep (7.2 → 7.4, Truth 9.0) and incognito (7.5, 914 views in 11 h). The hook is the source's own claim ("downscaled before processing"); one mechanism from hook to end (photo → squares → tokens → a cap → shrunk first → small text suffers); the hero hold sits in a planned silence with its own caption; the end card answers nothing. Incognito's numbers say a practical "this happens to *your* thing" stake beats an abstract mechanism, so the hero prop is the viewer's own photo.
- **Don't take:** math (an AI-capability claim not scoped to one model: here every number carries CLAUDE and the hero carries CLAUDE 4.7 AND LATER), wifi (frozen stretch before the stamp, empty stage under a wipe, coral on wipes), voiceclone r2 / gta-lucia (props over the rail that boundscheck can't see: every scene here is clipped to the safe box x 90–930, y 300–1430 and the rendered frames are pixel-scanned).

## Three structures
1. **One print on one desk (chosen).** The paper photo print is the only hero: it shrinks inside a dashed outline of its old size, the grid knife cuts it into squares, one square lifts and flips to 1 TOKEN, it joins typed word tiles on one tally tape that runs into a gauge with a hard stop, and the gridded print returns for the hero (79 × 60 = 4,740) with the coral stamp. A torn edge opens the receipt close-up (the consequence and the tip). The end card is frame 0's full-size print again.
2. **Two photos side by side** (a 12 MP photo and its shrunk copy). Clear ratio, but it reads as before/after of two different photos and splits the eye; the dashed outline does the same job on one object.
3. **The receipt first** (open on unreadable fine print, explain backwards). A stronger puzzle, but frame 0 would show the consequence, not the stake, and "your photo" would land after 10 s.

## Native move
**The grid knife**: a paper ruler with 28-px teeth that slides across the print and leaves the square grid behind it. It is the mechanism itself (patches), it reads with the sound off, and it returns on the receipt close-up so the viewer sees the same squares fall across the fine print.

## Motion personality
Calm, paper-on-a-desk: springs at ζ ≥ 0.85, slides along one axis, no zooms out of the hero, nothing bounces. One heavy sound (the stamp thump); the rest are paper (slide, snick, needle knock, pop).
