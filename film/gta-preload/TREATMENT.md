# Treatment: "GTA 6 Needs Up To 212 GB" (Delta, gta-preload)

One hard idea: **GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB, and even the boxed copy is just a download code, so everyone has to make room before November 19.** One hero number: 212 GB.

## Benchmarks
| | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| Phone-storage "will it fit" Shorts (the storage-full dialog with a bar) | One instrument everyone already reads at a glance: a storage bar with a free gap. The viewer checks their own console in their head. | The screenshot of a real settings screen (reads as a real console UI and as a claim). Ours is a cut-paper card tagged RE-CREATED. |
| Unboxing Shorts that open an empty box | The lid flip in a pause is the reveal; the empty space carries the joke. | Real box art and logos. Our box is a plain ink box with our own GTA 6 type, no cover art. |

## Three structures
1. **Brick vs bar (chosen).** One paper brick printed 212 GB and one cardboard storage bar that stay in every scene. The brick tries the gap, is too wide, the box turns out to hold only a code that becomes the same brick, then blocks pop out and the brick fits. The loop returns to the brick over a full bar.
2. Countdown calendar from Nov 10 to Nov 19 with tasks on each day. Rejected: dates in the present tense break the date-proofing, and a list reads as a template.
3. Two consoles side by side (PS5 / Xbox), each filling. Rejected: Xbox size and unlock time are not published, and two heroes split the beat.

## Native move
The storage bar: a long hand-cut cardboard tube with grey "other games" blocks and a teal free gap. Every space beat pushes, jams, pops or fills it with a paper sound.
