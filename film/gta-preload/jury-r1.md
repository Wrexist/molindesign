## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **14.5-17.9 s: NO DISC (PS5) beside BOTH CONSOLES · ROCKSTAR (Truth and Clarity).** The orange chip lands at ~14.5 s, before NO DISC (~15.3 s), and both stay up until ~17.9 s. Change the order:
   - NO DISC and its PS5 credit stamp in the "code" pause (~15.0 s).
   - They peel off under a swipe at 15.9 s, on "So".
   - Only then does CODE-IN-BOX · BOTH CONSOLES · ROCKSTAR land, at ≥ 16.1 s, and it leaves by 17.6 s.
   - The two chips never share a frame. Re-check every frame from 14.0 to 18.5 s.
2. **10.4 s, voice "Bought the box instead?" (Truth).** The box is on sale from Nov 12, so for a Nov 10-11 post change the line to **"Getting the box instead? Bad news."** It stays true on any date and still covers pre-orderers. Keep the say-hint so Whisper hears "Getting". Caption: GETTING THE / BOX INSTEAD?
3. **0.0-1.5 s: frame 0 is a six-token title card (Hook, capped at 6).**
   - Frame 0 = `UP TO 212 GB` only (4 tokens). The `GTA 6` pill slams in at 0.25-0.45 s, under the spoken "G T A six", and is readable well before 2 s.
   - Make frame 0 a jam, not a label. The brick (keep it ≥ 700 px wide) is already moving left at frame 0 and has its nose pushed into the bar's 170 px teal gap.
   - The gap visibly bulges and the grey blocks squeeze 20-30 px, with motion blur on the brick.
   - Thud at the first contact (~0.35 s), raised from -16.9 to about -9 dBFS.
   - Recount tokens after the fix.
4. **13.2-15.9 s: the box reveal, "show the empty" (wow, Shareability).** Today the slip covers the opening and the box is a cube that reads as a mystery box. Fix it in four steps:
   - Redraw the box as a flat game case, 520x660 px, tipped 25° toward camera so the inside is visible: a dark, empty tray at least 300x380 px.
   - The single slip pops out alone (paper flutter SFX).
   - The camera does a 1.15x push into the empty tray for 0.6 s.
   - The DOWNLOAD CODE slip widens to 560 px with 28 px padding, so XXXX-XXXX-XXXX is not cut on either edge.
   This is the frame people send to the friend who pre-ordered the box, and the poster candidate. Put the poster here, after the stamp lands and with the caption gone.
5. **Whole film: dead air (Retention).** 34 % of the runtime is silence, with 0.65-1.13 s after nearly every line.
   - Keep the two written 0.5 s pauses (after "gigs", after "code").
   - Close every other gap to 0.30-0.40 s: open 3.5 → 3.2, fit → 4.6, check → 6.9, and so on.
   - Target dur ≈ 21.5 s at ~2.7 written words/s.
   - Re-time all picture events and re-run the in-mix Whisper and VAD gate.
6. **0.0-3.5 s and 22.0-24.4 s: one composition held 3+ s.** After fix 3, add events:
   - 1.2 s: the brick bounces back off the gap; FREE flashes teal.
   - 2.4 s: the underline swipes; the grey blocks rattle.
   - End: YES/NO chips land at 22.6 s. Then at 23.6 s the bar's free gap pulses on "yours", and at 24.1 s the brick nudges toward the gap again. No dead 1.5 s.
7. **5.5-8.1 s: the "fit" beat never jams (Clarity, Craft).** The brick shrinks to ~280 px and hovers above the gap.
   - Keep the brick big and pull the camera out instead (0.85x for 0.4 s), so 212 GB stays the giant.
   - Drop it into the gap tilted 8°, overlapping both neighbouring blocks by ≥ 40 px.
   - Add a crunch at -9 dBFS in the 4.93-5.6 s gap.
8. **10.4-10.9 s and 21.0-21.7 s: carry-overs (Craft; the "every wipe ends before the next card reveals" lesson).**
   - Hold the BOUGHT/GETTING caption until the page wipe has fully cleared the settings card and its PS5 chip (≥ 10.85 s).
   - Clear the NOV 19 calendar off the end brick before WILL GTA 6 FIT ON YOURS? shows.
   - Also shorten the 24.5-25.1 s cream wipe to ≤ 0.3 s, so the loop doesn't pass through a blank page.
9. **All scenes: generic backdrop and an empty bottom 27 % (Craft).** The synthwave sunset with palm silhouettes is the stock "Vice City" look, and LESSONS gta-longestwait already moved off it to a paper room.
   - Put the bar inside a hand-cut generic console on a paper TV bench, with the console body and a controller filling y 1400-1900 (shapes only; text stays above y 1450 and left of x 930).
   - Vary the composition per scene: brick right / box left / calendar top-left, not centred every time.
10. **Settings card at 8.2-10.4 s.** The FREE SPACE widget (grey bar plus pink-ringed teal square) reads as a toggle switch. Draw it as a horizontal usage bar in the same style as the hero bar, so the two rhyme.
