## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB, and even the boxed copy is just a download code, so everyone has to make room before November 19.** One hero number: **212 GB**.

Search phrases: `GTA 6 size` / `GTA 6 212 GB` (spoken from 0.2 s), `GTA 6 pre-load` (spoken by ~5 s). The director shows `GTA 6 · 212 GB` on frame 0 and `PRE-LOAD` on line 2; the SEO agent captions both.

DM-able moment: `box` → `code`. The box you buy at the store has no game in it, only a code (drawn as an opened box with a single slip of paper). People send this to the friend who pre-ordered the boxed copy.

## 2. Hook options
1. **"GTA 6 needs up to 212 gigs."** **Chosen.** 6 words, GTA in word 1, one big number, a threat about the viewer's own console. "Up to" is Rockstar's own scope ("up to 212 GB depending on your device"); without it the line would overstate on Xbox.
2. "GTA 6's box has no disc." Strongest myth-break and very shareable, but the no-disc news has been out since June 2026 (VGC, June 24), and PlayStation's page is the only primary source that says "disc" in so many words (PS5). Kept as the mid-film twist, voiced as "just a download code" (Rockstar's own "code-in-box", both consoles).
3. "Will GTA 6 fit on your PS5?" A test, but it drops Xbox owners and has no number in the first second.

Frame 0 (≤ 6 words, GTA first): `GTA 6` pill, huge **212 GB** paper block already sliding toward a console's storage bar, small `UP TO` above 212 (GTA, 6, UP, TO, 212, GB = 6 tokens). Recount after every fix (LESSONS gta-editions r2).

## 6. Left out, and why
- **Exact Xbox size and an Xbox unlock time:** Rockstar says "up to 212 GB depending on your device"; neither Rockstar nor Xbox publishes a separate Xbox figure or an unlock hour that I could read. The voice says "up to", the PS5's 212 GB and midnight unlock appear only as PS5-scoped chips.
- **How to start pre-load on PS5 (rest mode, auto-download checkbox):** primary and useful, but a second idea and PS5-only. Good material for a follow-up Short ("Your PS5 won't pre-load GTA 6 unless…").
- **"Pre-load needs a pre-order":** PlayStation's steps assume a pre-order; I found no single primary sentence covering both consoles. Not claimed.
- **Comparison with drive sizes** (825 GB launch PS5, 1 TB Slim, 512 GB Series S): models differ and usable space isn't on a primary page; any "a quarter of your drive" line would need scoping on three models.
- **Disc version later:** unconfirmed, contradicted. Out.
- **Final install size after a day-one patch:** unknown. The film never says the game "is" 212 GB, only that it "needs up to" that for pre-load.
