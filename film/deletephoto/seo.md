Google Photos too. Google Photos Help, "Delete photos & videos": "Photos and videos that you delete will stay in your trash for 30 days before they are permanently deleted." And: "If you delete a photo or video that's backed up in Google Photos, it will stay in your trash for 30 days."

A friend's copy. Apple Support, "How to use Shared Albums in Photos on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro" (published Oct 6, 2026): "Any photos that you saved or downloaded from a shared album to your Photos library stay in your library even if the shared album is deleted or the person who created it stops sharing it."

Google: "When you empty your trash, you permanently delete any photos and videos in your trash, and they can't be restored."

Deleted one by accident? Apple: "If you accidentally deleted a photo or video, you can recover it within 30 days in the Recently Deleted album." Google: "You can only restore photos and videos if they're still in your trash."

So, is yours empty? Yes or no.
