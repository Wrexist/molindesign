> Honest version: it only waits if the setting is on and has learned your routine (about 14 days), and heat still counts, so keep it out from under the pillow. Is your phone set to wait? Yes or no.

**Ready replies for the first hour:**
- **If:** So overnight charging is totally fine?
  **Reply:** Not always. With the setting off, the phone can sit at 100% for hours, and Samsung US says to unplug "after the device has reached a 100% charge to avoid a swollen battery." With the setting on and the phone kept cool, the phone handles the long hours at full.
- **If:** Mine went straight to 100% last night.
  **Reply:** That can happen. Apple says it delays past 80 percent "in certain situations" and needs at least 14 days and 9 long charges in one place to learn; Pixel says Adaptive Charging "may turn on". Check that the setting is on, and give it a couple of weeks of the same routine.
- **If:** Should I just set it to stop at 80%?
  **Reply:** That's your call. iPhone 15 and later can set a charge limit from 80 to 100 percent, Pixel 6a and later have Limit to 80%, and Galaxy has Battery protection options. The trade is less battery for the day in return for less time at full.
- **If:** Is wireless charging worse?
  **Reply:** Different topic, and we didn't cover it here. The same rule from Apple applies though: if the phone gets hot while it charges, take it out of its case and keep it somewhere with air around it.
