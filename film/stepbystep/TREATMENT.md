# Treatment: Why "think step by step" helps AI

Factory #19 · `stepbystep` · voice first (Ming US_James, seed 7), every beat keyed to a word with `at(id, word)`.

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from:** voiceclone (7.1 → 7.3 → 7.5, Truth 9.0) and incognito (7.5). The hook is the source's own sentence; one mechanism runs from hook to end (the steps become part of the text the answer is guessed from); every number keeps its model and test on screen; the hero hold sits in a planned silence with its own caption; the end card answers nothing.
- **Don't take:** math (parked: hook and body explained different things, and an AI-capability claim wasn't scoped), wifi (frozen stretch before the stamp, empty stage under a wipe, dead tail, coral on wipes), voiceclone r2 (a prop group crossing x 930 that boundscheck couldn't see).

## Three structures
1. **The answer strip (chosen).** One paper machine on one desk: the paper's own puzzle, the box that prints 8 when asked straight, then prints three step tabs and 4 with the five words, and the tabs fed back into its intake. The order-swap beat re-uses the same strip with the answer printed first. The newer box prints its own tabs. The hook's five tiles come back as the end card.
2. **Bars first.** Open on 17.7% → 78.7% and explain backwards. The number is the hook, but frame 0 would show the answer, and the puzzle (the human stake) would land after 10 s.
3. **Two chats side by side.** A re-created chat with and without the five words. It reads as a real UI (not allowed) and splits the mechanism across two screens.

## Native move
The answer strip: a cream receipt the box prints, one torn tab per step, with the ANSWER row printed last. It is the paper's two-stage pipeline ("[X0] [Z] [A]", §3.1) drawn as paper.
