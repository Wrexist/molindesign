## Description
GTA 6 has iguanas. On Rockstar's Only in Leonida page, one screenshot from the Leonida Keys is captioned, in Rockstar's own words: "An iguana walks in front of a mobility scooter in the Leonida Keys as people pass by in the background." The iguana is right there on the asphalt, bottom left, while a woman rides past on a blue mobility scooter.

Just a cute background detail? In real Florida, green iguanas are not native and are considered an invasive species, says the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). On January 21, 2020, the National Weather Service in Miami posted: "This isn't something we usually forecast, but don't be surprised if you see Iguanas falling from the trees tonight as lows drop into the 30s and 40s." And last winter, during a cold snap on February 1 and 2, 2026, people brought 5,195 cold-stunned green iguanas to FWC drop-off sites (Sunrise 3,882, Tequesta 1,075, Marathon 215, Fort Myers 23).
