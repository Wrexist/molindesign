## Three structures
1. **A photo print with a caption on its back (chosen).** Rockstar's screenshot is a paper photo print. It flips over to show Rockstar's own caption (the alt text) written on the back, flips back for the street beat, and returns for the vote, so the loop seam is the same print. The real-Florida half is a field guide: cream paper, our cut-paper iguana with ID leaders, a palm, a thermometer, tally bars.
2. A browser page re-created (Only in Leonida) with the screenshot in its slot. Rejected: a re-created web page is a template look and needs RE-CREATED tags over Rockstar's own picture.
3. The trailer iguana. Dropped by the orchestrator: both edges of that shot belong to the published gta-everytrailer (SAFE-EDIT rule 7, no shot in two films).

## Native move
**The paper iguana.** One hand-built cut-paper iguana (spikes, throat fan, ringed tail, the marks FWC lists) is the film's own creature: it walks across the dip, stands for the OUR ID leaders, tips off a palm when the forecast says "falling", and topples with three more when the cold snap stuns them. The photo-print flip is the second move (front = Rockstar, back = Rockstar's words).
