## Description
Before GTA 6, there was a GTA that almost never happened.

The first Grand Theft Auto began at DMA Design in 1995 as Race'n'Chase, a cops-and-robbers driving game. Development went badly. DMA's Gary Penn said the publisher, BMG Interactive, wanted to can it, the game crashed all the time and the car handling was appalling.

Then, in Penn's words: "One day, I think it was a bug, the police suddenly became mental and aggressive." The cops tried to ram you off the road. "That was awesome, so that stayed in."

GTA came out in 1997. GTA 6 arrives November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Would GTA be the same without psycho cops? Yes or no?
