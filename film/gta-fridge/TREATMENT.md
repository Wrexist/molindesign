# Treatment: GTA 6 has a fridge menu

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from — gta-trunkstream (Delta, jury r2 7.2 / Truth 8.5):** official footage on a big framed card as evidence, our readings as stickers outside the footage, a ring on each thing the voice names, a run-on end line into the hook. **Don't take:** its crude hand-drawn people and cars (Craft 6.0) and a 520 px hero phone. Here the footage itself is the hero; the only drawn object is a fridge, a shape that can be drawn cleanly.
- **Learn from — "GTA 6 has jail time" style detail Shorts (VIDEO-PLAN format 5, 551k):** one feature, shown, in the first second, then the list of what's in it. **Don't take:** the uncommented clip run and the music bed. Every excerpt here is under 5 s, muted, annotated, and there is no music.

## Three structures
1. **Walkthrough in order** (walk up → prompt → open → list → drink): honest, but frame 0 would be a man's back in a hallway. Fails the frame-0 rule.
2. **List countdown** ("5 drinks in a GTA 6 fridge"): punchy, but we can't claim five (the list fades at its edge) and countdowns drag.
3. **Hook on the menu, then rewind** — **chosen.** Frame 0 is the open fridge with the list already on it (the payoff in the hook frame), then "walk up … open it" replays how you get there, then the list is read row by row, the one mystery (icons only some rows get), one brand detail (Fleeca magnet), the DRINK / DROP prompt, and a vote that loops into frame 0.

## The native move
**The fridge door.** Scene changes are a cream fridge door swinging shut across the frame and open again on the next beat (hinged on the right edge, eased, no bounce). The same door opens on "Open it", so the transition *is* the feature.

## Look
Deep berry ink (#1d0e1f) with the apartment's pink as the accent, cream tags with ink type, sun-yellow rings for our marks. The footage sits on one big framed card (840 px wide) per beat; our redrawn list sits on a dark "menu" panel, in big condensed type (≥ 56 px), with our redrawn icons in the source's colours (teal bolt ring, purple eye ring; the shake row: pink two-lobe ring + teal bolt). Plates from the same apartment (dimmed 55 %, blurred) sit behind our panels.
