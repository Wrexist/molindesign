## Description
GTA 6 has a fridge menu. In Rockstar's An Extended Look (at 7:15), a man walks through a pink apartment's kitchen to a fridge covered in magnets, photos and sticky notes, and the button prompt just says FRIDGE. Open it, and a list comes up over the shelves: Logger Beer, Lavazas Beer, Blitz Berry Smoothie, Blitz Green Smoothie and Protein Shake. The beers get no icons; the smoothies and the shake each get two small round icons, and Rockstar hasn't said what they do. A Fleeca Bank magnet is even on the fridge door, and once he's holding a bottle, the prompts read Drink (R1) and Drop. Rockstar says the Extended Look was captured entirely from in-game footage on the PlayStation 5.
