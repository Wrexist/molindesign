The NSA says restart your phone weekly. Its Mobile Device Best Practices sheet says: "Power the device off and on weekly." So should you restart your phone every week?
A restart can wipe out some spyware, but the NSA's own chart says a weekly restart "Sometimes prevents" spearphishing and zero-click exploits. A restart doesn't close the hole; an update can. So update, and restart weekly. On a Samsung Galaxy phone you can set it on a schedule: Settings › Device care › Auto optimization › Auto restart › Restart on schedule.

The NSA sheet ("Mobile Device Best Practices", U/OO/155488-20 | PP-20-0622 | Oct 2020 rev 1.1), page 1: "Power the device off and on weekly." and "Update the device software and applications as soon as possible." Page 2, the chart "WHAT CAN I DO TO PREVENT/MITIGATE?": the column "Turn Device Off & On Weekly" is marked "Sometimes prevents" for "Spearphishing (To install Malware)" and "Zero-Click Exploits"; every other row in that column is "Does not prevent". The chart's three marks are "Does not prevent (no icon)", "Sometimes prevents" and "Almost always prevents".

Why a restart can help: in one 2023 iPhone spyware case, Kaspersky's "Operation Triangulation" report found "a successful zero-click compromise via an iMessage attachment" and said: "The malicious toolset does not support persistence, most likely due to the limitations of the OS. The timelines of multiple devices indicate that they may be reinfected after rebooting." So a reboot can wipe it out, and those iPhones may get infected again.

Why the update matters: Apple's iOS 16.5.1 (released June 21, 2023) fixed a kernel flaw, CVE-2023-32434, credited to researchers at Kaspersky. Apple: "Apple is aware of a report that this issue may have been actively exploited against versions of iOS released before iOS 15.7." That is one flaw in one case, not every attack.

Check yours. Samsung US Support: "Tap Auto optimization. Tap Auto restart." "Enable Restart on schedule. Your device will restart automatically on your selected date and time." "Auto restart only runs when the device is not being used. By default, the screen must be off and the battery level must be at least 30%."

Fine print: A restart can help; it doesn't stop every attack, and the NSA lists it as one practice among many. The Kaspersky quotes describe one 2023 iPhone case, not all spyware or Android. Samsung: "Menu names, settings, and screen layouts may vary depending on the model and software version." The film makes no claim about scheduled restarts on iPhone or Pixel.
