## Description
GTA 5 came out before the PS4. It launched September 17, 2013 on PS3 and Xbox 360; the PS4 launched in the US and Canada on November 15, 2013.

Then it came out again: PS4 and Xbox One on November 18, 2014. PC players waited while the date kept slipping (January 27, then March 24), until April 14, 2015. Then PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, digitally, on March 15, 2022. Three console generations, one game.

GTA 6 starts on just one: Rockstar announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, for November 19, 2026.
