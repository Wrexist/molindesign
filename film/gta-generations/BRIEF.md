**Truth-proofing (read first).**
- **Hook scope.** "GTA 5 came before the PS4" = the *game* launched Sept 17 2013 (PS3 / Xbox 360), the PS4 launched Nov 15 2013 in the US and Canada (Sony). 59 days, derived. It never says GTA 5's PS4 version is older than the PS4. Frame 0 shows the dates under the caption so the claim is checkable sound-off.
- **"Three console generations"** is a count of the console families GTA V has launched on: PS3/Xbox 360 → PS4/Xbox One (Take-Two's own Oct 29 2013 release calls them "the next-generation consoles coming in November") → PS5/Xbox Series X|S. GamesRadar (Sept 17 2026) uses the same framing: "13 years in and on its third console generation". **PC is not counted as a generation** and is never shown on a generation shelf.
- **No "only / one of few" claim.** The idea "GTA V is one of very few games sold across three console generations" appears only in an aggregator/search summary and fan-level pages, no reliable source with a list. **Left out.** No "first", "only", "record" anywhere.

## 1. One hard idea
**One game, three console generations: GTA 5 came out on PS3/Xbox 360 (2013), again on PS4/Xbox One (2014), again on PS5/Xbox Series X|S (2022). GTA 6 starts on just one: the current one.**

Main search phrase: `GTA 5 release date` / `GTA 5 PS4` ("GTA 5" spoken at 0.2 s, "PS4" by ~1.5 s, frame-0 caption `GTA 5 CAME BEFORE THE PS4`).

DM-able moment: `three` — the one GTA 5 case sitting on three stacked shelves of consoles, `3 GENERATIONS · 1 GAME`. And the hook itself ("GTA 5 is older than the PS4?") is the line people send.

## 2. Hook options
1. **"GTA 5 came before the PS4."** Chosen. 6 words, GTA first, breaks a belief (many first played it on PS4), checkable with two dates. Beats the backlog's "GTA 5 came out on three console generations", which is a flattering fact with no contradiction (our weakest opener type).
2. "GTA 5 spans three console generations." 6 words, true, but no stake.
3. "GTA 6 skips what GTA 5 did." Teases, but vague and risks implying a missing platform.
