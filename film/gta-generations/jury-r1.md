## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **14.5–17.4 s · `pc` line, Truth.** "Two delays" undercounts: PC was first promised for fall 2014 (E3 2014), and press called the April move the third delay. Re-voice to drop the number: text "PC players? It kept slipping. Then April 2015." (say "P C players? It kept slipping. Then April twenty fifteen."). Keep the two struck dated stamps (both are in Take-Two's tables). Do not add a `FALL 2014` stamp unless a Rockstar primary source is added to BRIEF.md. Log the PCR / E3 finding in BRIEF.md.
2. **28.1–35.5 s · `plat` and `date`, Truth.** Every GTA 6 label goes on the **top shelf** beside the GTA 6 case. Hang the `PS5` and `XBOX SERIES X|S` tags from the top-shelf slab and place them on the shelf face at y 560–640, not dropping into the PS4 compartment. Put `ROCKSTAR · ANNOUNCED` directly under them, still inside the top compartment. Put `NOV 19, 2026 · ROCKSTAR` on the top shelf too, or in the caption slot above the bookcase once the caption is off. Dim the lower two shelves to about 25 % so no GTA 6 label sits beside an old console.
3. **36.0–39.0 s · end vote tags, Truth and craft.** Each vote tag goes beside its own slab: `PS3 / 360` on the bottom shelf, `PS4 / ONE` in the middle, `PS5 / SERIES` on top, `PC` on the desk side or outside the bookcase at x ≥ 700 with no slab under it. No tag may cover a different console's label. All four stay identical, none highlighted. **Wow:** on "first", give each shelf's standby light a short pulse in turn (bottom → top, 0.15 s apart), so the vote reads as "pick your shelf".
4. **0.0–2.5 s · frame 0, Hook.** Cut the frame-0 caption to ≤5 words (≤4 preferred), e.g. `OLDER THAN THE PS4?` or `GTA 5 > PS4`. Keep the full line in the voice, and pop the full caption in at about 1.2 s if you want it. Put the sealed `PS4` box on the second shelf **at frame 0** (still sliding the last ~120 px) so frame 0 shows the conflict, not an empty shelf. Push in to 1.1× so case and box fill x 120–930.
5. **24.6–26.5 s and poster · payoff stamp, Clarity, craft and Share.** `3 GENERATIONS · 1 GAME` must not cover the PS4 / Xbox One shelf. Put it in the caption slot above the bookcase (y 300–480) in the pause after "One game", or across the top rail. 6. **19–39 s · PS5 slab label, craft.** Widen the label to fit `XBOX SERIES X|S` (≥ 330 px) or set it in two lines `XBOX SERIES` / `X|S` at ≥ 36 px. Pixel-check that no glyph is clipped, in the poster too.

## Generic or AI-made?
The set doesn't read as AI: the walnut grain, paper tags and taped box look hand-built. What reads as template is the static frontal framing on one prop for 35 of 39 s, the same torn-paper caption strip on every beat, and identical cream tags on every label. Fixes 3, 5 and 8 add life without a new beat.
