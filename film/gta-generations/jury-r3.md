## Fixes by impact
1. **0.0-1.0 s, packaging (SAFE-EDIT rule 6).** "GTA 6" is neither in the first spoken second nor in the planned title, and the film posts on the night GTA 6 launches, when every search is "GTA 6". The first GTA 6 word comes at 25.7 s. Change: title "GTA 5 Came Before the PS4 (GTA 6 Is Out Tonight)" or similar, with GTA first. Put the coral `GTA 6` case on screen at frame 0, top shelf, no words, already lit, so the payoff is planted. If you can re-voice, make the hook "G T A six is out. G T A five came before the P S four." (+~1.1 s; drop `q` per the BRIEF cut order to win the time back).
2. **0.0-2.5 s, wow on frame 0 (raises the hook).** The still is dim brown on brown: a dark slab, a case and a box, and nothing proves "before" with the sound off. Make it literal: the bottom slab is ON (green light, the disc half in, a warm TV-glow spill on the shelf) and GTA 5 is visibly being played, while the PS4 box sits sealed and taped above, unlit. Raise the bottom compartment's exposure by about 25 % and add a spot pool on the case. Then the frame reads "playing GTA 5 while the PS4 is still in the box" with no text at all, which is the film's best image and a second poster.
3. **28.6-36.2 s, dead middle on one shelf.** About 7.5 s pushed in on the top shelf: PS5 and SERIES tags, a chip, then a date card in the same spot (sheet frames 30-35 are nearly the same). Retention dips just before the end question. Shorten `plat` to "Rockstar lists PS5 and Xbox Series X and S." (BRIEF cut order, about -0.6 s), and add a physical event on "just one" (25.7-27 s): the lower two compartments' lights click off one by one, bottom then middle, 0.25 s apart, each with a relay tick in the 0.4 s pause. Then the camera pull at 33 s instead of holding the push.
4. **19.9-21.6 s, collision.** The `MAR 15, 2022 · TAKE-TWO · DIGITAL` card's top edge touches the two-line caption box ("AND XBOX SERIES X AND S"), and its bottom sits on the PS5 slab's label. Three plates are stacked in about 200 px. Hold the card until the caption peels (line end + 0.1 s), or scale it to 0.85 and move it 40 px down and 60 px right so there is at least 24 px of clear space from both caption and label.
5. **36.0-39.0 s, end tags double-label the slabs.** Each vote tag (`PS5 / SERIES`, `PS4 / ONE`, `PS3 / 360`) pins straight on top of the slab label that already says the same thing, so you see two labels per shelf, and `•PS5 / SERIES` crowds its hole dot against the P. Fade the slab labels while the tags hang (as already done in `plat`), or pin each tag into the empty ghost-outline slot on the right of its compartment, with 16 px inner padding on the tag.
6. **8.0-9.5 s and 23.2-25.6 s, type size.** In the wide shots the slab labels (`PS3 / XBOX 360`, etc.) fall to about 20-24 px cap height, under the 36 px floor. That is the one frame ("three console generations") where you need to read them. Cap the pull-back at about 1.15x of the current wide, or scale the labels 1.5x in the wide only.
7. **5.0-7.2 s, a still-ish hold.** Once the dates leave, the bottom-shelf shot holds with little new (sheet frames 6 and 7 are near-identical, and the caption is gone at 7.0). Land the disc-into-slab slide and the green power light on "Xbox 360" (about 5.6 s), then pull back by 6.6 s, so something changes at least every 1.2 s.
8. **38.9-39.2 s, seam.** The PS4 box drops across the GTA 5 case and hides its "GTA" for about 6 frames before frame 0. Land the box by 38.9 s, then slide the case in, so the loop frame never covers the hero.
9. **Voice, 7.2 s.** "So how many generations is it on?" is the weakest line, a filler question in Ming's flat read. Either cut it (fix 1 needs the time), or sharpen it to "And it wasn't done." (5 words, a re-hook rather than a quiz).

## Generic or AI-made tells
- The console slabs are interchangeable grey bricks. They read as a stock "cozy room" vector kit more than as hand-built paper props; there are no edge highlights, no cut-paper shadows, no wear. Give each generation's slab its own shape and finish (thick, glossy and curved; flat matte; tall and white-grey, all generic) so climbing the shelves also reads as time passing.
- The coral glowing outline rectangles at 22.9-27 s look like a UI focus ring, not an object on a shelf. Make them a dust-free patch on the shelf (a lighter rectangle on the wood), which says "something sat here" physically.
- The torn-paper caption strips are the house look. Fine, but they now sit on every Delta film, so the set has to carry the novelty (see fix 2).
