# Treatment: "GTA 5 Came Before the PS4" (Delta, gta-generations)

One hard idea: **one game, three console generations (2013, 2014, 2022); GTA 6 starts on just one.**

New set for this film (no plum wall, palm, `?` tower, case towers, stopwatch, punch card or paper city):
- **Set:** a living room at night. A dark walnut bookcase with three shelves fills the frame (bottom = oldest, top = newest), a warm lamp pool on the wall to the left, a cool TV glow breathing on the wall to the right, slow dust in the lamp light. Everything bleeds the frame; props stay whole inside x 90–930.
- **Consoles:** generic matte slabs (rounded block, vent stripe, disc slot, standby light). No logos, no real silhouettes, no controllers. Each carries only a cream paper label with its two names (`PS3` / `XBOX 360`).
- **Hero:** one plain GTA 5 case (ink case, coral spine, big cream `GTA 5`, no art), 150 × 215 px, standing beside the slab on its shelf.
- **Side set (the dip):** a desk to the right of the bookcase with a generic monitor (no marks) and a paper wall calendar.

## Three structures
1. **The case climbs the shelves (chosen).** One object, one direction (up), one shelf per generation, so "three generations, one game" is the picture itself. GTA 6 then lands on the top shelf only, which needs no extra sign.
2. A row of three TVs, the game hopping screen to screen. Rejected: TVs show screens, which invites game imagery we can't use, and a left-right row reads as a list.
3. A timeline ruler with game cases stacked on dates. Rejected: an infographic, the look juries docked on Delta (gta-pc, gta-vicecity).

## Native move
**The case hop.** The GTA 5 case jumps up one shelf with a thunk per generation and leaves a dashed ghost where it stood; at "Three console generations" both ghosts and the case are in one frame. The same case is on the bottom shelf at frame 0, so the seam (a sliding walnut cabinet door) lands exactly on frame 0.

## Frame 0 (no title card)
Camera pushed in on the bottom shelf: the GTA 5 case already sliding into place beside a dark, unlabeled slab (hero ≥ 215 px, the bookcase fills the frame). A sealed cardboard box marked `PS4` glides in from the right onto the shelf above by 0.3 s (≥ 400 px travel in the first second). Caption `GTA 5 CAME BEFORE THE PS4` (6 words). The two dated cards land only in the pause after the line.

## Numbers rule (LESSONS gta-longestwait r1)
Every date card carries only its own date and owner, and leaves under the next move before the next card lands. The tape measure on the bookcase's left post shows only the current year's label; older notches stay as blank ticks. PC dates live only on the desk calendar. No `59 DAYS`, no counter that rolls.
