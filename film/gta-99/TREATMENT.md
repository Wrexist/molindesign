# gta-99 — treatment

**One idea:** GTA 6 scored 99 on Metacritic, tying a 1998 Zelda for the top of the site's all-time list, from far more critics (150+ vs 22). Kicker: three GTAs in the all-time top seven.

## Benchmarks
| | Learn | Don't take |
|---|---|---|
| gta-lawsuit (Delta, remake 7.1 / Truth 9.0) | Paper pieces that move as rigid bodies, every hit written into a pause (VAD gate), the stamp as the hero sound, the last frame = frame 0. | The felt desk, folder and courtroom (juries fail reused sets); the slow 39 s pace. |
| hotcharging (7.4 / Truth 9.0) | The number on frame 0 already printed, the condition chained to the number every time it shows. | A counter rolling under a label (LESSONS gta-lawsuit r1): every number here slams final. |

## Three structures
1. **Trophy table (chosen).** Two gold medals on ribbons are the tie; jars of critic slips are the 22 vs 150+; a score board is the top seven. One hero per beat, layout changes at 1998 and at the board.
2. Scoreboard ticker (a stadium board flipping scores). Rejected: flip/roll numbers break the "slam final" rule and read as a counter.
3. Timeline 1998 → 2026. Rejected: a list of dates, no picture for "tied".

## Native move
**The medal swing.** Each medal hangs on a long satin ribbon from above the frame and swings as a damped pendulum (no bounce). Frame 0 is the GTA 6 medal mid-swing (≈300 px of travel in the first second, all of it printed). The stamp slam (`TIED · BEST EVER`, `3 GTAs IN THE TOP 7`) is the hero sound.
