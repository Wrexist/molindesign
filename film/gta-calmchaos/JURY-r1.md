## Fixes, ranked by impact
1. **16.5–18.6 s, rifle line (Truth, gate).** Either re-cut the footage to the window shot at about 840.5–842.0 s and re-time the stopwatch splash and line to `0:50`/"Fifty. Guns out the window.", or keep the 838 clip and change the line and label to "Forty-eight. A rifle, handed over." (`RIFLE` chip OK). Check that the new range stays clear of gta-wanted's 841–848.5 plates; if it collides, take the second option.
2. **9.0–11.6 s, quote bubbles (Truth, gate).** Remove the speaker tails, or point both at a neutral spot such as the car's centre line or the windshield. Stack the bubbles as `ONE: "COULD BE A COP…"` / `OTHER: "HE'S JUST CAUTIOUS."` so the picture matches "one says / the other says".
3. **6.86–11.65 s, plates under a running clock (Truth).** Don't run `pdrive` (786–789.6 s) under 0:04. Use a plate from 790–797 s (e.g. 797–801 s, after the quote) so the clock never runs over footage it doesn't time. Or freeze or hide the stopwatch while a plate is up, and tag the plate `SAME SCENE`.
4. **Whole film, y 1320–1920 (Craft +1, Hook, Share).** Enlarge each footage card to a 4:5 crop of about 1000x1250 at y 330–1580, centred on the ringed object, and move the label chip to about y 1600. Or put a second element in the band, such as a source timeline ruler `13:10 ──●── 14:09` with a playhead that jumps to each beat (the clock made literal). Measure YAVG of y 1400–1900 above 50 in every second.
5. **19.6–21.7 s and frame 0, the flip (wow / Share).** Make the flip the money shot. Play 849.3–850.5 s full-bleed (1080x1350+) with a 1.00→1.12 punch-in on 083, the ring tracking the truck, a low impact hit plus a short 2-frame white flash at 849.6 s, and `0:59` stamped huge next to it at 160 px. For frame 0, start the hook clip at 849.55 s so the truck is already on two wheels and the ring is up at 0.0 s.
6. **22.9–26.9 s, static end card (Retention).** Cut it to about 2.2 s and make it move. Give each tile a looping 1-second footage thumbnail (the calm car on SMALL TALK, the flip on SHOOT OUT) with a pulsing tap ring alternating between them, and drop "Which one are you here for?" to one question: "Small talk or shootout?"
7. **11.6 s and 16.3 s, silent splashes (Sound).** Raise those two clock hits from -21 to about -10 dBFS (move them into the pause, or widen the pause before "At 31" and "48" by .15 s), so all five clock beats land the same way.
8. **27.0 s, loop line (Loop).** Make the last line run into the hook: "…shootout? Pick one. Because GTA 6 goes from small talk to shootout in under a minute", or end on "Watch the clock." with the frame-0 layout, and drop "Because".
9. **11.6–12.9 s, wipe overlaps (Craft).** Clear the cop bubbles and caption before the sweep passes, keep the `0:31` splash off the back-seat card (place it above y 280 or finish it before the card lands), and move the label chips so they never cover the `CLIP · ROCKSTAR` tag (it is half-hidden on 4 cards).
