## Description
On PlayStation, GTA 6 unlocks at each store's own midnight on November 19, 2026. So it starts in New Zealand.

PlayStation Store unlock times, in New York time (EST):
- New Zealand: midnight NZDT = 6 AM, Wednesday Nov 18
- Sydney: 2 hours later. Tokyo: 2 hours after that.
- UK: midnight GMT = 7 PM, Wednesday Nov 18
- US store: midnight Eastern = 12 AM, Thursday Nov 19

The US PlayStation Store lists one time for the whole country, midnight Eastern. That makes it 9 PM Wednesday in Los Angeles, 10 PM Mountain and 11 PM Central. New York waits 18 hours after New Zealand.

Xbox players: Rockstar and Xbox have not published a store-by-store time we could check, so look at your Xbox store listing.
