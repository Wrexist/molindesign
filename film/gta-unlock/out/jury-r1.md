## Fixes ranked by impact
1. **19.4-22.4 s, the dip is invisible (Craft, Clarity, Share).** I saw the full pull-out turn the five clocks into ~70 px chips with ~18 px digits and NY/LON/TYO/SYD/NZ tags at ~24 px. The 18 HOURS stamp lands at 20.35 s, after "eighteen hours" is spoken at ~19.6 s. This matters because it's the film's hero number, and on a phone it's a strip of dots. **Change:** stop the pull-out at two clocks only, NY (left, x 90-480) and NZ (right, x 600-930), each ≥ 220 px tall with ≥ 72 px digits, and drop LON/TYO/SYD from this shot. Keep the orange tape between them at full width and print 18 HOURS AFTER NZ big on the word "eighteen" (silent visual at ~19.6 s, stamp sound kept in the 20.3 s pause).
2. **20.77 s line `but` and the LA chips (Truth).** The voice says "the US store **uses** New York's midnight", and LA wears UNLOCKED at 9 PM. But PlayStation's US support page says "midnight local time", so the payoff rests on one listing. **Change:** make the voice and caption "But the US store **lists** New York's midnight." Add "LISTED" to the LA card (WED NOV 18 · 9 PM PT · LISTED) and keep the US PS STORE LISTING · NOV 16 credit. Re-curl en-us on the day you post: if it isn't 05:00Z, don't post. Description line: "PlayStation's support page says midnight local; the US store listing shows 12 AM ET / 9 PM PT. Xbox: check your Xbox store."
3. **Captions at 11.8-14.8 s and 14.8-17.9 s (Clarity, a LESSONS repeat).** "LONDON UNLOCKS AT 7 / PM, NEW YORK TIME." splits "7 PM", and "TWO HOURS / LATER, SYDNEY." splits "two hours later". **Change:** use "LONDON UNLOCKS AT 7 PM, / NEW YORK TIME."
