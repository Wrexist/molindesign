## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 6 unlocks at each PlayStation store's own midnight, so midnight sweeps the globe: New Zealand first, New York 18 hours later, and the US store's single time puts LA at 9 PM the night before.** Hero number: **18 HOURS**. Hero picture: the midnight line sweeping across a hand-made world strip, clocks flipping to 12:00 one by one.

**Search phrases:** `GTA 6 release time` (on screen from frame 0 as the pill, captioned by SEO), `GTA 6 New Zealand` (spoken from 0.2 s: "GTA 6 starts in New Zealand"). Secondary: `GTA 6 unlock time`.

**DM-able moment:** `la`: "So in LA, that's 9 PM. The night before." The West Coast friend gets sent this. (Backup: the 18-hour gap between Auckland and New York.)

## 2. Hook options
1. **"GTA 6 starts in New Zealand."** **Chosen.** 6 words, GTA first, a place nobody expects in a GTA video, and it is true as said: of the 31 PlayStation Store regions I read, New Zealand's listing is the earliest (11:00 UTC Nov 18). It avoids the backlog's "unlocks in New Zealand first", which is a stronger claim than I can check across Xbox and every store; "starts" describes the order of the sweep, and line 2 scopes it to PlayStation within 3 s.
2. "GTA 6 unlocks in New Zealand first." The backlog hook. True for the PlayStation stores I read, but Xbox is unknown and "first" invites "Kiribati/Samoa/Tonga hit midnight earlier" comments (none of them has a PS Store region, but the line would still need a scope it can't carry in 6 words).
3. "LA plays GTA 6 the night before." Strongest DM line, but it gives away the payoff on frame 0 (LESSONS: frame 0 shows the conflict, not the answer) and rests on one store listing. Kept as the payoff.

Frame 0 (≤ 5 words on screen): pill `GTA 6 RELEASE TIME` (search phrase, 4 tokens) is too many with a city name, so: `GTA 6` pill + `NEW ZEALAND` tag on the first clock already flipping to 12:00 = 4 tokens. The midnight line is already moving (≥ 300 px glide in the first second, LESSONS agent r1).

- NZ → US store: 11:00Z Nov 18 → 05:00Z Nov 19 = **18 h** exactly.

- UK unlock in New York: 00:00Z − 5 h = **7:00 PM EST Wed Nov 18**.
- US store unlock in LA: 05:00Z − 8 h (PST) = **9:00 PM PST Wed Nov 18**.
- Sydney 2 h after Auckland, Tokyo 2 h after Sydney (from facts 2-4).
