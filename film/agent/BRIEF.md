## 1. Topic and one hard idea

**An AI agent is the same next-word guesser from a chatbot, put in a loop: it guesses an action instead of a word ("click here"), but it can't click anything itself. Other software does the click and hands it back a screenshot, and it guesses the next step, again and again. Every step is still a guess, which is why the companies building agents say they should stop and ask you before they buy or send.**

Story order: the stake (nobody touched the mouse, yet the cursor is on send) → the question (what is an AI agent, actually?) → callback to part 1 (inside is a chatbot's next-word guesser) → the shift (now it guesses an action) → **hero: it can't click anything, the action is just text** → other software clicks and sends back a screenshot → the loop (think, act, look; ReAct 2022) → the turn (every step is still a guess) → the proof and DM moment (Anthropic's own launch demo: it went off to look at Yellowstone photos) → the bridge to the viewer (that's why it's meant to ask you before it buys or sends) → the question that loops into line 1.

**Native move for this film: the ticket.** Every action is a slip of paper the caret types. Tickets feed, get pressed, get stamped, get stopped at a gate. It reads hand-made, and it's literally true: the model's "click" is a few words of text that another program reads.

**Main search phrase: `what is an AI agent`** (spoken inside `ask`, ≈ 2.3–4.0 s). Show **WHAT IS AN AI AGENT?** in the upper third from ≈ 2.0 s and in the caption's first line. Secondary phrases for the SEO agent (volumes not checked): `AI agents explained`, `how do AI agents work`, `ChatGPT agent`, `Claude computer use`, `agentic AI`.

**DM-able moment:** Anthropic's own launch demo: the agent "took a break" and browsed photos of Yellowstone. Second: the click ticket stamped JUST TEXT ("it can't actually click anything").

**Proposed YouTube title (4–8 words, Title Case, one emoji):** **"What an AI Agent Actually Is 🤔"**. Alternatives: "Why AI Agents Can't Really Click 🤔", "The AI That Got Distracted by Yellowstone 😮".

- `trick` "It can't click anything.": "The model doesn't execute the function itself" (Google); "your application runs every call" (Anthropic). The stamp `JUST TEXT` = the request is a structured text block (tool_use / function call) that software reads.

- `loop` "Think, act, look. Again and again.": ReAct's thought-action-observation (fact 6) in plain words; "again and again" = "restarting the loop" / "while loop" (facts 2, 5). Not in quote marks.
- `wrong` "every step is still a guess": series framing; supported by "error-prone" (fact 8). Our words.
- `yellowstone` "In Anthropic's own demo, it stopped to browse Yellowstone photos.": fact 8, paraphrased outside quote marks; the on-screen quote is the source's exact words with a leading "…". "Own demo" = demonstrations recorded for Anthropic's launch.
