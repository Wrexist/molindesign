# Treatment · agent ("What an AI agent actually is")

## Benchmarks
- **Learn from phonephoto (first cut 7.5, best of the last four):** one hand-made prop carries the whole film and the mechanism is shown on the prop itself. Here the prop is the click ticket: the guesser's "click" is a slip of text, typed by the series caret.
- **Learn from qrcode (8.0):** prove the on-screen claim on the frame. The ticket's `CLICK (412, 880)` is the frame position of the button the paper finger presses: the button's centre is set from the same constant that prints the ticket.
- **Don't take from phonephoto r2 / password / gpus:** the hero image on screen for 0.35 s and exiting on a zoom; labels sitting on the wrong prop; six coral stamps. Here the THINK · ACT · LOOK ring holds fully still (≥ 1.2 s) and leaves on the torn edge; one coral stamp; every label sits beside the one prop it names and is keyed to its spoken word.

## Three structures
1. **The ticket's journey (chosen):** stake (cursor on send, nobody at the mouse) → question → the caret from part 1 → it guesses an action → the ticket → JUST TEXT (hero) → other software clicks, a screenshot comes back → the loop held still → every step a guess → Anthropic's Yellowstone demo → the same ticket `CLICK (SEND)` stopped at an ASK FIRST gate in front of the frame-0 email → yes/no. One prop travels through every scene.
2. "What if your AI could click?": a hypothetical opener. Rejected: the real thing already exists, a hypothetical makes it sound made up.
3. Yellowstone first ("This AI got distracted by Yellowstone"). Rejected (BRIEF §2): a joke without a stake; the film would be about one bug.

## Native move
The ticket. Every action is a strip of receipt paper the caret types out letter by letter. Tickets feed, tear, get stamped, get read by the clicker box, and get stopped at a gate. Scene changes are the house torn edge rising along one axis with the next scene drawn under it.
