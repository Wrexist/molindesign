## Description
GTA 6 is out, but not on PC. Rockstar's own GTA 6 page lists exactly two platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said Rockstar "always starts on console" (to Bloomberg, via VGC, May 2026). History says PC players wait: GTA 3, Vice City, San Andreas and GTA 4 each reached PC in under eight months (196 to 224 days). GTA 5 came out on PS3 and Xbox 360 on Sept 17, 2013, then PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, and reached PC on April 14, 2015: 574 days, almost 19 months. Red Dead Redemption 2 took just over a year (Oct 26, 2018 to Nov 5, 2019). Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6. The dashed bar is our own math (GTA 5's gap added to Nov 19, 2026), not a Rockstar date.
