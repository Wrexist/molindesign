## Three structures
1. **Countdown:** a big "days since console launch" counter for each game in turn. One number at a time, so it reads as a list (vicecity's flaw).
2. **Platform card only:** the Rockstar page and the empty PC slot for the whole film. One stake, but no picture of *how long*.
3. **The wait chart (chosen):** the stake first (a PC that can't get GTA 6, then Rockstar's page with two filled platform slots and a dashed PC slot), then one chart where every PC wait is a pink bar to scale. Four short bars, then GTA 5's bar runs nearly the full width and becomes the hero. The GTA 6 row waits at the bottom the whole time with an open bar and a "?".

## Native move
A pink bar that grows to scale across the chart, with the month counter rolling beside it. The short bars stop before a dashed 8 MO line; GTA 5's crosses it and keeps going to ALMOST 19 MONTHS. The GTA 6 bar never grows.
