# Treatment · Why your phone gets hot when charging

Factory #35 (`hotcharging`), the director's plan. Script and sources: `BRIEF.md`, `vo/script.json` (Ming US_James, seed 7, no music, no AI tag). Engine, gates and sound come from `../5gbattery/` (#34: `CLIP_RUN`, `LABELS_RUN`, safe box, quote plates, peel/label/hold patterns, captions, SFX gate, the loop seam at t = 1/60). The look is this film's own: a kitchen bench in cut paper, a charger in a wall socket, and the charging cable drawn as a fat paper pipe with a tap on it.

## Two benchmarks
- **Learn from `5gbattery` (#34, r2 7.4 / Truth 9.0):** every number prints on the frame the picture proves it; scope lines on every number; an empty slot in the lower third that later fills; the summary drawn in chalk on the hero hold. **Don't take:** its bench meter and its two-needle gauge (the hero prop changes every film), and its status-bar tile.
- **Learn from `overnightcharge` (#32, r2 7.3):** a drawn charge line over the hero hold turned a dead hold into the poster frame. **Don't take:** its "80 % CHARGE" label that outlived the fill; here the 80 never appears without "iPHONE · IF THE BATTERY GETS TOO WARM" in the same frame, and the shelf is drawn only once the chart's line reaches it.

## Three structures
1. **The pipe and the tap (chosen).** The cable is a paper pipe from the charger to the phone, amber current dashes flowing through it, a cream tap on it labelled CHARGING CURRENT. A heat strip on the phone warms cream → amber; wireless charging and a game make it warmer (orange); at "Too hot" it turns coral and the battery stalls at 80. The phone's own paper hand comes out of its side and turns the tap down: the twist is a physical act on the hero prop. A chalk slate over the bench draws the charge line (steep, flattening near full, a coral shelf at 80) for the hero hold. Then the re-created screens, then back to the bench to unplug.
2. **The thermometer phone.** A phone with a big thermometer beside it. Clear, but it prints temperatures on the phone, which the brief forbids (rule 3b.3), and it has no "who did it" moment.
3. **The broken charger court case.** The charger in the dock, the phone as the culprit. Fun, but it turns a calm "this is normal" film into blame and needs a lot of type.

## The native move: the tap
The tap on the pipe is the film's thread. Wide open from frame 0 (dashes flowing). At `stall` the dashes thin to a trickle. At `twist` the phone's own cream hand reaches out of the phone's left edge and grips the wheel; on "slows" (`protect`) it turns the wheel down 150° and the dashes slow, the strip cools coral → amber. At `cool` the plug comes out of the wall (pop), the pipe empties, the strip fades to cream. At `end` the plug is back in, the tap open, the strip cream: frame 0.

## Layouts (two changes across the middle third)
A bench close-up (camera 1.3×, the phone glides up 330 px in 0.55 s) → the camera pulls out to the whole bench on "So why" (2.5 s): charger, pipe, tap, phone → at `more` a wireless pad slides under the phone and the pipe re-plugs into the pad; a paper controller hops onto the screen → **change 1, `protect` (20.5 s): the chalk slate** slides over the bench (hero hold, poster) → **change 2, `iphone` (24 s): the settings screens, upright**, iPhone Battery, the phone turns to Pixel Battery diagnostics → the bench again for `cool` → A for `end` (camera pushes back to 1.3×, YES / NO).

## Rules for this film
- Frame 0: three words (**WARM OR HOT?**, the "?" amber, never coral), the phone at 1.3× gliding up ≥ 300 px, the heat strip cream and warming, current dashes already flowing. The end card is the same picture.
- Coral only from "Too hot": the strip's hot cells, the trickle, the STUCK stamp, the BROKEN? tag and the chart's 80 shelf. Before that the strip tops out at amber/orange. YES / NO are cream.
- Every **80** (battery print, caption card, stamp, chart shelf) shares its frame with **iPHONE · IF THE BATTERY GETS TOO WARM** (≥ 36 px). No chip says "80% = TOO HOT". The caption is broken so "80%." is its own card, landing with the battery print.
- No temperature anywhere on the phone or strip; the strip says only COOL → HOT (which way is good).
- Quotes word for word, "…" at every cut, tagged with the source. Settings screens RE-CREATED, generic rows, no checkmark, no switch, no highlighted state, neutral chevrons. Pixel scoped **PIXEL 6a & LATER**.
- Hedges kept in credits: "MIGHT LIMIT", "MAY SLOW OR PAUSE".
- No fire, smoke, swelling or damage: the heat curls are three thin paper strips.
- Lower third (y 1150–1430) filled every second: the pipe's bottom run, the tap label, then plates; a dashed empty slot waits under the bench and the 80 scope plate prints into it.
- Props stay whole inside x 92–928, y 302–1428 except during the opening camera move; backgrounds bleed full width.
- SFX: dry hits in the written voice gaps only, line end + 0.3 s, margin 0.2: tick (hook), slap (stall, short body to fit the gap), knock (twist, the hand grips the tap), one dry thump (protect, hero), tick (cool). A sixth tick on CHECK YOURS was dropped by the gate: no clean gap before "iPhone".
