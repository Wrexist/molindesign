Why does your phone get hot when charging? A battery warms up as it charges, so a warm phone on the charger is normal. Wireless charging and gaming while it charges make it warmer. If it gets too hot, the phone protects its own battery by slowing or pausing the charge, and an iPhone might stop at 80% until it cools down. That stop is the phone's software, not a broken charger.

Apple, "Charge and maintain your iPhone battery": "A battery warms up as it charges, which can reduce its lifespan. To reduce the effect of heat and prevent overheating, iPhone gradually reduces the charging current as the battery approaches full charge."

Apple, "If your iPhone or iPod touch won't charge" (section "If your device stopped charging at 80 percent"): "Your iPhone might get slightly warmer while it charges. To extend the lifespan of your battery, if the battery gets too warm, software might limit charging above 80 percent. Your iPhone will charge again when the temperature drops." Scope: the 80% number is iPhone-only, and only "if the battery gets too warm".

Apple, "If your iPhone or iPad gets too hot or too cold": "You might notice that your device feels warmer when you: … Wirelessly charge your device … Use graphics-intensive or processor-intensive apps, games, or features, including camera apps … These conditions are normal." And, when it's too hot: "Charging, including wireless charging, slows or stops".

Check yours on iPhone. Apple, "About iPhone charge speeds": "You can see if charging was on hold on your iPhone by opening Settings and tapping Battery." Path: Settings › Battery.

Check yours on Pixel. Google Pixel Phone Help, "Check & understand your Pixel’s temperature": "Important: Available on Pixel 6a and later." Path: Settings › Battery › Battery diagnostics. "Device temperature diagnostics shows the current estimated device temperature and one of four temperature states of your device" (Cold, Normal, Warm, Elevated). Scope: Pixel 6a and later; the temperature is Google's estimate.

Google, "Help keep your Pixel phone from feeling too warm or hot": "If your phone feels warm, you shouldn’t be too concerned." When it's too hot, "Your phone may: … Reduce charging speeds." And: "If your phone is too hot, disconnect it from its power source if it's plugged in, move it to a cooler place, and do not use it until it has cooled down."

Samsung, "Keep your Galaxy device at its normal operating temperature": "While they may feel warm under certain conditions, like charging, this does not affect the device's lifespan or performance." When a Galaxy overheats, "…charging may pause…".

Fine print: The 80% hold is Apple's, for iPhone, and Apple says software "might" do it "if the battery gets too warm"; Pixel and Galaxy pages say charging may slow or pause, with no number. Heat isn't the only reason an iPhone stops at 80%: Optimized Battery Charging can also hold it at 80% until you need it, and iPhone 15 models and later can have a Charge Limit set. The 32–95°F ranges on Apple's and Samsung's pages are air (ambient) temperatures, not the phone's own temperature. **Ready replies for the first hour:**
- **If:** So my charger is fine?
  **Reply:** If it's an iPhone sitting at 80% while warm, that's the phone's software, not the charger: Apple says "Your iPhone will charge again when the temperature drops." If it won't charge at all, Apple's page says to check the cable and adapter "for signs of damage" and to try a different outlet.
- **If:** Mine stops at 80% and it's not hot.
  **Reply:** Then it's probably not heat. Apple's own page lists Optimized Battery Charging (it waits until you need the phone to finish past 80%) and, on iPhone 15 models and later, a Charge Limit you can set. Either one can be why it stops at 80%.
- **If:** Does fast charging ruin the battery?
  **Reply:** None of the Apple, Google or Samsung pages we used say that, so the video doesn't claim it. What Apple does say: iPhone "gradually reduces the charging current as the battery approaches full charge" to limit heat.
- **If:** I don't see Battery diagnostics on my Pixel.
  **Reply:** Google says it's "Available on Pixel 6a and later" (one of its pages says Pixel 6 and later; we went with the stricter one). Look under Settings › Battery › Battery diagnostics.
- **If:** My Samsung gets really hot charging.
  **Reply:** Samsung's advice: "Disconnect the charger, and close any running apps until the device cools down." It also says to charge "on a hard, flat surface". If it keeps happening, contact Samsung support.
