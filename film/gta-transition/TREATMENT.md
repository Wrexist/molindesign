# Why Delta is becoming a GTA 6 channel (Delta)

Vertical 1080x1920, about 78 s. One hard idea: **the Delta triangle flips into a 6**, then a plain reason, plain list, plain promise.

## Benchmarks
- **Kurzgesagt-style flat explainers**: learn flat hand-built shapes, one color per idea, icons popping on a spring. Don't take the slow cosmic pacing.
- **Retro Vice-City-era posters (striped sun, palms)**: learn palette and striped sun. Don't take grid floors, lens glow or chrome text.

## Three structures
1. **Talking-head style letter** (text on a plain background): rejected, no motion.
2. **Fake channel-page walkthrough**: rejected, reads as a screen recording of YouTube.
3. **The channel flip** (chosen): scene = channel; a three-stripe wipe between them; mark flips from triangle to 6 and back into frame 0 for the loop.
