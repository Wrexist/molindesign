# Treatment: The trailer songs (gta-songs)

## Benchmarks
- **film/gta-records/** (Delta #2, posted): learn the voice-first keying with `at(id, word)`, the slanted pink and teal cut, one rolling counter as the native move and the loop-back dissolve to frame 0. Don't take its two competing numbers or its balance ending, which asks the viewer to compare two records.
- **Cassette-deck hardware (1980s tape counters and VU meters):** learn that a mechanical counter and a needle are numbers you can feel move, and that both belong to the same era as the 1989 song. Don't take any brand, logo or real product layout: our deck is a flat, hand-built shape.

## Structures considered
1. Timeline of three trailers and their songs. Clear, but a list (gta-vicecity, held at 7.2, taught that a walk across items reads as a list).
2. Bar-chart race of trailer songs. Needs a second and third number, and Trailer 2's 182,000% has an unclear window (BRIEF fact 7), so it can't be shown.
3. **One tape, one counter, one turn (chosen).** A dusty 1989 cassette comes back to life; its deck counter rolls past three digits to 36,979% and holds the whole frame; trailer 2 adds a second tape; the launch trailer breaks the row of tapes with something new. The end asks old or new, with both shown equally.

## Native move
The cassette and its deck counter. Frame 0 is the dusty grey tape already drifting as it seats in the deck, cobweb swaying, with GTA 6 and 1989 on screen. On "revived" a cloth wipe clears the dust, the shell turns pink, the reels spin and the three-digit counter starts turning. On the hero line the counter overflows its three wheels, the deck widens to five and lands on 36,979%. Its own sounds sit in the pauses: a clunk as the tape seats before the first word, a reel whirr, a ratchet of counter ticks and a bell when the number lands, a needle hitting its peg after "never like this". Cuts are the slanted pink and teal line rising under the old scene, with the next object already drawn under it.

## Sound
No music. Tape-deck foley from the same timeline, about 30 hits in 42 s, each placed in a pause from what unhinted faster-whisper (base.en and small.en) heard in the finished mix (`vo/heard.js`), at least 0.15 s from every heard word and at least 4.7 dB under the neighbouring voice (`duck.smr_db` 56): the tape seating in the deck before the first word (voice onset 0.31 s), the REVIVED stamp in the pause after "revived", a speeding wind-up ratchet in the pause before "Streams" and roll ticks in the gaps of the number line, the counter's ratchet and a short bell after "percent", the needle hitting its peg after "this", the third slot cracking after "pattern", tears under four of the cuts, a pop on the vote and a swish home into frame 0. Beats with no free pause stay silent.
