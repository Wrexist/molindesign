## Description
After inflation, GTA 5 cost more than GTA 6. GTA 6 costs $79.99, but GTA 5's $59.99 from 2013 is over $85 in today's money.

On paper, GTA 6 costs $20 more: $79.99 for the Standard Edition, against $59.99 for GTA 5 when it launched on September 17, 2013. But US prices rose about 43.9% between September 2013 and September 2026, so GTA 5's $59.99 then is about $86.33 now. That is more than GTA 6's $79.99.

The math, in plain words: the US consumer price index (BLS CPI-U, all items, US city average, not seasonally adjusted) was 234.149 in September 2013 and 336.940 in September 2026, the latest month published. 336.940 divided by 234.149 is about 1.439, so prices rose about 43.9%. $59.99 times 1.439 is about $86.33. That is $6.34 more than $79.99. GTA 5 would only stop costing more if prices had risen less than 33.3% (79.99 divided by 59.99). This is our own arithmetic on the BLS figures; Rockstar has never compared GTA 6's price with inflation.

Both are US launch list prices of the standard editions. This ignores sales and later discounts, special and Ultimate editions (GTA 6 Ultimate is $99.99), other countries, and disc vs digital.
