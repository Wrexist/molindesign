## 1. One hard idea
**GTA 6's $79.99 feels like a record, but GTA 5's $59.99 from September 2013 is over $85 in today's money, so in real terms GTA 6 is the cheaper launch.** One hero number: **over $85**.

Search phrases (say "GTA 6 costs" at 0.2 s, show `GTA 6 PRICE` from frame 0; caption): `GTA 6 price`, `how much is GTA 6`, `GTA 6 $80`, `GTA 5 price 2013`.

DM-able moment: line `hero` ("GTA 5's $59.99 is over $85 today"). Backup DM, not spoken (one hero number per film): in 2013 money, GTA 6 costs about $55.

## 2. Hook options
1. **"GTA 6 costs $80. Too much?"** (6 words, "GTA 6" as words 1-2) **Chosen.** It states the belief the viewer already holds and asks for their yes/no in 3 s; the film then breaks it. It's fully true as spoken (the $79.99 Standard, said as "$80" the way Engadget and most press round it; the tag on screen always shows $79.99), and the last line repeats it so the loop replays.
2. "GTA 5 cost more than GTA 6." (6 words) The sharper contradiction and the best title, but as a frame-0 line it is false without "after inflation", and a sound-off viewer reads `$59.99` against `$79.99` and calls us wrong for 2-3 s (LESSONS: a negation and its claim share one card; Truth 8.5 gate). Kept as the title and as the payoff (line `more`).
3. "GTA 6 is cheaper than GTA 5." (6 words) Same problem as 2, and "cheaper" invites "it's $20 more" comments before the explanation lands.

Frame 0: one big cream price tag `$79.99` with a `GTA 6` pill and kinetic `TOO MUCH?` (4 words/tokens: GTA 6, $79.99, TOO MUCH?). No GTA 5 tag yet (that's the turn).

**Derived (our arithmetic on facts 4-6; list on screen as `BLS CPI-U · SEPT 2013 → SEPT 2026`):**
- Ratio: 336.940 / 234.149 = **1.4390**. Prices rose **43.9%** → spoken "over forty percent" (rounded down).
- GTA 5 in Sept 2026 dollars: $59.99 × 1.4390 = **$86.33** → spoken and shown "over $85" (rounded down; true by $1.33).
- Claim check: GTA 5 costs more in real terms if the ratio is above 79.99 / 59.99 = **1.3334**. It is 1.4390, so the claim holds with a wide margin (it would only fail if CPI fell to 312.2, a 7% drop).
- Gap in today's dollars: $86.33 − $79.99 = $6.34 (not spoken).
- Backup DM, not spoken: GTA 6 in Sept 2013 dollars = $79.99 / 1.4390 = **$55.59** ("about $55").
- "$20 more on paper": $79.99 − $59.99 = $20.00 exactly.

**Nuance kept in the voice (line `fine`) and on screen:** US list (launch) price only, standard editions both sides; ignores sales, later discounts (GTA 5 was later sold for much less), special editions, other regions, and digital vs disc. We say "US prices" for CPI-U (CPI-U, the index for all urban consumers; not spoken). Rockstar has never compared GTA 6's price with inflation; no line says it did.
