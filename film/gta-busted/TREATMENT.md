## Three structures
1. **Countdown cards**: four full-screen cards, one per step, a tick at the end of each. Clear, but it is CNS's look and a list with no object to care about.
2. **The arrest, rewound**: open on BUSTED, rewind to show what you should have done. Strong story, but the rewind spends 5 s before the first step and the steps lose their counter.
3. **The property receipt (chosen).** A jail property receipt docked in the lower third lists what the cops take (FIREARMS, VEHICLE, STASH BAGS, POCKET CASH), every row stamped CONFISCATED in the hook. Each step rips its stamp strip off and slams a coral KEPT; KEPT 0/4 climbs to 4/4. The threat sits on screen the whole film and every step visibly removes part of it, which is the goal gradient the format runs on. The dip (impound) hangs an IMPOUND LOT tag on a row we already "won".

## Native move
The perforation rip: the CONFISCATED strip tears off its row (dashed tear line, strip falls and turns), the KEPT stamp slams under it, the counter slams to the next number. Four times, each in a written pause with its own scratch + stamp sound; the fourth (POCKET CASH) is the hero with a ding.

## System (our design)
Ink stage, cream type, one coral accent, oxblood for police stamps, manila for tags, carbon-blue for the receipt. Pinned yellow evidence-tape header (BEFORE YOU GET BUSTED + GTA 6 pill, a siren dot that blinks on each step), a manila STEP tag top right that flips on "One/Two/Three/Four" (coral for 4), place cards at y 482–704 with an icon disc and a source line, props in y 740–1060, receipt y 1086–1430, captions at y 300–440 with 1–2 coral words. Plates: official Rockstar video, muted, dimmed about 38 % (ink only behind the caption band and the receipt), one per beat, < 4.9 s each; from "bank" to the loop seam the frame is all ours. Frame 0: GTA 6 pill, BEFORE YOU / GET BUSTED, a coral 4 badge on the receipt (it flies into the STEP tag, which reads 4 STEPS until Step 1), the receipt sharp with four ruled rows, Lucia's face in x 180–600.
