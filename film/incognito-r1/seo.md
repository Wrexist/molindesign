So what does incognito mode actually hide? It's printed on Chrome's own incognito screen.

Under "Your activity might still be visible to:" Chrome lists "Websites you visit", "Your employer or school" and "Your internet service provider". Incognito only forgets on your device. Google's Chrome Help page says: "While Incognito can help keep your browsing private on your device, it doesn't make you invisible." Every page still crosses your network to the site, and the same page says organizations that manage your network "may be able to observe your activity in Incognito." It forgets for you. Not for them.
