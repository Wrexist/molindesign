# Treatment: The record trailers (gta-records)

## Benchmarks
- **film/memory/** (house factory film): learn the voice-first keying with `at(id, word)`, the word-highlighted captions in the upper third, the scene wipe and the loop-back dissolve to frame 0. Don't take its paper-and-chat props or its palette.
- **Sports-scoreboard counters (odometer tickers on broadcast graphics):** learn that a number that visibly rolls is the whole story, and that it needs a unit and a clock beside it. Don't take the broadcast chrome.

## Structures considered
1. Timeline of both trailers (two dates, two numbers). Clear, but static and a list.
2. A bar chart race. Needs only numbers, but the twist (different measures) gets lost.
3. **One odometer, then a balance (chosen).** The same wheels roll to 90,421,491, then 475M, and the end weighs them: the viewer feels that the two numbers are not the same kind of number. Twist at "But count YouTube alone".

## Native move
The odometer: digit wheels that roll (carry-coupled, last wheel continuous). Frame 0 is already rolling; the 24-hour ring on frame 0 completes on "One day." The same wheels return for 475M. Cuts are a slanted pink and teal line rising under the old scene.
